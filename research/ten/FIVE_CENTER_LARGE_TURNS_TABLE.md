Thus every claimed bound can be checked by rational arithmetic from the stated formulas; decimal approximations are not inputs.

## 2. Complete root cover

The max/min and adjacent-sum constraints from Section 1 are always imposed.

In the tree notation, a number is its certificate case. `Ti(a; L; R)` cuts coordinate `i` at `a`, sending the closed lower half to `L` and the closed upper half to `R`. Each listed cut is the midpoint of its current coordinate interval. `S38` is the singleton `(135,0,90,45,90)`, already a vertex of case 38. `S9` is the singleton `(135,90,0,90,45)`, already a vertex of case 9. These are the only leaves requiring a cross-reference; no lower-dimensional cell is omitted.

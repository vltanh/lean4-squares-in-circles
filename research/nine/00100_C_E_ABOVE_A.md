# E lies above A throughout the nonnegative-A, nonpositive-D caller

Retain the full normalized original-nine 001/00 caller: nine
independently oriented, pairwise interior-disjoint physical unit
squares with all original corners in the closed Q-disk,
Q=1105/256; actual AB, BC, EF, FG; strictly weak CD; the five
selected center cones; and y_D-y_C>2/3. Suppose A's canonical
tilt is nonnegative and D's is nonpositive. Then

\[
                        \boxed{y_E-y_A\ge g_A+g_E}.      \tag{1}
\]

No H-order, H/I order, or alternate-chain escape hypothesis is used.
Thus (1) applies to C887, Cd402, and Cb882 with their unchanged
original predicates. All nine squares and all 36 physical pairs
remain in the caller. The row sums in this proof use only A through G.

## 1. The complete contrary domain

The [endpoint-inset theorem](00100_ENDPOINT_INSETS_ARE_VERTICAL.md)
proves that both A/E inset orders cover the original physical pair.
Suppose the contrary order

\[
                         y_A-y_E\ge g_A+g_E.             \tag{2}
\]

The [G-order theorem](CB882_G_ABOVE_D.md),
[E-down endpoint bound](CB882_E_DOWN_ENDPOINT.md), and
[E-down middle-height proof](CB882_E_DOWN_F_ABOVE_B.md) give

\[
 y_G-y_D\ge g_D+g_G,\qquad
 x_E+h_E>-11/16,\qquad y_F-y_B\ge g_B+g_F.              \tag{3}
\]

Each cited proof uses no H-order hypothesis. In particular the F-up
input here is the conditional E-down hand proof; it does not depend
on the conclusion (1). The
[left-cap proof](CD402_H_ABOVE_B.md) also supplies |y_A|<1/4 and
x_X+g_X>-15/16 for every original X distinct from A.

The complete finite proof is printed in three appendices:

* [The three roots and every subdivision and successor](00100_C_E_DOWN_COVER.md).
* [All 6,635 literal necessary rows in 109 contexts](00100_C_E_DOWN_ROWS.md).
* [All 1,322 simultaneous bound deductions and 38 terminal row sums](00100_C_E_DOWN_CERTIFICATES.md).

Every coefficient, positive integer weight, and reserve is rational
and printed. Each terminal reserve is positive. No saved numerical
success result or executable is a premise of the proof.

## 4. Exact deductions, transitions, and the complete induction

For a context box z_q in [l_q,u_q], define

\[
 \mathcal L(v)=\sum_{v_q\ge0}v_q l_q+
                   \sum_{v_q<0}v_q u_q.
\]

If a positive weighted sum of the literal rows is A dot z<=B, a
listed deduction `(k,s,b)` follows from the nonnegative reserve

\[
 10^6s b-\left(B-\mathcal L(A-10^6s e_k)\right)\ge0.
                                                               \tag{4}
\]

Every deduction in one context uses its original box, never a bound
from an earlier line in the same batch. A terminal contradiction is
the strictly positive reserve

\[
                         \mathcal L(A)-B>0.              \tag{5}
\]

All weights and rational reserve lower bounds for (4)--(5) are
printed in the certificate appendix.

For a successor, intersect the coordinate bounds with all deductions
of its parent context. A new w interval [l',u'] is justified from
the resulting component bounds b_-<=b(w)<=b_+ by monotonicity:
either l' is the previous lower endpoint or b(l')<=b_-, and either
u' is the previous upper endpoint or b(u')>=b_+. Then intersect
with any angle or necessary physical-axis sign cut printed in the
cover. Recompute all a,b,|b|,g intervals by the stated rational rules.
Every endpoint and every input component bound for these comparisons
is printed in the adjacent contexts. Only integer arithmetic and
rational comparisons are required.

The cover appendix lists all three initial roots, 33 binary angle
subdivisions, one three-way C/D-axis subdivision, every successor,
and all 38 terminal contexts. It contains no open node. Induction
from the three exhaustive A/E roots shows that any hypothetical
original satisfying (2) remains in at least one listed child and
eventually reaches a terminal context. Its positive reserve (5)
is impossible. This excludes (2), and the exhaustive A/E inset
orders prove (1) on the entire stated original-nine caller.

The surviving E-up configurations, including the two unrestricted
extra squares, still require a separate exclusion.

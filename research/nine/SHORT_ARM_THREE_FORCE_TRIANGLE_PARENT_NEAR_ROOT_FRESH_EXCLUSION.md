## 1. A complete force-free far-corner obstruction

can contain P while avoiding int H.

if P is a T vertex, pi if it is a side-interior point, and 2pi

quadrant. At a T vertex its right-angle sector has disjoint
interior from the Southwest quadrant. At least one of its two
own inward axes consequently lies in the closed Northeast
quadrant: a right-angle sector avoiding the open Southwest
quadrant cannot have both endpoints outside the closed Northeast
quadrant. The complete actual unit segment in that axis direction
belongs to T.

At a T side-interior point, its whole inward half-plane must
avoid the Southwest quadrant. Its own inward unit normal is
therefore in the closed Northeast quadrant, and the unit segment
in that direction again belongs to T. This also includes the
weak endpoint axes. In either case T contains P+v for a unit

    |P+v|^2
       =A^2+B^2+1+2(A*v_x+B*v_y)
       >2*(9/10)^2+1+2*(9/10)
       =221/50=1105/256+663/6400>R^2.                 (2)

The vector inequality uses v_x+v_y>=1 and is strict because
both coordinates of P exceed 9/10. This is a contradiction.
Every reflection of both the actual corner and its occupied

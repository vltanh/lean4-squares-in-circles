## 2. A boundary tip cannot have this small transverse proper port

The following local lemma is stated separately because it uses no
triangle or intact-petal premise.

Suppose d>1 and an actual positively loaded unit S has a genuine
positive radial concurrence Q_S=t F_S, t>0. Suppose one of its actual
inward contact normals, directed from its neighbor into S, and the
unchanged application are

    P_S=(w,-P), n=(s,-c),
    0<w<=epsilon<1/60, 11/15<P<1,
    s>=0,c>0,s^2+c^2=1, s/c<w/P,
    Q_S=P_S+k n, k>=0.                                 (6)

**Small-transverse-port lemma.** This entire physical situation is
impossible, with every physical circle mask and original mean retained.

First

    |P_S|^2<1+epsilon^2<(1+epsilon)^2=d^2.               (7)

A unit with two physical circle corners is an actual radial S square,
whose full radial coordinate interval is [d,d+1], so every point has
norm at least d. This physical mask, including either individual zero
weight, is excluded by (7). Thus S has one physical circle corner

    Z=(X,-Y), X>0,Y>0, X^2+Y^2=R^2.

The positive radial concurrence and (6) give

    X/Y=(w+k s)/(P+k c)<=w/P<1/44.

Because d>1 and R<25/12,

    X<25/528<1/20,
    Y>1, 0<Y-P<R-P<27/20.                             (8)

If P_S is a vertex of S, its distance from the different circle vertex
Z is either one or sqrt(2). But (7) gives

    |Z-P_S|>=R-|P_S|>R-d>1,

because d+1=sqrt(R^2-1/4)<R. Also (8) gives

    |Z-P_S|^2=(X-w)^2+(Y-P)^2
              <(1/20)^2+(27/20)^2=73/40<2.             (9)

This is impossible. Every vertex application and side-mean endpoint
which is a vertex of S is included.

Otherwise P_S lies in the relative interior of an actual side of S,
so n is its own inward side axis. Put v=(c,s). In the original
orthonormal n,v frame the true normal interval is [p_n,p_n+1], where

    p_n=P_S.n=ws+Pc>2/3,
    p_v=P_S.v=wc-Ps, 0<p_v<=w.                         (10)

Indeed s/c<1/44 gives c>10/11, and P>11/15. The actual unique
farthest corner maximizes the positive normal coordinate, so

interval. The positive radial relation and Q_S.v=p_v>0 make its

unchanged relation gives

    Z_v=p_v (p_n+1)/(p_n+k)
          <=p_v(1+1/p_n)<(1/60)(5/2)=1/24,             (11)

a contradiction. Both possible locations of the original application
have been exhausted, proving the lemma.

This is the full small-port geometry of

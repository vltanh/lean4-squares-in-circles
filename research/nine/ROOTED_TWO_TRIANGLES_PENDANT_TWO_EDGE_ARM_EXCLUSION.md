## 1. The actual two-edge arm clears its triangle root

The genuine terminal leaf L is S or D. Its neighbor U is physically
circle-clear by the radial-leaf central-endpoint theorem. Its true degree
two therefore transmits equal opposite forces. Rotate the actual leaf
direction to e=e_x. The complete original transmitter law gives

    p_UL=r_L e,   p_AU=x e,   x=r_L-w,
    r_L=d for S,  r_L=r for D,  w in {1,sqrt(2)},
    A subset {z:e.z<=x}.                                  (1)

Both actual applications and the full transverse means are retained.
The D/diagonal alternative is impossible in the common selected convention:
its original UL contact would be vertex/vertex with a radial normal at
the nonzero point r e. The remaining three original formulas satisfy

    |x|<31/60<r.                                           (2)

These are precisely the degree-independent radial-channel comparisons
proved in TRIANGLE_TWO_BRIDGES_CHAIN_EXCLUSION.md, Section 1, equation
(3a). For clarity, use sqrt(2)<17/12, R>=79/40, and
sqrt(R^2-1/4)>19/10. The three values are r-1, d-1, and d-sqrt(2);
the negative endpoint of the last is greater than
19/10-1-17/12=-31/60, and the other lower endpoints are larger.
Their upper endpoints are at most d-1<1/60. Finally
r>79/40-17/12=67/120>31/60.

The actual p_AU belongs to A. Every point of a circle-touching unit has
norm at least r, by its diameter sqrt(2). Equation (2) therefore proves
A physically circle-clear, even if B has extra forces or H was originally
allowed a circle load. No boundary-triangle argument or load assumption
on B is used for this clearance.

## 2. The triangle ray theorem leaves exactly an inward small support

    L is S,  U has side width one,
    x=d-1=epsilon>=0,  d>=1.                              (3)

The application epsilon e is an actual outward radial support of A.

    O belongs to the original closed A.                    (4)

At epsilon=0 this follows directly from p_AU=O. No original side-mean
endpoint or vertex feature is omitted.

## 3. The other genuine leaf is S

If M were D, its genuine root C would contain O in its open interior.
That would overlap the distinct closed A containing O in (4). Therefore
M is S. Its root C is physically circle-clear by its own leaf theorem.
D is positively loaded: it has true degree two and its two neighbors H,C
physically touch, whereas an unloaded degree-two transmitter would put
them beyond two opposite supports of positive width and prevent contact.

We retain H's entire circle system and both original triangle pairs
until physical clearance is proved below. No force equation at B is
replaced by a smaller-degree equation.

## 4. The critical radius: H is clear and the C triangle moment is nonzero

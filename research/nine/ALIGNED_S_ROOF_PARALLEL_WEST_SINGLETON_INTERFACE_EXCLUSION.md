    X=[epsilon,d] times [-b_X,a_X],

own term and zero leaf term. Thus these two axes are impossible.

If one return is West, full vertical balance makes the other horizontal;
the occupied-leaf exclusion makes it West also. Otherwise both return
normals are strict vertex directions, and their vertical components
have opposite signs. Both right-side components would contradict full
horizontal balance. A right/left pair at opposite vertices is excluded
by the purely physical opposite-vertex triangle theorem. Consequently
both strict normals use the two left-side vertices. After one whole
reflection so the physical boundary A is above, the full cover is

    parallel row: XA and XH both West;
    vertex row:   XA Northwest at (epsilon,a_X),

## 2. Obtuse support normals of a unit require port distance at least one

We use the following elementary physical fact.

If p,q are actual support points of a unit square with actual
outward supporting normals u,v, and u dot v<0, then

    |p-q|>=1.                                          (4)

This includes arbitrary supporting side means and all endpoints.

Indeed, if |p-q|<1, two different vertices are impossible.
A vertex and a nonincident side are also impossible, since
their distance is at least one. Two side interiors cannot
belong to opposite sides; they therefore belong to one side
or adjacent sides, whose outward normals are equal or
perpendicular. If one point is a vertex and the other lies on
an incident side, both normals lie in the same closed right-angle
vertex normal cone. Coincident points are covered by that same
normal cone, or by a single side normal. In each of the exhaustive
remaining cases u dot v>=0. This proves (4).

Suppose first h_n<0. By (1),(2),

    g dot h=g_n h_n+g_f h_f<0.

The outward M normals are -g and -h, so (4) applies to its
two actual ports Q and P=(d,1/2). But (1), d<61/60, give

    |Q-P|^2
       <(61/60-1/8)^2+(2/5)^2
        =13753/14400<1.                                (5)

Here d-Q_n>0 because d>1>3/4. The exact reserve in (5) is
647/14400. This excludes the whole strict-inner-corner C
alternative, without using any equation or circle mask at M.

## 3. The entire original C-owned upper-side mean

It remains that h=f and P=(X,1/2), d<=X<=d+1.

If M owns f, its actual f interval is [1/2,3/2]. The strict
signs g_n>0>g_f make its minimum-g support the upper-left

    u=(-c,s),       v=(s,c),       c,s>0, c^2+s^2=1.

whose f coordinate is1/2+s+c>=3/2. This is impossible.

unchanged mean on P+u+[0,1]v. Both whole alternatives give

    Q_f>=1/2+s,       s<2/5,       c>9/10.              (6)

The last inequality follows from c^2=1-s^2>21/25>81/100.

We next determine M's physical circle mask before using its
inside-unit concurrence. The contact point P cannot lie on

    |P+u+v|^2-|Z|^2=-2Xc+s+1

The full true-M2 theorem applies with both actual boundary

But the other original proper port relation is

Thus both cases in (3) are impossible, proving the theorem.

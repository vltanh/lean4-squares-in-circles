# Complete subdivision for the seven-original E-down table

This is the finite induction for [the whole C-family E-order theorem](00100_C_E_ABOVE_A.md). Every context and its necessary rows are printed in the [row appendix](00100_C_E_DOWN_ROWS.md); all simultaneous deductions and terminal sums are printed in the [certificate appendix](00100_C_E_DOWN_CERTIFICATES.md).

All three roots retain the common contrary E-down domain, G-up, F-up, the E-down endpoint inequality, and every original physical hypothesis. The A/E physical-axis routing proves that their union contains every contrary original. The extra E sign in the third root is necessary for that primary normal to point downward.

| Root | A/E owned signed axis from A toward E | Additional frame restriction | First context |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | A negative secondary | None | C000 |
| 1 | E negative secondary | None | C042 |
| 2 | E positive primary | w_E<=0 | C104 |

For every nonterminal context, first apply all its simultaneous deductions using that same context box. A successor then uses the resulting center bounds and the stated outer frame intervals. At `angle i`, the two targets are respectively w_i<=t and w_i>=t, intersected with the already proved interval; both include equality. At `CD axes`, the three targets are the positive C-primary, C-secondary, and D-secondary physical separators, in that order. The necessary sign of C is retained on each selected C axis. A displayed sign annotation records an additional necessary intersection before the child context. The final original in each child is unchanged.

Every transition is checked by interval intersection and the increasing rational function b(w)=2w/(1+w^2), as specified in the theorem chapter. Every terminal is its printed positive row sum. No context is an unproved stopping point.

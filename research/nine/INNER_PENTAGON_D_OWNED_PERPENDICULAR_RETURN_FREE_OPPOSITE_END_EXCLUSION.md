## 10. A uniform height barrier for every shared-AB mean

    f=-tau*g+lambda*h,
    0<=tau<=1, r<=lambda<=r+1,
    tau^2+lambda^2=1.

    alpha*tau>beta*lambda>=alpha*lambda,
    tau>lambda>=0.                                   (19)

Of the two unit directions perpendicular to f,

alpha*g+beta*h. It is the other inward H edge -e.

    e=lambda*g+tau*h,
    g=lambda*e-tau*f,
    h=tau*e+lambda*f.                                (20)

    (T_A)_x=(tau-lambda)*b-a*(1+tau+lambda)>0

    a/b < (tau-lambda)/(1+tau+lambda),

    g_y=-lambda*b-tau*a<0,

This excludes the entire shared-AB alternative.

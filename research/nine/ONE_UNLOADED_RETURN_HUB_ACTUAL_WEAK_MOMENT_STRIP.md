positive leaf. Assume:

   has true positive degree at least three.

   true unloaded degree-two transmitter whose two distinct actual

is positive. The genuine leaf can be S or D, and all boundary degrees

**Theorem.** The following conclusions hold for the actual configuration.

* H meets the closed disk D_epsilon and is physically clear.
* At most one original private edge incident to H has zero origin
  moment; every other private edge is nonzero.
* The original HX moment is nonzero. All original H-to-G moments
  have its opposite weak sign, and at least one has the opposite
  strict sign. This is the entire original H star.

      H=[a,a+1] x [b,c],

## 1. Original domain and the complete shared-E normal form

Retain all nine actual contained unit squares and the original H4_D0
positive graph

    HA, HB, HC, HD, AB, BC, CD, DE, EA.

The circle window is

    79/40<=R<=sqrt(1105)/16.                              (1)

three. C and E retain their full loaded degrees three and two.
Assume A,B,C,E physically touch the circle. This holds for L0124
and for the physical-boundary-A mask of L124, without changing A's
zero total circle coefficient in the latter. All other original
units, contacts, masks and circle coefficients remain.

Suppose HD is free under the strict physical point-normal convention
and DE is an actual positive-length shared side. Normalize D's actual
frame by a rigid chart so that

    D=[0,1]^2,
    P_H=(0,0), D->H=(-a,-b), a,b>0.                    (2)

The vectors here include their full original positive magnitudes.
HD is a physical singleton at a vertex of each unit and the original
normal strictly exposes each vertex.

Because DE is a positive-length side contact, its outward D normal
is one of D's four axes and E is parallel to D. West or South DE
would make the full D force equation give a strict northeast DC
normal at (1,1). The two strict opposite D-vertex returns H and C
cannot physically touch: strict unit reach places their whole units
in x<1,y<1 and x>0,y>0 respectively, so an intersection would lie
in the interior of D. Thus DE is East or North. Reflect the entire
chart across the D diagonal if necessary to make it East.

Write its original force and full physical mean as

    D->E=(mu,0), P_E=(1,s), mu>0,
    E=[1,2] times [r,r+1], -1<r<1,
    max(0,r)<=s<=min(1,r+1).                            (3)

Full D force balance gives DC=(a-mu,b). If mu<a this is again
strict northeast and contradicts the touching opposite returns.
If mu=a it is D's North axis, whose dot product with the actual
southwest DH normal is negative. The physical owned-axis triangle
obstruction excludes that case as well.

Therefore mu>a. Set c=mu-a>0. The exact full D system is

    D->C=(-c,b), P_C=(0,1), mu=a+c.

Its torque about (0,0) is c-mu*s=0. Consequently

    s=c/(a+c), 0<s<1.                                  (4)

The full original shared-side mean in (3) is unchanged; (4) proves
it is strictly inside D's East side, even if it is an endpoint of
the actual overlap with E. In particular all three D applications
are pairwise distinct. Define the original unit DC normal by

    n_C=(-p,q),
    p=c/sqrt(c^2+b^2), q=b/sqrt(c^2+b^2),
    p,q>0, p^2+q^2=1.                                  (5)

No C-side ownership is inferred or imposed. The actual CD singleton
may be C-owned or free, and the proof covers its entire original
normal and application domain.

## 2. The unchanged HC normal points strictly upward

Strict unit reach at H's minimum-southwest support (0,0) gives

    max_H x<1, max_H y<1.

At C's minimum-northwest support (0,1), it gives

    max_C x<1, min_C y>0.

Hence every actual point S in the full HC contact set satisfies

    0<S_y<1, S_x<1.

If S_x>0, S lies in the interior of D, impossible for a point of
another actual unit. If S_x=0, then S belongs to H intersect D,
which is the singleton (0,0), contrary to S_y>0. It follows that

    S_x<0 for every actual S in H intersect C.           (6)

Let N be the unchanged actual normal from H into C, at its actual
singleton application or full shared-side mean S. The two supporting
half-planes, tested at H's (0,0) and C's (0,1), give

    N.(0,0)<=N.S<=N.(0,1), N_y>=0.

If N is East, the common supporting line would give
S_x=max_H x>=0 because (0,0) belongs to H. If N is West, it would
give S_x=max_C x>=0 because (0,1) belongs to C. Both contradict
(6). Thus every original HC owner and mean has

    N_y>0.                                              (7)

No force at H or C has been deleted in this physical sign argument.

## 6. Physical touching and one actual C point force xi<3/2

We first record the exact vertical reach of both touching units:

    max_H y<=a/sqrt(a^2+b^2),
    min_C y>=1-p.                                      (16)

axis v must satisfy a*v_x+b*v_y<=0, whence v_x<0 and
v_y<=a/sqrt(a^2+b^2). This proves the first bound. For C, a
negative-y inward unit axis at its minimum-n_C vertex satisfies
-p*v_x+q*v_y>=0, so v_x<0 and -v_y<=p. At most one inward
axis can have negative y component, giving the second bound.
For a C-owned side, the same bound follows directly from the
complete pose in Section 5: its negative-y tangent extent is
(1-rho)*p<=p, while n_C has positive y component. This retains
all original C masks and applications.

The physical HC intersection therefore implies

    a/sqrt(a^2+b^2)+p>=1.                              (17)

Every unit square contains P+n when P is one of its actual
minimum-n support points and n is a unit vector. At a vertex,
write n in the inward orthonormal frame: both coefficients lie
in [0,1], so that point belongs to the unit. At a side-interior
point the inward normal is the unit side axis, so the complete
normal unit segment is present. Side endpoints are covered by
either calculation. Apply this physical observation to C:

    Z=P_C+n_C=(-p,1+q) belongs to C.                    (18)

Suppose xi>=3/2. By (11), the actual contained point Z obeys

    |Z-O|^2>(3/2+p)^2+(1+q-s)^2.                       (19)

    |Z-O|^2>(3/2+p)^2+q^2
             =13/4+3*p>=35/8>1105/256.

    a/sqrt(a^2+b^2)>5/8,
    b/a<sqrt(39)/5.

By (5), b/c=q/p>sqrt(55)/3, and hence

    c/a<3*sqrt(39)/(5*sqrt(55))<3/5,

    |Z-O|^2>9/2>1105/256,

    xi<3/2, X_E=1-xi>-1/2.                             (20)

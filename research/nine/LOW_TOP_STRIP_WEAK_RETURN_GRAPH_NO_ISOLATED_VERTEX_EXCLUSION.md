    M_I<=0, X_I<0,                                  (3)

and that every member of J physically touches at least one distinct

vertices is sufficient, but is not necessary.

**Full-star theorem.** This entire actual configuration is impossible.

The sums (2) are sums of the original data, not the force and application

    0=X_I+sum_(j in J) (F_j)_x<0,                    (13)

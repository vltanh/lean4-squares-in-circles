# Scope of the finite A stabilization statement

The frozen A constructor admits a coordinate proposal only when its exact
improvement, after outward rounding to the `2^-20` grid, is at least `1/2000`.
Its recorded stabilization after at most two rounds is stabilization under that
selected cutoff. It does not prove that no smaller exact improvement is
possible. The four-round cap was not exceeded, and the A attempt is complete.

The frozen source, payloads, raw report and 44-member delta are unchanged. This
note narrows the reading of “fixed point” in their preparation prose; it adds no
new deduction, row, replay, continuation or closure credit.

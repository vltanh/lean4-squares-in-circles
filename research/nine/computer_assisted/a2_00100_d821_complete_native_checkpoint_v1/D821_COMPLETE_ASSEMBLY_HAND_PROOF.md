## 3. What a rational node certificate proves

The geometry adapter reconstructs each printed necessary row from the
same original caller, its certified intervals, and any retained physical

and the accepted corner inequalities. Each family retains its own hand
derivation and independently checked literal reconstruction.

Write the validated inequalities as `a_j dot z <= b_j`, and let
`|z_k|<=M_k` be the independently proved absolute variable bounds. The
checker reads only nonnegative exact rational row multipliers. If their
weighted sum has coefficient vector a and right side b, then

\[
 0\le b+\sum_k M_k|a_k|.
\]

Consequently a strictly negative right side excludes the node, with
positive rational reserve `-b-sum(M_k*abs(a_k))`. For a bound on an
objective c, the same calculation uses residual `a-c`, proving

\[
 c\cdot z\le b+\sum_k M_k|a_k-c_k|.
\]

The checker verifies the complete rational sum, residual correction,
and outward rounding before intersecting the new bound with the old
one. Empty certified intervals and exact physical-frame contradictions
give separate explicit terminals. Numerical LP points, optimizer
status, split scores, and floating-point reserves are not premises.

The four-product unit-frame corner family substitutes exact matching
rectangle lower planes into the identity

\[
 \|c+(\varepsilon u+\tau v)/2\|^2
 =x^2+y^2+\tfrac12+\varepsilon\alpha x+\tau\alpha y
  +\varepsilon\beta y-\tau\beta x.
\]

The expanded corner norm minus its affine lower expression is two
coordinate squares plus four nonnegative rectangle products. The final
inequality's total slack is the sum of those six terms and the original

Rebuilding those planes from the current independently
propagated intervals is essential; a plane from a different caller is
not transported. The accepted packet and root review prove the entire

missing branch as excluded.

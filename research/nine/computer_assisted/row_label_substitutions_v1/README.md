# Two literal applications of accepted seven-original row theorems

Two accepted theorems quantify over distinct physical originals rather
than fixed names. These bindings make two additional applications explicit:

| Accepted theorem | Literal application | Quantitative row guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opposite-endpoint full-angle AHID row | Source H is original B | A,B,I,D ordered; opposite A/D signs; total variation at most 23/100 |
| Actual-AB two-link row | Source I is original H or I | B,X,D ordered; two-link variation at most 3/5; accepted D/G sign and strict horizontal-gap guards |

The complete hand proofs are
[opposite ABID](opposite_abid/tree/OPPOSITE_ABID_LITERAL_SUBSTITUTION.md)
and [either extra in the actual-AB row](actual_ab_either_extra/ACTUAL_AB_ANY_EXTRA_LITERAL_HAND_PROOF.md).
The [root physical review](ROOT_PHYSICAL_REVIEW.md) explains the unchanged
physical caller and the closed-Q equality contradiction.

Neither operation relabels the original mask, path, marked bits or physical
constraints. Temporary permutations are used only by the discovery guard.
The independent guards directly read the actual original indices and
reconstruct each printed bound. Both applications retain all nine frames,
36 original corners and pairs, five original center cones and exact bits.

## Exact replay and inherited scalar proofs

Run `python -B -S verify_all.py --output replay.json` from this directory.
The driver verifies every delivered source hash and relocates both guard
suites into a temporary directory before executing them with the Python
standard library. The opposite-ABID suite checks 1,458 states and rejects 273
wrong physical rows. The either-extra suite checks 12,150 states, 54 correlated
translation cases and 3,024 damaged literals. The finite controls validate the
guard implementations; the literal-substitution proofs establish the
continuous physical implications.

The scalar tables themselves are unchanged accepted inputs:

- [Opposite-endpoint source](../opposite_tilt_ahid_row/README.md): 80 complete
  domains, 1,405 leaves and 878,125 exact coefficients.
- [Actual-AB source](../actual_AB_two_link_row_v1/ACTUAL_AB_TWO_LINK_ROW_HAND_PROOF.md):
  28 complete domains, 103 leaves and 12,875 exact coefficients.

This package records fresh guard arithmetic, not another replay of those
scalar tables. Actual original-cell applications must bind and independently
replay their inherited scalar source together with their original ancestry
and complete closed cover. All closure credit remains zero here: 220/231
original cells and 8/32 masks are still closed.

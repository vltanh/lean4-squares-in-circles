# Two-direction theorem: audit and remaining global gap

The previous two-direction summary is a **conditional draft**, not a certified global gate. The implication from Greene's partition (3,3,3) to a geometrically valid 3x3 skew grid needs a separate proof: chain-antichain ranks alone do not supply the claimed unique ordered grid with all required projection inequalities. The claimed support minimizations and the four-case algebra also require independent derivation from the physical square frames. Until these steps are checked, do not mark the two-direction theorem as proved.

The global gap is more fundamental: a packing can have pairwise separating normals distributed among more than two directions. The exact unrestricted emitter covers these, but the existing row certificate does not. A complete proof must either (i) give a finite, exact, exhaustive certificate over all 36 pair disjunctions with all zero faces, or (ii) prove a global geometric/stress reduction whose hypotheses are verified for every minimizer. A certificate of the conditional two-direction theorem cannot substitute for this.

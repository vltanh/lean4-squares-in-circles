## Native-only boundary

The 48 checker source snapshots are preserved and hash-bound, but the new
launcher imports them from the retained native runtime. That runtime still
expects the published legacy inventories, fixtures and original path layout.
Those fixtures are not rebuilt into a cold packet by this delta. Changing
their paths requires a checked transport; source snapshots alone do not
establish that transport.

The other 468 inherited old tree records are outside the fresh ancestry
replay and outside this small archive. Their identities and every sibling
remain represented by the source and grafted manifests and the inherited
hash inventory. Their existing proofs must remain available for eventual
whole-original assembly. The delta itself proves only the eight specified
substitutions and retains seventeen unfinished alternatives.

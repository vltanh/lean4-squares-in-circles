## Why these rows help

The original unit-frame identity makes the squared norm of every corner

    x²+y²+1/2+epsilon alpha x+tau alpha y
               +epsilon beta y-tau beta x.

Each of its four coordinate/frame products receives an independent valid
rectangle lower plane. The center squares receive rational tangent lower
planes. All sixteen rectangle patterns are simultaneously necessary
inequalities. The full slack also includes the original corner's disk
containment slack. `SAME_STATE_PHYSICAL_ATTACHMENT.md` gives the derivation
and exact caller integration boundary.

`strict011_four_product_rows.Context` calls the unchanged fresh physical
model first and constructs the extra rows from its returned current boxes.
`independent_strict011_four_product.Adapter` calls the unchanged independent
adapter first and sends additional rows, with those independently propagated
boxes, to the unchanged accepted decoder. It preserves exact row indices,
ordinary rows, absolute bounds and all selected SAT auxiliary variables.

The optional numerical suggestion module calls no solver and returns only
parameters. Parameters may be carried forward; their rows must always be
rebuilt for the current certified state. Cached coefficients from an earlier
state are not accepted.

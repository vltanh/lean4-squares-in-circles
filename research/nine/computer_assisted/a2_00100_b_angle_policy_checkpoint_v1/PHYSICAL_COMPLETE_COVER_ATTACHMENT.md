## Discovery policy and actual exercised behavior

The new B7c policy allows normalized physical-corner angle selection once
either two physical SAT alternatives or five proxy alternatives establish
placement. At its first retained example, old node349 had one selected SAT
pair and five selected proxy pairs. A fresh phase point selected a G cut
at0 with normalized corner excess about0.172924. Its previously completed
three parent bound rounds were retained; no extra parent bound round was
needed before this cut. Both new children subsequently closed.

The B16a example exercises the later-round callback itself. Old node189
had seven selected proxies and no selected SAT pair. After its second
completed bound round, the phase point already computed by the solver
selected C at0, with normalized corner excess about0.102023. The completed
two-round parent prefix and both excluded children are all in the exact
replay. This example would have waited until the remaining round budget
under the preceding first-round-only return rule.

The early-return thresholds remain normalized corner excess at least1/10
or the existing frame defect at least1/5, with angle width at least1/16.
The callback adds no solve, mathematical row or proof rule. The new
`b_completed_round_angle_search_explicit.py` takes the actual runtime engine
object as an explicit argument; all three driver call sites pass that object.
The older B16a callback's runtime identity was separately checked and its
exact source bytes are included. Every completed bound round is preserved.

Floating phase points and branch scores only select the finite proof to try.
Acceptance depends on independently reconstructed exact rows, nonnegative
rational multipliers, paid coefficient residuals, positive exact reserves,
and the complete closed covers. It does not depend on the accuracy of the
discovery score or on a numerical infeasibility claim.

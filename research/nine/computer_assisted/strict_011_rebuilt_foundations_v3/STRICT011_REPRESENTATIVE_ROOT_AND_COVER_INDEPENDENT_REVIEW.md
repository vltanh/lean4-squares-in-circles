every canonical frame `(alpha,beta)` to `(alpha,-beta)`. Its u-axis is the
reflected old u-axis; its v-axis is the negative of the reflected old v-axis.
Consequently the corner-sign bijection is `(epsilon,tau)` to

does not. Relative sine changes sign. The reviewed polynomial checker
independently expands all resulting support literals and the five exact

Swapping the complete physical originals H and I fixes all marked labels A
through G. All corners and pairs are permuted. A pair whose sorted-label

and this swap commute. Applying reflection only when beta_D is negative,
and the swap only when x_H exceeds x_I, produces both beta_D>=0 and x_H<=x_I.
Both zero faces are retained. These are representative restrictions after
a whole-caller bijection, not unconditional inequalities for an arbitrary
untransformed caller or any registered 001 original cell.

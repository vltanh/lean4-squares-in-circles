## 2. The free roof makes the D normal strictly steeper

The D frame (u,v) is orthonormal and left-handed. In that frame put

    alpha=(r0,-s0), f=(-s0,-r0),
    r0=e*H>0, s0=e*(a-b*p)>0,
    r0^2+s0^2=1.                                   (5)

Here H=b+a*p<1 implies
a-b*p > a-b*(1-b)/a = (1-b)/a>0. The unit identity in (5)
also follows directly from c=e*p and a^2+b^2=c^2+e^2=1.

In (1), alpha and f are the two inward unit side directions at
the actual T vertex u. Since beta supports T there as its inward
normal and is genuinely free, its two coefficients are strictly
positive:

    beta=x*alpha+y*f, x,y>0.

The original selected beta is unchanged. Equations (2),(5) give

    r=x*r0-y*s0>0,
    s=x*s0+y*r0>0,
    r0*s-s0*r=y*(r0^2+s0^2)=y>0.                   (6)

Thus s/r>s0/r0. The strict inequality is the entire geometric
content needed from the free normal. It remains valid throughout
the full allowed vertex cone; no angular subrange is imposed.

## 3. Full D3 balance locates its original shared-side mean

Let gamma,nu,kappa>0 be the original magnitudes on AD,DT,DC.
The complete D force equation is

    -gamma*East+nu*beta+kappa*v=0.

Since East=a*u+b*v, its two actual components and its original
moment about the unchanged AD application are

    nu*r=gamma*a,
    kappa=gamma*b+nu*s,
    nu*s=kappa*tau.                                 (7)

The last sign uses det(u,v)=-1:
det(u,beta)=s and det(v+tau*u,v)=-tau. The circle load at D is
zero and there are exactly these three positive pair forces, so
(7) neither drops nor adds a force.

Eliminating only their positive magnitudes gives the exact identity
for the unchanged mean,

    tau=a*s/(b*r+a*s).                              (8)

Put tau0=a*s0/(b*r0+a*s0). All denominator terms are positive.
Equation (6) gives, without an approximation,

    tau-tau0
       = a*b*(s*r0-s0*r)
           /[(b*r+a*s)*(b*r0+a*s0)] > 0.            (9)

Moreover the actual frame identities yield

    b*r0+a*s0
       =e*[b*(b+a*p)+a*(a-b*p)]=e,
    tau0=a*(a-b*p).                                (10)

The number tau0 is only a comparison value computed from the
existing alpha. No hypothetical contact or replacement force
system is asserted at tau0.

## 4. The whole physical B and C are strictly separated

Both u and v have positive horizontal components a,b. The entire
actual square (3) consequently has

    min_(z in C) z_x = b+a*delta
       >= b+a*tau-a
       > b+a*tau0-a
       = b*(1-a*H)>0.                              (11)

The weak inequality is precisely the actual-mean membership
delta>=tau-1. The strict inequality is (9), and the exact equality
uses (10), a^2+b^2=1 and H=b+a*p. Finally 0<a<1 and 0<H<1
make the last sign strict.

Equations (4),(11) separate the entire actual B and C by the

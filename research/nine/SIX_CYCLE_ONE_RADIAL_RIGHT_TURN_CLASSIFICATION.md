## 4. Exhaust both actual length choices and force the 45-degree frame

Let \(L_B,L_C\in\{1,\sqrt2\}\) be the unchanged lengths from

actual B-to-C concurrence distance, including its unchanged

\[
|z_C-z_B|=1+L'_B+L'_C.
\tag{2}
\]

so it is right isosceles with this side as hypotenuse. Combining

\[
\sin\alpha+1+L_B
=\cos\alpha+1+L_C
=\frac{1+L'_B+L'_C}{\sqrt2}.
\tag{3}
\]

The complete four ordered length choices are now immediate.

* If one of \(L_B,L_C\) is \(\sqrt2\) and the other is one, the

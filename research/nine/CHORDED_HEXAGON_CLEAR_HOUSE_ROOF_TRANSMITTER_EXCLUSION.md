## 2. The remaining complete two-hub geometry

Write the full A-to-B unit normal as eta. Equation (6) and complete
A balance give the exact formula

    v_0=t/(1-pH),            0<v_0<1,
    eta=(-1,-v_0)/sqrt(1+v_0^2).                         (11)

The minimum-eta side of the actual B contains S in its relative
interior. Let

    A_0=1/sqrt(1+v_0^2),     B_0=v_0/sqrt(1+v_0^2).

Then eta=(-A_0,-B_0), Jeta=(B_0,-A_0), and the actual support-span
bound gives

    max_B x <= -1+B_0 <0.                               (12)

### 2.1. Family I with a D-owned DC is already impossible

The D concurrence is the intersection of the line through u along
alpha with the horizontal AD line:

    q_D=(a-bp,0).

If D owns DC, (4) gives tau=a(a-bp). The full horizontal support-span
bound at the actual C port, with normal v=(b,-a), yields

    min_C x >= b+tau a-a
             =b(1-aH)>0.                               (13)

The equality uses a^2+b^2=1 and H=b+ap. Its last sign is strict
because 0<a,H<1. This contradicts (12) and physical BC. Every
D-owned singleton, genuine shared side and original mean is included.

Thus family I has a C-owned DC at v. Write its actual unit normal as

    zeta=-l u+m v,      l,m>0, l^2+m^2=1.

If nu,kappa are the actual DT,DC magnitudes, the complete unloaded
D force and torque give

    nu(r-s)=gamma a>0,      kappa(m-l)=gamma b>0.          (14)

Hence r>s and m>l. Here beta=alpha, so

    r=d(b+ap)=dH,       s=d(a-bp).                       (15)

The complete geometry also gives a>b. Indeed, if b>=a, then
zeta_x=mb-la>0 and the support-span bound gives min_C x>b-1.
Equation (11), or the full A torque, gives max_B x<-1+1/sqrt(2).
Since b>=a implies b>=1/sqrt(2), these bounds forbid BC.

In particular,

    a>b>0,    a>1/sqrt(2),    p>(a-b)/(a+b).             (16)

The last inequality is exactly r>s from (15). The actual C normal
line runs from the proper interior q_D toward v, so also

    zeta=(-w,-a)/sqrt(a^2+w^2),    w=a-b(1+p).            (17)

Its first component is allowed either sign; no sign of w is assumed.

### 2.2. Family II retains the entire shared-C slide

## 3. A real T--U--B channel forces a very small lower slope

Suppose U's two neighbors are T,B. The actual A is a physical unit
bypass. UNIT_BYPASS_NORMAL_CHANNEL.md gives a single side channel
of reserve one and transverse separation of U from A.

Orient a unit vector w from B toward T. Let P_T and P_B be the
unchanged actual U applications on T and B. Then

    P_T-P_B=w,
    min_T w.x - max_B w.x=1.                            (20)

Since P in T and S in B have P-S=(1,1), write

    w=(h,k), h,k>=0, h^2+k^2=1.                         (21)

Indeed w.(P-S)>=1; a negative coordinate would make the sum of
the two coordinates strictly less than one.

Let j=(-k,h). The full A projection interval in this direction is

    [-ht, k+hH].

The transverse bypass theorem retains exactly two possible placements:

    U subset {j.x<=-ht},       or       U subset {j.x>=k+hH}. (22)

Neither an A-U contact nor a zero transverse gap is assumed.

### 3.1. The lower transverse placement is impossible

Every minimum-w point of T is on its minimum-alpha side, because
w.alpha=hc+kd>0. On that side w.f=-hd+kc.

If w.f<0, the minimum selects its positive f endpoint relative to P.
Its j coordinate is strictly greater than hH, so cannot be <=-ht.

If w.f>=0, then kc>=hd. This forces k>0, and for h>0 gives
k/h>=d/c>1. The maximum-w support of B is on its minimum-eta side,
because w.eta<0. Its tangential coefficient is

    w.Jeta=(h v_0-k)/sqrt(1+v_0^2)<0.

This is strict also when h=0. It selects the negative Jeta endpoint
relative to S. Since j.Jeta<0, the actual B port has j coordinate
at least

    j.S=k-ht>-ht.

Thus it too cannot lie in the lower alternative of (22).
This covers the equality w.f=0 and both axial w endpoints.

### 3.2. Full upper-placement reach, including all means

Hence the upper alternative of (22) holds.

The case h=0 is impossible: every minimum-w T port lies on its
minimum-alpha side, whose f coordinate relative to P is at most
1-ell. Its j coordinate cannot reach k+hH=1, since that would require
(1-ell)d>=1.

The case k=0 is impossible as well. The maximum-w B port selects
the positive Jeta endpoint, so its j coordinate is at most j.S=-t,
whereas the upper placement requires at least H>0.

Therefore h,k>0. The actual minimum-w T port has the form

    P_T=P+q f,        q<=1-ell.

Its required j coordinate is at least k+hH. Thus

    k <= (1-ell)(kd+hc).                                (23)

For the actual B port, its required j coordinate exceeds j.S by
at least h>0. Because j.Jeta<0, its tangential coordinate must be
negative. The maximum-w feature can have this sign only if
w.Jeta<=0. This includes a genuine side contact with an arbitrary
actual mean. Therefore

    k/h >= v_0.                                        (24)

Set z=d-a>0. Equations (9),(23),(24) give the necessary inequality

    v_0 <= k/h <= p z/(1-z),       z=d-a.                (25)

The denominator is positive: z<1-a<1. This deduction kept every
actual T/B side mean and both possible transverse orders. It used
no circle or degree equation at T or B.

## 4. The whole T--U--B domain is impossible

We now combine (25) with the complete lower-square reach.

### 4.1. Entire range b<=1/2

Here a>=sqrt(3)/2>4/5 and d>a. Hence

    p<b/a<=5/8,       0<z<1-a<1/5.

The exact identity

    z=d^2(b-ap)(b+ap)/(d+a)                             (26)

follows from d^2=1/(1+p^2) and a^2+b^2=1.
Also b-ap=2b-H<=1-H=t. Since 0<d,H<1 and d+a>1,
(26) gives z<t. Consequently

    pz/(1-z) < (25/32)t < t < v_0,                     (27)

contradicting (25). This includes b=1/2 without a zero-denominator
limit or discarded shared endpoint.

### 4.2. Entire range b>1/2

Now p<(1-b)/a by H<1, and z<1-a. Therefore

    pz/(1-z) < (1-b)(1-a)/a^2
              =(1-a)/(1+b)<1/5,                       (28)

where a>1/sqrt(2)>7/10 and 1+b>3/2 prove the last strict bound.
Thus v_0<1/5 and

    max_B x <=-1+B_0<-4/5.                             (29)

In family II, (18) gives min_C x=b+delta a>b-a>-1/2.
This already contradicts (29).

In family I, use the entire normal cover (17). If w<=0, its
horizontal component is nonnegative, and min_C x>=b-1>-1/2.

If w>0, put q=w/a. By (16),

    0<q=1-b(1+p)/a < (a-b)/(a+b) < 2/7.                (30)

For the last comparison, b>1/2 and a<sqrt(3)/2<9/10;
the ratio increases with a and decreases with b, so it is strictly
less than (9/10-1/2)/(9/10+1/2)=2/7.

The full C-owned side geometry or the support-span bound gives

    min_C x >= b-(a+w)/sqrt(a^2+w^2)
              >= b-1-q > -11/14 > -4/5.               (31)

The last reserve is 1/70. This contradicts (29) again.

Equations (27)--(31) exclude both complete roof families whenever
the actual transmitter connects T to B.

## 5. A real T--U--C channel in family I is impossible

It remains to keep the other endpoint choice at the unchanged U.
Family I has C-owned DC by Section 2.1. Work now in the actual
left-handed orthonormal D frame (u,v). Its contact points are

    p_DT=u=(1,0),     p_DC=v=(0,1),
    alpha=(r,-s),    zeta=(-l,m),

with r>s>0 and m>l>0 from (14). The global positive quarter-turn has

    f=Jalpha=(-s,-r),       Jzeta=(m,l)                 (32)

in this left-handed frame. The real T side contains u and
P=u+ell f; its extent from u in direction -f is at most 1-ell.

Orient w from C toward T. The actual reserve-one channel gives
w.(u-v)>=1, hence

    w=(h,-k), h,k>=0, h^2+k^2=1.
    j=(k,h).

The whole D projection interval on j is [0,h+k]. The actual U lies
either below zero or above h+k in that transverse coordinate.

### 5.1. The lower transverse placement cannot occur

The minimum-w T face is its minimum-alpha face because
w.alpha=hr+ks>0. A T port with j coordinate <=0 must lie from u
toward positive f. The coefficient w.f=-hs+kr must therefore be
nonpositive.

The maximum-w C face is its minimum-zeta face because
w.zeta=-hl-km<0. A C port with j coordinate <=0 must lie from v
toward negative Jzeta. Therefore w.Jzeta=hm-kl must be nonpositive.

If h,k>0 these imply

    h/k >= r/s >1,       h/k <= l/m <1,

a contradiction. If h=0, the minimum-w T point is on the negative
f end and has positive j coordinate. If k=0, the maximum-w C point
is on the positive Jzeta end and has positive j coordinate.
Thus both axial limits are excluded too.

### 5.2. The upper placement contradicts the full D3 torque

The cases k=0 and h=0 also fail in the upper placement: for k=0
the minimum-w T point is toward positive f and has negative j
coordinate, while for h=0 the maximum-w C point is toward negative
Jzeta and has negative j coordinate. Hence h,k>0.

The T port must exceed j.u=k by at least h. Its available negative-f
extent is at most 1-ell, so

    h <= (1-ell)(ks+hr),
    h/k <= (1-ell)s/[1-(1-ell)r].                       (33)

The C port must exceed j.v=h by at least k. It therefore lies toward
positive Jzeta, and maximum-w support requires

    h/k >= l/m.                                        (34)

All equal-axis support faces and their actual means remain in these
weak inequalities.

The *complete* unchanged D force/torque equations (14) give exactly

    l/m = a s/[b r+(a-b)s] > s.                         (35)

The denominator is positive and strictly less than a, since a>b
and 0<r,s<1.

On the other hand ell=a/d>a>1/sqrt(2)>1/2, while
r/(1+r)<1/2. Thus

    (1-ell)(1+r)<1,
    (1-ell)s/[1-(1-ell)r] < s.                          (36)

Its denominator is positive. Equations (33)--(36) contradict one
another. The whole parallel-roof T--U--C family is excluded.

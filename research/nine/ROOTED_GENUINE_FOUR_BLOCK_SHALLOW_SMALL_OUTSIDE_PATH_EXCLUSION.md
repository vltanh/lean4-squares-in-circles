## 1. Uniform theorem and exact outside-path hypotheses

Let Q0 be a genuine terminal four-body biconnected positive block at H:
each of its three private bodies has no positive pair edge outside Q0.
H may retain arbitrary further positive edges and its full original
circle system. Suppose O belongs to int H.

Let n,j be actual orthonormal own directions of H. Suppose its n-facing
own side has positive support sigma<=epsilon, and an actual positive
edge HX has unchanged application p on that closed side, with

    p.n=sigma, 0<sigma<=epsilon, |p|<3/8.               (2)

The application may be a relative-side point or a side endpoint.
Its actual normal need not equal n at an endpoint. No degree, force
balance, circle mask or concurrence is assumed at X.

Assume there is an actual simple positive path

    H-X-X2-...-L                                      (3)

whose vertices after H are all distinct from Q0's private bodies,
and whose final actual unit L is physically circle-touching. All
original degrees and any extra forces at the path vertices remain.

**Theorem.** This entire configuration is impossible.

The path in (3) is a subset of the actual selected graph. It is not
a newly inserted contact or an abstract replacement for a branch
force. The proof uses only its physical embedded path to the circle.

## 5. Scope of the extension

The new conclusion uses neither a two-pair equation at X nor an
external resultant substituted for the path in (3). It does not
require a C4 outside Q0, a particular leaf equation, a force sign

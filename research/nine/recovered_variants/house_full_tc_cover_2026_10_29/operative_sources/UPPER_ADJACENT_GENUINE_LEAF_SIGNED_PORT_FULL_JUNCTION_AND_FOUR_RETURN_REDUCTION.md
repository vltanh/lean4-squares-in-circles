## 1. A positive resultant gives a positive actual application coordinate

Keep the complete physical hypotheses of the accepted boundary-free
two-application theorem3808: actual contained unit squares with pairwise
disjoint open interiors in the same disk about O, sqrt(2)<R<1+sqrt(2),

literal original application values. At each such value P let G_P be

**Theorem A.** Fix any nonzero linear coordinate e, and suppose e.F>0.

1. If there is only one actual application P, then e.P>0.
2. If there are two distinct actual applications and e.G_P>=0 at one
   of them, then e.P>0.

The complete3808 theorem gives P=sF, s>0, in the first case. In the
distinct nonparallel case it gives q=sF=P-lambda*G_P for some lambda>=0,
after absorbing the positive group norm into lambda. Thus

    e.P=s*(e.F)+lambda*(e.G_P)>0.                 (1)

Its exact distinct parallel exception is an actual radial S square:
both original groups and every original pair normal point strictly
opposite the full radial F. Therefore e.G_P<0 when e.F>0, contrary to
the second hypothesis. These are all three complete alternatives.
Physical-two concurrence may be outside K; membership of q in K was
not used. Coincident original applications are handled by the first
alternative, not by assigning the sign of one constituent force to its
whole coincident group.

**Corollary A2.** For a true loaded degree-two K, if e.F>0 and one
original outward pair force at its actual P has nonnegative e projection,

for coincident applications the first part of Theorem A already applies.

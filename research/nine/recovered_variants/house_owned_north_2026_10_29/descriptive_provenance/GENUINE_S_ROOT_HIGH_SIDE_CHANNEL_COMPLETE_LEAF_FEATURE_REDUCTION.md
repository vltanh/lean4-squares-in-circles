## 3. Every other possible actual leaf vertex is excluded

    |P|^2<=epsilon^2+q^2<1/3600+25/64<4/9<d^2.     (7)

Therefore

    p.u=P.u=-h*a+q*t<=0,
    q*t<=h*a<=epsilon*a.                            (8)

epsilon=0. Moreover a>q/2. Otherwise a<=q/2<5/16 and (8)

    a^2+t^2<(5/16)^2+(1/120)^2<1.

The exact squared norm consequently satisfies

    |P+v|^2=1+h^2+q^2-2*h*t-2*q*a
              <1+h^2<=1+epsilon^2<d^2.             (9)

All strict inequalities are justified by q,t,h>0 and a>q/2.
This contradicts |p|=d. The argument even retained the weak
radial-support boundary in (8); it does not omit an own-side
limit by pretending a selected normal is strict when it is not.

### 3.2. The opposite vertex is uniformly too far for this radial support

    |P+u+v|^2-d^2

This is one full-interval comparison. It excludes the whole

The only remaining non-axis vertex is P+u. Its actual equation

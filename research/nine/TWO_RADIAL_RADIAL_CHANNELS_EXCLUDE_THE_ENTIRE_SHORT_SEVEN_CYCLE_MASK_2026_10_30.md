### 3.1 A reusable full-degree radial-star identity

The same retained-circle constraint has a general form. Let an actual
radial loaded square have every original positive pair application at
one of its two strict inner vertices p_+=(d,1/2), p_-=(d,-1/2).
At p_sigma write each unchanged outward normal as
(-c_i,sigma*s_i), c_i,s_i>0, with magnitude mu_i>0. No bound
on the number of forces at either vertex is imposed, and no other
positive pair force is omitted. Put

    h_i=d*s_i+c_i/2,
    M=sum_plus mu_i*h_i=sum_minus mu_i*h_i,
    r_plus=(sum_plus mu_i*c_i)/M,
    r_minus=(sum_minus mu_i*c_i)/M.

The equality defining M is the full origin torque equation. Both
sets of forces must be nonempty, and M,r_plus,r_minus are positive.
The radial and tangential components of the complete circle resultant
are exactly

    F_e=M*(r_plus+r_minus),
    F_f=M*(r_plus-r_minus)/(2d).

Indeed sum_sigma mu_i*s_i=M*(1-r_sigma/2)/d, and the
circle force is the negative sum of all original pair forces.
Nonnegative individual circle coefficients are therefore precisely

    |r_plus-r_minus| <= (d/(d+1))*(r_plus+r_minus),
    1/(2d+1) <= r_plus/r_minus <= 2d+1.             (3.6)

Both endpoints correspond to an allowed zero individual circle weight.
For a true-degree-two radial star, r_sigma=c_sigma/h_sigma.
This includes the RR port used above, whose h=d and c=sin(2phi).
The formula is a necessary full-star criterion for arbitrary degrees
at the stated two physical vertices, not permission to omit a force
on another side or at another application. It may be reused directly
in branching blocks satisfying that complete support premise.

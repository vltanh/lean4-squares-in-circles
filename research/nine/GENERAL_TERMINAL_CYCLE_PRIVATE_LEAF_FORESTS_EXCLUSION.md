## 1. Complete local theorem and the actual outside component

Let the selected positive graph contain the simple cycle

    H,T_1,...,T_m,H, m>=2.                                (2)

At each private T_i the only positive edges are its two cycle edges
and any number of genuine leaves attached to T_i. There are no private
chords or other private positive forces. The root H keeps arbitrary
other positive forces and its entire original circle load. Suppose
a different component of G minus H from the private cycle path

    O in int E.                                           (3)

**Private-leaf cycle theorem.** This entire configuration is impossible.

The theorem permits every placement and number of actual private
genuine leaves, every private circle-load mask, and arbitrary additional
root branches. It does not call (2) a terminal block of the full
block-cut tree when its private vertices carry leaf bridges.

Every closed unit of (2), and every private leaf, avoids O by (3).
The geometric two-leaf-root theorem in
TRIANGLE_THREE_BRIDGES_DOUBLE_LEAF_ROOT_EXCLUSION.md Section 1 puts O
in the closed root of any two distinct genuine leaves, including its
exceptional d=1 origin-vertex case. It uses no equation at the root's
other neighbors. Thus at most one genuine leaf can occur at each T_i.

The outside component containing E has a positive circle load B.
Otherwise it is a nonempty all-unloaded set with its only positive
external neighbors in H, contradicting CY.LOAD.1. The actual positive
E-to-B path avoids H, all private cycle bodies, and all private leaves.

No actual physical cycle (2) can have nonzero winding about O. This
is the complete outside-branch argument of
GLOBAL_CLOSURE_TERMINAL_SIMPLE_CYCLE_ORIGIN_BRANCH_EXCLUSION.md Section 2:
choose the arbitrarily close common physical plane embedding to avoid

The disjoint original positive E-to-B path keeps the actual boundary

the unbounded face. Weak contact junctions are included by choosing
the occupied routing neighborhoods smaller than the fixed clearances.
No hole-area or completion-capacity theorem is used here.

## 2. Full external vectors at every original private body

for its full original circle resultant; it may be zero. If G_i is

    epsilon=d-1>0,
    G_i=lambda_i e_i, lambda_i>0,                         (4)

    det(P_1,u)=k, det(P_2,v)=-k.                          (5)

If a genuine private leaf exists and k=0, an actual cycle contact at
its origin-free root is a radial nonleaf contact with another
origin-free unit. ROOTED_ORIGIN_FREE_RADIAL_CONTACT_EXCLUSIONS.md
Section 3 excludes it, with no degree premise at that other unit.

    k>0.                                                  (6)

At least one G_i is nonzero: a real private leaf supplies one, or

## 5. The complete private radial path, including zero steps

Contract genuine unloaded private bodies only to their actual
transmission segments in the auxiliary representative. Retain all
reserves and means. Use (7) for a nonparallel positive private pair
and the two real nodes (8) for a parallel pair. Consecutive nodes
q_1,...,q_s and their directed vectors f satisfy

    q_(j+1)-q_j=L_j f_j, L_j>=0,
    det(q_j,f_j)=k,
    K_j q_j=f_(j-1)-f_j, K_j>0.                         (10)

The first and last directed vectors are u and -v. Every actual
channel and every internal parallel step has strictly positive
length. Summing the unchanged private equations and (9) gives

    M=sum_i G_i=sum_j K_j q_j=u+v.                       (11)

A zero step identifies consecutive nonzero nodes. Combine their
positive radial coefficients and cancel the intervening vector.
This deletes only a zero segment and preserves (10)--(11).

At least two nodes remain. A parallel pair has the two distinct
ports in (8), separated by a positive step. If there are at least
two nonparallel positive private pairs and no parallel pair, their
G_i directions are distinct. To see the full physical scope, map
each such i to its actual boundary unit: T_i itself for a loaded
degree-two body, and its real leaf L_i for a leaf root. These units
are all distinct. Genuine leaves have positive degree one, so none
can equal a cycle body, and two different roots cannot share the
same genuine leaf. Actual boundary-center directions differ by more
than pi/5; each full nonnegative circle resultant lies within pi/12
of its own center. Since pi/5>2(pi/12), the directions cannot agree.

The only possible one-node case is therefore one nonparallel positive
private pair, with every other private body a true unloaded
transmitter. This includes a lone genuine-leaf root. Its actual
two cycle directions, and hence u,v, are nonparallel. Properness and
the actual end channels give

    q=P_1+c_1u=P_2+c_2v, c_1,c_2>=0.

Since m>=2, at least one coefficient is positive. If the other is
zero, q is both in H and strictly on an outward normal ray of H.
If both are positive, both actual ports are the unique nearest
point of the convex H to q, forcing equality of the ports and then
equality of two nonparallel rays. Both alternatives are impossible.

Thus after deleting zero steps there are at least two nodes, and
every remaining internal segment has strictly positive determinant.
Let eta>0 be its total positive angular advance; each internal
increment lies in (0,pi). Actual endpoint properness gives

    q_1=P_1+c_1u, q_s=P_2+c_2v, c_1,c_2>=0.             (12)

The end segments P_1-to-q_1 and q_s-to-P_2 have nonnegative angular
advance, positive when nonzero, because their directed lines have
moment k>0. All substitutions above preserve the winding of the
unchanged original cycle by the O-free physical hulls and chords.

## 6. The full root closure has the same uniform contradiction

The closed convex H avoids O, so its angular shadow has width less
than pi. If eta>=pi, closing the private representative inside H
has advance greater than -pi and the full loop has positive winding.
Its original simple physical cycle therefore has winding one,
contradicting Section 1. It remains to consider 0<eta<pi.

Suppose first u,v are nonparallel and their actual lines intersect
at q. Equation (5) gives q=tM. The positive radial nodes in their
short endpoint cone give M=Aq_1+Bq_s with A,B>0. Write
q_1=q+a u, q_s=q+b v. Coefficient comparison yields

    Aa=Bb=1-t(A+B).

Neither coefficient vanishes, since the endpoint vectors are
independent. Both cannot be negative: (12) would place q on two
strict outward normal rays of H, contradicting nearest-point
uniqueness. Thus a,b>0. With Delta=det(u,v),

    k=-t Delta>0,
    det(q_1,q_s)=[t(a+b)+ab]Delta>0.

These signs force t<0. Write q=-sigma M, sigma>0. The auxiliary
polygon q,q_1,...,q_s,q has positive increments, with its internal
advance less than pi and each new root-line increment less than
pi. Its total positive advance is below 3pi, hence equals 2pi.

For any actual D in H, the difference from the actual representative
is D,P_1,q,P_2,D. Its triangle D,P_1,P_2 has zero winding inside H.
The other triangle P_1,q,P_2 has no edge through O. If O is not
inside it, the correction has zero winding and the actual cycle
has winding one, already impossible. If O is inside it, write
P_i=q+b_i u_i with u_1=u,u_2=v. In the independent u,v basis
originated at q, O has coordinates (sigma,sigma), so b_1,b_2>0.
Both original root ports are proper.

Choose an own coordinate in which the closed H interval lies
strictly positively. Its two ports have positive coordinate and
triangle interiority forces q's coordinate negative. Hence the
same coordinate of M is positive and H avoids the entire nonpositive
M ray. The full degree-free actual-neighbor theorem
ROOTED_NONPOSITIVE_RAY_PROPER_PORT_CAP_EXCLUSION.md applies to this
negative concurrence and the two original proper ports. It puts
the two distinct actual neighbor centers at distance less than one.
This contradicts their disjoint open incircles.

For opposite parallel root vectors u=a e,v=-b e, a,b>0, (5) gives
both root port heights strictly negative. Equations (12) preserve
these signs at the endpoint nodes. Every node of the short positive
endpoint cone then has negative height, so the positive sum (11)
cannot be axial. This includes u+v=0.

For equal-sign root vectors u=a e,v=b e, the actual ports have
opposite strict heights. They are distinct points of one exposed
side, so e is a true side axis and

    H=[h_0-1,h_0]e+[b_1,b_1+1]Je, b_1<0<b_1+1.

Origin avoidance forces h_0>1 or h_0<0. In the first case both
actual neighbor centers have e-projection greater than 3/2 and norm

In the second case the actual private path advances positively
from a lower-left port to an upper-left port, so its advance is
greater than pi. Closing inside H through the negative axis has
positive advance as well. The actual cycle again has positive
winding, contradicting Section 1. Every actual side mean remains.

These are all root alternatives. They prove the private-leaf cycle
theorem for the entire original domain, without a canonical motion,
an assumed root ray, a root degree reduction, or a hole-capacity call.

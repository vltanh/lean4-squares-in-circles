Keep all nine actual interior-disjoint unit squares in

    79/40 <= R <= sqrt(1105)/16,
    d=sqrt(R^2-1/4)-1,       epsilon=max(d-1,0),

with the same compatible selected physical certificate and all original

genuine S/D choices, all original means and every physical circle

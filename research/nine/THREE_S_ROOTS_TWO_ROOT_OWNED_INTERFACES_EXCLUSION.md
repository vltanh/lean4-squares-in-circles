Suppose each actual A-to-H and C-to-H normal is owned by its respective
root A or C. The complete local S-star cover has exactly two ways
to meet this condition:

* a tilted root uses its actual far +u side axis;
* an aligned root has parallel actual triangle reactions, both along

The owned transverse axis in an aligned star is forbidden by the
physical triangle nonobtuse fact and its genuine leaf equation, and
an owned longitudinal axis forces the entire parallel pair. The
tilted feature cover leaves precisely the stated owned +u case.
These are the full local covers in Sections 2--5 of
TWO_S_TIP_TRIANGLES_HIGH_RADIUS_HUB_CLEARANCE.md, valid with the
complete arbitrary H external aggregate.

If H is parallel to either root, the separately reviewed complete
source THREE_S_ROOTS_PARALLEL_TRIANGLE_INTERFACE_EXCLUSION.md
excludes that original interface with the actual H leaf and third
root retained. We may therefore assume H is nonparallel to both.
Each actual owned-root normal then strictly exposes a vertex of H.

For a tilted root its actual port Q obeys

    |Q|^2<241/3600<(3/8)^2,

using the other genuine root's actual epsilon point in the local
small-port estimate. For an aligned parallel star its unchanged two
means have opposite transverse signs. The boundary tip's transverse
height exceeds 16/25, so the actual H height has absolute value less
than 9/25. Its actual longitudinal coordinate is epsilon. Hence

    |Q|^2<1/3600+(9/25)^2<(3/8)^2.

Both genuine H ports thus have norm below 3/8. Their distance is
strictly below 3/4<1, so they must be the same actual H vertex Q.

At either tilted root, its real owned-side parameter is sigma*b,
with 0<=sigma*b<d*b<1. The zero endpoint would be an owned root-axis
normal at a singleton vertex/vertex contact with the nonparallel H,
forbidden by the original compatible strict point-normal convention.
Thus Q is in the relative interior of its actual side.

At either aligned parallel root, if its actual mean were an endpoint
of the back side, the same singleton vertex/vertex endpoint rule
would forbid its owned axis. This rules out both real endpoints,
without replacing its actual mean. Its Q is likewise a side-interior
point.

The two actual roots therefore each occupy an open tangent half-plane
near the same Q. If these half-planes are not opposite they overlap.
If they are opposite, together they cover a neighborhood of Q except
for one line; H's inward right-angle vertex sector cannot stay on
that line and overlaps one of the two roots. Either alternative
contradicts the original pairwise disjoint open interiors.

This is a complete exclusion of all four combinations of aligned
parallel and tilted owned-root stars. It extends the previously
reviewed THREE_S_ROOTS_BOTH_TILTED_OWNED_INTERFACES_EXCLUSION.md

## 3. A geometric inequality for the entire remaining orientation interval

\[

\tag{11}
\]

\(c\ge\sqrt{1-(35/64)^2}>837/1000\). By (3),
\[

 \frac{321}{250}\frac{29}{64}+\frac{837}{2000}

 E=n\cdot f=su+cv>0,\qquad D=n\cdot g=sv-cu.

                 <N-1-s+\max(0,-D)+E.             \tag{12}

                E+\max(0,-D)-s<K-N.               \tag{13}

### 3.2. N at least 401/300

Put \(y=c/(1+s)\in(0,1)\). The half-angle identities and (10) give

\[

       <\frac{401}{300},

 E-D-s<\frac{99}{70}c-s=:w.

       \ge2-2B(y)w-w^2

\[
 P(y)=1-\frac{134}{35}y+\frac{169}{35}y^2

\tag{19}

\tag{20}

Therefore (18) is strictly positive. Since \(K,N+w>0\), it gives
\(K-N>w\), proving (13) in this full remaining interval.

## 1. The whole rooted-diamond pressure theorem

Let H,K,A,B have the five actual positive edges

    HK, HA, HB, KA, KB.

Require true degree three at K and true degree two at A and B.
H may have any additional positive neighbors and any actual degree.
There is no positive AB edge; physical zero-stress contacts are not
removed. Put

    S = F_A+F_K+F_B,
    F_T = 2 sum_(circle corners z of T) lambda_z z.

Then the actual block satisfies

    c_H dot S >= 0.                                    (1)

No origin-containment premise is imposed on H. No secondary minimizer
is required for this local pressure theorem. It does not say that each
individual degree-two tip pressure is nonnegative; the known LOW
negative-tip obstruction remains valid.

### 1.1. Load and mask cover independent of outside forces

A and B must have positive circle load: an unloaded true degree-two
body transmits opposite forces, separating its two neighbors by its
positive square width, whereas H and K actually touch. K must be
loaded by NO_UNLOADED_DIAMOND_HUB.md: its true three neighbors H,A,B
contain the two actual physical edges HA and HB. No equation at H
is used in either conclusion.

If H physically touched the circle, H,K,A would be a forbidden
physical boundary triangle. Therefore H is physically circle-clear
and has zero circle force. All three original block loads are positive.

The physical boundary path A--K--B has its original cone identity

    F_K = alpha*F_A + beta*F_B,       alpha,beta>0.       (2)

BOUNDARY_THREE_PATH_RESULTANT_CONE.md gives this without a degree-two
equation at K. Suppose, for contradiction, c_H dot S<0. Equations
(1)'s contrary sign and (2) select a genuinely negative endpoint.
The full radial-neighbor theorem makes every two-circle-corner
neighbor strictly positive at H, even at an individual zero weight.
Rename the actual negative physically unique endpoint A. Thus

    c_H dot z_A<0,

and the full original physical masks, in order (K,A,B), are precisely
SUS, UUS, SUU and UUU. Reflection exchanges A and B and leaves K
at true degree three.

### 1.2. The replacement for every old H5/petal pressure step

Let n,a,l be the original H-to-A, H-to-K and H-to-B normals,
with their unchanged positive magnitudes mu,eta,nu. Sum the real
A2/K3/B2 equations. Every internal AK and KB force cancels at its
actual application, giving

    S = mu*n + eta*a + nu*l.                         (3)

The same sum of origin torques is zero. In particular two incoming
forces parallel to n retain their separate applications in torque;
they may be added only in the vector identity (3).

The standing contrary hypothesis is exactly

    mu*(c_H dot n)+eta*(c_H dot a)+nu*(c_H dot l)<0.   (4)

In the old exact H5 component, (4) followed from the intact petal's
positive pressure and the full H5 balance. Here it follows directly
from the original three-body block sum. It supplies every pressure
sign in the old proof, including negative HB projection after the
other two terms have been shown nonnegative. A derived nonnegative
or positive left side now contradicts (4) directly.

No physical position or equation of the old X,Y is needed to make
this substitution. In contrast to the parallel ABC proof, the
diamond proof never uses an end-petal support-height bound to place
the root. The real HK contact places H and is retained throughout.

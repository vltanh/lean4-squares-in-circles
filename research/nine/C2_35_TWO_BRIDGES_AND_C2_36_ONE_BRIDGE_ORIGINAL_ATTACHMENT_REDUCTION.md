## 2. Uniform disk-avoiding block outside a closed-origin leaf root

Let Q be any actual cyclic biconnected positive block in a connected
positive component. Suppose every actual unit of Q avoids closed
D_epsilon. Let an actual E in that same component, outside Q, contain
O in its closed square and have a genuine positive leaf L.

**Uniform theorem.** This configuration is impossible.

Neither E nor any Q vertex is assigned an intact triangle or a bounded
positive degree. E need not contain O openly and need not contain the
epsilon disk. The disk-avoidance premise is on the whole actual Q.

### 2.1. No original Q edge has zero origin moment

An original zero-moment Q edge would be radial at its actual nonzero
application p=t e, t>0. Name its inner unit U and outer unit V so that
U lies in e.x<=t and V in e.x>=t. The full contained-neighbor bound
gives t<=d. Since U avoids O, one reflected own interval is [a,a+1]
with a>0. At its radial maximum-e point p the coordinate in this axis
is a+1, so t>1 and a<=t-1<=epsilon. If both own intervals missed
zero, both absolute coordinates of p would exceed one, forcing
t>sqrt(2)>d. Its other own interval therefore contains zero, and
the actual point (a,0) of U lies in D_epsilon. This contradicts
the stated avoidance.

This exhausts every actual owner and shared-side mean: zero moment
is precisely parallelism of its original application and normal.
The argument uses no force equation or degree bound at either body.

### 2.2. The full block moments force an enclosing physical cycle

Every connected component outside a maximal biconnected block attaches
to that block at at most one vertex. Sum its complete original torque
equations. Its internal pair moments cancel at the unchanged common
applications, and all circle moments vanish. Its total external moment
at that sole block vertex is zero. Applying this to every outside
component leaves a positive circulation of the full original edge
moments on Q, with no discarded outside force.

Orient each Q edge in its positive-moment direction. All its moment
weights are nonzero by Section 2.1. A finite nonempty positive
circulation has a directed simple cycle C, of length at least three.
The full degree-free port-order theorem in the second pinned source
gives strictly positive winding of this actual center/contact cycle
about O, since all its actual units avoid D_epsilon.

Use the one common compatible physical embedding. It realizes C as a
Jordan curve with that winding, choosing routing neighborhoods within
the strict O clearance of the actual C bodies. The compact segment
O--c_E avoids those bodies: except possibly O it lies in int E, and
O itself is outside every C body by the closed-disk premise. Thus
c_E is strictly inside the same Jordan curve.

The genuine leaf L has positive circle load, since otherwise its one
nonzero pair force could not balance. It is therefore a physical
boundary body. Neither E nor L belongs to C: E is outside Q and L
has true degree one. Their actual positive edge E--L is disjoint from
C in this same embedding. Consequently c_L is inside the cycle too,
contradicting L's protected exterior-face access.

This proves the uniform theorem, including the closed-origin boundary
case at E. No boundary path was invented and no contact was rerouted
independently of the common physical embedding.

## 3. Full block-attachment graph cover

Two distinct maximal cyclic blocks either share exactly one articulation
or are vertex-disjoint. They cannot share two vertices. When disjoint,
their unique connecting path in the block-cut tree consists entirely
of bridges. Any other bridge belongs to a pendant tree attached at a
single core vertex; two core attachments would create another cyclic
block or merge existing blocks.

For a triangle and an order-q block with b bridges, the complete
component count is

    1+(3-1)+(q-1)+b = q+b+2.

Thus both original cells considered here use all nine actual units.
No completion square is dropped, and there is no extra vertex on a
bridge unless that edge's place in the count supplies it.

At any triangle vertex its complete outside force system has zero
origin moment. This follows by summing the entire actual attached
components, just as in Section 2.2. A circle force at the vertex also
has zero moment. These are precisely the moment premises of the pinned
triangle theorems; no shortened H equation is used.

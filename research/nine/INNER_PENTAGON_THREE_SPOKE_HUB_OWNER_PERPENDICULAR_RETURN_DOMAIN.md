## 2. A strict coupled support inequality: B0<b

This short scalar fact holds over the whole domain (1). Put

    S0=a+b, L0=a-b, p0=L0/S0.

The nonempty strict p interval gives p0<b/a, hence
a^2-b^2<2ab. Squaring the positive sides gives 8a^2b^2>1. Therefore

    ab>1/(2sqrt(2))>8/25,
    0<L0<3/5.                                       (4)

The radical comparison in (4) is equivalent to 625>512. With a,b
fixed, differentiate the actual coupled expression for v0, retaining
H=b+ap and t=1-H. The exact numerator is

    dv0/dp = [1-a-b-t^2]/(1-pH)^2 <0.               (5)

At p=p0 the continued formula has H=1/S0 and

    v0(p0)=S0(S0-1)/(S0^2-L0).

The strict inequality v0(p0)<b/a is equivalent, after multiplying
positive denominators, to L0 S0^2<1. Since S0^2=2-L0^2, (4) gives

    L0 S0^2=L0(2-L0^2)<(3/5)(2-9/25)=123/125<1.   (6)

The cubic is increasing on [0,3/5], since its derivative is at least
23/25. Equations (5)--(6) prove v0<b/a at every actual p>p0. Applying
the increasing map x/sqrt(1+x^2) proves

                              B0<b.                (7)

No pose, side mean or force ratio has been relaxed independently in
this calculation. The strict margin in (6) is 2/125.

## 8. A complete local circle obstruction under four scalar bounds

    d>1/3, k<1/8, h0>3/5, g>d.                      (23)

    Z_C-O=R(d,k)/sqrt(d^2+k^2).

                       (xi+g,k-1-h0).

    (g-d)^2+(1+h0)^2
      +2R[(g-d)d-(1+h0)k]/sqrt(d^2+k^2).             (24)

    (1+h0)[1+h0-2R(k/d)]
       >(8/5)[8/5-(25/6)(3/8)] = 3/50>0.            (25)

The monotonicity of x(x-25/16) for x>=8/5 justifies the last weak
endpoint comparison explicitly. Thus the actual B point lies outside

    O=(1-sqrt(R^2-1/4), k-1/2).

The same actual B point Q+eta has displacement

    (sqrt(R^2-1/4)+g-d, -1/2-h0).

Both squared coordinates exceed the corresponding squared coordinates

both zero individual circle-weight endpoints and uses no radial

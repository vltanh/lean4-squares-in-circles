    O in int H.

Every other actual closed unit of the nine-square packing avoids O.
In particular vertex-disjoint T and Q are impossible, regardless of
the graph path between them or the complete forces at their roots.

This is an original-force statement about a true terminal four-block.
It is not asserted for a four-block with an externally decorated
private vertex, or for a triangular face whose complete external

## 3. Some actual unit contains O, so the blocks share H

They have disjoint vertex sets or share exactly one articulation.

bounded interiors or nested bounded interiors: the second curve

first. Since both interiors contain O, they must nest. Choose a
private actual body vertex on the inner cycle, different from their

    O in H, and every other actual closed unit avoids O.  (3)

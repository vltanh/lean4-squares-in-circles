2026. The review covered the 256-commit history, the main mathematical

## 1. What the old counts establish

The actual archived registers contain 224 CLOSED and seven OPEN entries in
the fixed 231-cell cover of `001/00`, and eight CLOSED / 24 OPEN entries in
the 32-mask cover. The seven originals are A-c744; B16a, B7c60, B462; and
C887, Cd402, Cb882. Finishing them would establish `001/00` and its reversal
`100/00`, leaving 22 masks. These are different accounting units from the
historical 7,915 positional roots.

The unrestricted lower bound, global equality and final manuscript remain
open. The older READMEs and registries drifted among 53/178, 168/63,
220/11, 222/9 and 224/7 current-mask checkpoints. Repeated handoffs also
retained superseded instructions. The cleanup replaces those competing
entry points with one [current plan](PROOF_PLAN.md); the dated evidence is
preserved at the [archive snapshot](ARCHIVE.md).

## 2. Why the previous approaches stalled

| Route | Useful result | Remaining difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Stress and contact graphs | Physical force/torque certificates; uniform unloaded-module bounds; finite topology inventories | Whole-network hypotheses, true degrees, generic nonidentity claims, higher-cut/chorded blocks and all-nine callers remain substantial |
| Common-frame and few-exception geometry | Sharp theorems for large special classes | Exact equality examples obstruct universal common-frame or at-most-two-exception normalizations |
| Rotating center-cone orders | Unrestricted completed \(4+3\) extraction and diagonal-event frames | Consecutive comparisons may be weak; the finite-poset input and global terminal caller need explicit treatment |
| Fixed masks and interval certificates | Complete computational closures of some full domains, many local inequalities | Large proof data, incomplete remaining masks, and sharp boundary cases; finite coverage alone gives no termination bound |

The stress route's 19-term translational certificate must not be combined
silently with the later 27-atom physical torque certificate or a separately
proved planar selection. Likewise, deleting cut edges cannot manufacture a
two-port hypothesis. The earlier direct-triangle obstruction was eventually
resolved computationally; stale graph notes should not be read as the final
status of that local theorem. Its resolution still did not close all global
callers. The old corpus remains useful research, but is not the active paper
proof's dependency tree.

## 3. A terminal index is not the arithmetic table

The archived `010/11` closure reports 56,001,065 new strict leaves. Essential
inputs include a 9,688,640-leaf height theorem and a 27,552,502-leaf side
exclusion. Its occupancy assembly is useful, but those inputs have not been
replaced by the short hand arguments the present task seeks.

B580 alone has 755 nodes, 537 terminals, 350 bound rounds and 66,464 rational
deductions. Its `COMPLETE_TERMINAL_TABLE.md` explicitly leaves coefficients
and multipliers in the bound node records; the printed columns identify
leaves and reported reserves. Portable replay uses 1,156 packet inputs plus
507 prior inputs. The Markdown table alone is therefore not self-contained
arithmetic.

This does not show that the finite certificates are false or impossible to
formalize. It shows that the current delivery depends on much more than the
displayed tables. Archive identity checks establish which bytes were read;
the mathematical proof also needs sound deductions, physical premises and
complete domain coverage. The new paper's table standard makes those
requirements explicit.

## 4. The strongest material to retain

The physical two-square cap has a complete scalar argument with a small
rational table. Two copies immediately give the weighted endpoint lemma
in [the draft](PROOF.md), which is less restrictive than actual \(4+3\)
chains. The three-square half-plane cap, center cap, outsider bounds,
relative-support inequalities and exact equality examples also remain useful.

The center-cone extraction has a real finite-poset dependency on Greene's
theorem. The source's partition calculation does not prove the cited
min–max theorem. The paper must state the exact cited input; a fully
self-contained version must also supply its proof.
Its unconditional diagonal-event argument includes persistent height four;
the later first-birth grid is conditional. At most 36 packing-dependent
event frames does not mean 36 fixed configurations.

Two exact obstructions should stay next to any proposed simplification:

- Every historically open mask has a selected-seven realization strictly
  below \(17/4<Q\). Thus the selected seven alone cannot exclude those
  domains. This does not prove that both extras are individually indispensable.
- Full-nine configurations exist at \(Q\) in `101/11` and all `111/de`
  masks. A closed-radius contradiction for those complete predicates would
  be false. Strict-radius reasoning must retain the equality boundary.

The corresponding tables and the common-frame/few-exception counterexamples
are retained in [sources](sources/README.md). They do not disprove the use
of a different endpoint choice or a different terminal direction.

## 5. Recommendation and limits

Develop the hand proof around the weighted endpoint inequality and the
three-square cap. Settle their dependency chain and the unrestricted
extraction, then prove one full all-nine geometric domain. The proposed
global routing statement is still unproved; the review supplies a better
specified research target, not a discovery algorithm or a promised short proof.

The cleanup retains mathematical sources, moves superseded machinery to
Git history and makes the remaining gap visible. It claims no new mask
closure and no new global equality classification.

Ordinary gate `OPEN` means that its full exit is not established. `DEFERRED` means intentionally out of scope; no implied proof.

- Every physical feature split must have a finite **proof of exhaustiveness**. A numerical pose sample, midpoint mesh, time limit, solver UNSAT without proof, incomplete interval B&B, or closed radius `q<=Q-ε` with fixed ε is NOT a proof of all `q<Q`.
- A **CLOSED local gate** does not close a coarse parent. It must be independently reviewed then joined to the literal full original caller; all other owner/load/mask/bridge cases and all `9-m` physical extra squares remain. Only then update the canonical 58-row source and the progress ledger.
- **Stop/pivot** explicitly if a discovered exact system has physically possible local roots or no terminating sign/capacity theorem; record the unresolved root as such. Instead of endless subgates, decide whether to test the full nine-square completion or an independent global direct exact method. Change the active gate and reason in PROGRESS before switching.
- **No false equality exclusion:** the explicit q=Q packing must pass the unrestricted model. The eventual proof is q>=Q; any claimed absence throughout `q<=Q` must establish a genuine feature that cannot occur at equality.

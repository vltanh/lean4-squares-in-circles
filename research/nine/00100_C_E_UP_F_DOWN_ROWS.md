The variable order, box conventions, row-family proofs and contraction rule are defined in that chapter. A row ID has scope only within its stated context; all coefficients and right sides below are literal rational numbers. Unlisted coefficients are zero.

The family label identifies the applicable proved rule. Multiple product-plane choices can have the same family label; the printed sparse coefficients specify the particular row without ambiguity. The numerical part of an ID merely distinguishes rows. No external row generator or executable is needed to interpret it.

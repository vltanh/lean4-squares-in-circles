## 5. The entire top-side KB alternative has positive HB pressure

This section is purely geometric and works for either physical B mask.
Assume k=g. If p=0, H has y<=H0<W while B has y>=W, so HB is already
impossible. We may assume p>0.

First let B have a tilted frame. Its bottom vertex Q0=(q_x,W) belongs
to K, so q_x>=X. Its two inward axes can be labeled

    e=(C,S1), vB=(-S1,C), C,S1>0, C^2+S1^2=1,
    B=Q0+[0,1]e+[0,1]vB.

The entire edge Q0+[0,1]e has x>=X and y>=W, so it misses H by (1).
For a point Q0+e+t vB on the other edge through Q0+e, membership in
H would require x<=X<=q_x, and hence t>=C/S1. But then

    y=W+S1+tC>=W+S1+C^2/S1=W+1/S1>=W+1,

which exceeds every H height by (1). This excludes that entire edge
as well as both of its endpoints. It also explicitly excludes the
opposite B corner, without relying only on a list of corner locations.

Any remaining actual HB contact is therefore on Q0+[0,1]vB, at
Q0+vB, or on Q0+vB+[0,1]e. At all these features the incoming B
support normal ell has strictly positive f component: its cone is
spanned by e=(C,S1) and -vB=(S1,-C). If ell_g<=0, then

    ell.n=E ell_f-p ell_g>0.

Every maximum-ell support point of H must consequently be U or a point
of U+[-1,0]j, all with y<=H0<W. None belongs to B. Thus ell_g>0.
Together with ell_f>0 and cf,cg>0, this gives c_H.ell>0, contradicting
(5). This includes a free normal at the allowed B vertex.

If B is aligned, it has the form [b0,b0+1] x [W,W+1]. Physical KB
implies b0+1>=X. The top edge is above every H point. The right edge
has x>=X, and if equality occurs the H points at that x lie below W.
Hence HB can use only the bottom or left edge, or their common lower
left vertex. Their incoming support normals have nonnegative f and g
components, at least one strictly positive. Again c_H.ell>0 by (4),
contradicting (5). Every aligned side mean is included in this argument.

Thus the whole k=g alternative is excluded.

remaining actual direction is

    ell=Jk=(-k_g,k_f),                                  (9)

whose two components are strictly negative.

## 7. The two actual support points have incompatible heights

    B=I+[0,1]k+[0,1]ell,

    P_g-Q_g=sigma k_g-theta ell_g>=0.

    H0+p-E<=H0<W,

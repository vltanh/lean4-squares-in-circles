## 7. The complete upper normal cover leaves only top and strict far

-T<=eta<=G. Equation (29) makes eta=(q_C)_y>0. By the end of
Section 5, that point lies in the relative interior of B's left side.

### 7.2. A strict northeast normal

Such a normal exposes R_0=(delta,G). This lies in the relative
interior of B's left side, strictly between its actual lower and upper
heights. Near R_0, B occupies the right half-plane and H the southwest
quadrant. A further square containing R_0 must have its tangent sector
in the remaining northwest quadrant. A unit-square sector has angle
at least pi/2, so it must be exactly that quadrant, with inward axes
-E_1,E_2. The point R_0 then cannot minimize a strict northeast
linear functional on that square: its inward -E_1 axis has negative
projection. This contradicts the actual H-to-C support. It does not
exclude the separate top E_2 contact.

### 7.3. The left side

\[
          (c_C)_x\le-X-1/2<-89/60,\qquad |c_C|<91/60.

\[

than 2pi/9 between two distinct physical boundary-unit centers.
This is the full centered-inner-arc theorem, not a comparison of
individual selected circle atoms. Both physical C masks and every

The only remaining directions are E_2 and strict northwest. They

Therefore such an enclosed actual unit is impossible. Any additional

### 2.2. Every nonparallel root pair has the exact negative-cone form

    -k=c t_Q/L, -k=e t_P/L.                            (5)

    Q_0=-t(u+v)=-t(F_P+F_Q), t>0.                      (6)

# No-long small cycles require an occupied side pocket

Date: October 30, 2026 UTC.

**Status: complete proposed whole-family corollary, awaiting independent
full review. It excludes the entire six-load diagonal seven-cycle
completion family, but leaves the side-pocket completion families.**

The new all-center diameter theorem makes the whole central free union
have capacity one for every short cycle with isolated transmitters.
The already accepted diagonal-channel theorem makes every diagonal
exterior pocket empty. Together they force an actual occupied side
pocket in any nine-unit completion, uniformly over all cycle lengths
from three through seven and all placements of the isolated transmitters.

## 1. Precise original domain

Keep nine actual closed unit squares, with pairwise disjoint open
interiors, contained in the same centered disk of radius R satisfying

    R^2<=Q=1105/256.

Keep one original compatible physical certificate, including all force
and moment equations, original normals, actual contact means and owned
side intervals, physical circle masks and individual weights, the
compatible diagonal convention, the selected physical plane embedding,
and its simultaneous circle spokes.

Suppose an entire positive component is a simple cycle on n actual
bodies, where

    3<=n<=7,

with at least three positive total circle loads. Assume no two
unloaded vertices are adjacent on this same original cycle. Thus every
nonempty channel between positive loads has exactly one original
unloaded full-degree-two transmitter. Its actual width is one or
sqrt(2), from its unchanged force and moment equations.

The remaining 9-n actual units are retained, regardless of their own
circle contacts, geometric contacts or stress status. The words
"side pocket" below mean the actual exterior region of an original
width-one channel, with all its physical subdivisions. They do not
mean a hypothetical cap chosen after moving a unit.

**Theorem.** At least one actual additional unit lies wholly in an
actual side-normal singleton-transmitter exterior pocket of the
original cycle.

**Consequently**, a nine-unit completion is impossible if all its
nonempty channels are diagonal, or if it has no nonempty channels.
The whole six-load seven-cycle with a diagonal singleton is therefore
excluded, including all physical endpoint masks, original mean values
and possible poses of its two completion units.

## 2. The required radius window belongs to the same nine squares

The complete accepted geometric theorem
NINE_RADIUS_TWO_MEASURE_LOWER_BOUND.md applies to these same nine
actual unit squares and proves

    R>2.

Its statement has no force graph, contact, load or orientation premise.
Thus the actual nine-unit caller satisfies both the radius window of
the isolated-transmitter central theorem and the lower bound R>=2 of
the whole diagonal-pocket theorem.

This is not a claim that an isolated n-body core must have radius above
two. No completion unit is discarded before using the nine-unit bound.
Its existing analytic measure assembly and inherited fixed arithmetic
remain accepted dependencies; this corollary introduces no replacement
measure or new computational certificate.

## 4. Complete exterior routing and exclusion of diagonal pockets

The actual region cover in SIMPLE_CYCLE_CHANNEL_POCKET_CAPS.md,

The same complete cover checks both potentially omitted exterior

radial endpoint cannot hold a unit. A direct loaded-to-loaded contact
has an empty exterior pair pocket. Extra physical contacts, including
zero-weight circle contacts at a transmitter, only subdivide their
original regions. They cannot let an extra cross an occupied barrier.

Every remaining exterior region here belongs to a singleton channel,
because the original unloaded vertices are isolated.

For clarity, I read its entire complete source before making this

The actual noncentral extra from Section 3 can therefore be in neither

## 5. Entire six-load diagonal seven-cycle and simultaneous consequences

If n=7 and six vertices have positive total circle load, the remaining
vertex U is the only unloaded one. Its two positive neighbors are
loaded, its full true degree is two, and all other channels are direct.
If U's actual width is sqrt(2), the unique nonempty exterior pocket
is diagonal and is empty by Section 4. All direct pockets are empty,
and the whole central union has capacity at most one. Its two actual
completion units cannot fit. This excludes the entire six-load
diagonal family at once.

No restriction on U's possible physical zero-weight circle contact,
on either endpoint's physical circle mask, on the original diagonal
side means, or on the locations of the extras was added. The sole
remaining six-load width possibility is the original side-normal
singleton, and its actual side pocket must contain an extra.

The same common theorem simultaneously excludes every all-direct or
all-diagonal no-long C3--C7 component in the nine-unit packing. It
does not rely on a separate placement-by-placement check. The
at-least-three-load assumption remains explicit; smaller two-load
cycle domains retain their separate existing exits.

For the remaining no-long seven-cycles one can also import the frozen
weighted occupied-arc budget, 2m+d<=15. At m=7 it gives d<=1,
where d is the number of actual diagonal singleton transmitters.
Let k be the number of positive loads and s the number of side
singletons. The entire current necessary cover is then:

| Positive loads k | Number of singleton transmitters | Still possible actual widths | Necessary completion location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 | Three side, or two side and one diagonal | At least one actual side pocket is occupied; central capacity is at most one. |
| 5 | 2 | Two side, or one side and one diagonal | At least one actual side pocket is occupied; central capacity is at most one. |
| 6 | 1 | Side only | That actual side pocket is occupied; central capacity is at most one. |
| 7 | 0 | None | Nine-unit completion impossible. |

Three or fewer positive loads cannot have four or more isolated
unloaded vertices on a seven-cycle: between any two unloaded vertices
there must be a distinct loaded vertex. Thus the displayed table is
complete for the no-long seven-cycle domain with at least three loads.
The weighted theorem is used only for the listed diagonal counts;
Sections 1--4 do not need it.

The table does not assert that a central extra and an occupied side
pocket cannot coexist. It does not make any joint-capacity claim for
different side pockets. The original side-pocket occupancies, true
means, completing squares and full graph degrees remain to be handled
in those cases.

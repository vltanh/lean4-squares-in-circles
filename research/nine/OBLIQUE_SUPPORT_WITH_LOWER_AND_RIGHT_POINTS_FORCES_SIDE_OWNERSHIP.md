## 1. The complete one-unit geometric statement

and let Q be an actual minimum-N support point of K, where the
unchanged inward unit normal is strict Northwest:

    N=(-c,d), c,d>0, c^2+d^2=1.                        (1)

Assume the same actual K contains points R,S with

    R_y<Q_y, S_x>Q_x.                                 (2)

The points R,S may be any actual contact points or means and need
not be distinct from each other. Their actual positions remain.

**Theorem 1.** Q lies in the relative interior of an actual K side,
K genuinely owns its original normal N, and for a unique actual

    K=Q+[0,1](-c,d)+[-sigma,1-sigma](-d,-c).            (3)

Moreover its entire support rectangle satisfies the strict bound

    (max_K x-Q_x)+(Q_y-min_K y)<1.                     (4)

This assertion retains all frames, both original contact means and
every normal-cone endpoint. The strict assumptions are exactly those
printed in (1)--(2); an axis direction is not silently included.

## 2. Every actual vertex feature is impossible

If Q were a K vertex, write its actual inward orthonormal axes as
a,b so that K=Q+[0,1]a+[0,1]b. The minimum-support hypothesis gives
N.a>=0 and N.b>=0, including equality at a normal-cone endpoint.

The actual lower point R in (2) forces at least one of these axes
to have strictly negative y component. Call it a. Since c,d>0,

    -c*a_x+d*a_y>=0, a_y<0

forces a_x<0. Thus a is strict Southwest. The two perpendicular
choices for b are strict Northwest and strict Southeast. The
Southeast alternative has strictly negative dot product with N
and is impossible. The remaining Northwest axis also has strictly
negative x component. Both actual inward axes therefore point
strictly left, so the whole K lies in x<=Q_x.

This contradicts the actual right point S in (2). The argument
has covered every vertex normal cone, including its axis endpoints
in the K frame. In particular it also covers every side endpoint.
No tangent axis has been chosen to suit the desired result.

An interior point of K cannot be a minimum support point for the
nonzero N. The only remaining actual feature is a relative-interior
side point. Its unique inward normal is precisely N. This gives
the entire pose (3), with the original parameter 0<sigma<1.

## 3. The whole-unit support inequality

The actual extreme coordinates in (3) are

    max_K x=Q_x+sigma*d,
    min_K y=Q_y-(1-sigma)*c.

Thus the left side of (4) is the exact convex combination

    sigma*d+(1-sigma)*c<1,

because 0<c,d<1. This proves (4), retaining the entire actual
unit and its original parameter. In particular, for any real
points R',S' in K,

    (S'_x-Q_x)+(Q_y-R'_y)<1.                          (5)

Every whole reflection or quarter-turn of (1)--(5) is valid in the
correspondingly reflected actual chart. These are rigid changes of

## 3. Neighbor sparsity and loading exports

The historical Corollary 6.1 states that the three physical
neighbors of an unloaded positive degree-three body induce
at most one physical pair edge.

The new theorem proves this for every original full
equilibrium. If two pair edges existed, they would share
one of the three neighbors, furnishing precisely the
diamond notation above. One edge supplies the touching
return pair and the second contradicts its strict third
support separation.

Consequently the equivalent loading rule is also preserved:

> A body whose true positive degree is exactly three and
> whose three actual neighbors contain at least two physical
> contact edges must have strictly positive circle load.

Here the circle coefficients are nonnegative in the original
certificate. If their total were zero, the entire pair star
would be the unloaded full star just excluded. No physical
circle contact is inferred merely from zero load; positive
load gives a physical circle point as usual.

All uses must still verify the true degree. A fourth positive
neighbor or a positive circle force prevents applying the
unloaded-star contradiction with one term omitted. The new
proof does not weaken that requirement.

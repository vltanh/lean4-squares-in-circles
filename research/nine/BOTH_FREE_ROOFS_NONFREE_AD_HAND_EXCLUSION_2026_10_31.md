## 3. Every physically shared AD is impossible

own side axis of both A and D. Its line is

    g.x=a,

with A on g.x<=a and D on g.x>=a. The pure physical triangle
side-normal obstruction gives

    h.g>=0, k.(-g)>=0.

Neither equality is possible: equality would make the corresponding
free roof normal perpendicular to an own hub axis and hence itself

    h.g>0, k.g<0.                                      (1)

and the second places Q at the minimum-g vertex support of D. Thus
P,Q both lie on the same shared supporting line g.x=a.

Since P-Q=(0,1) and g.(P-Q)=0, the unit normal g is East or West.
But h and k both
have strictly positive horizontal components, so h.g and k.g have
the same sign. This contradicts (1).

The shared-contact argument is entirely physical. It uses no original
AD mean, and therefore covers every endpoint-valued or interior mean

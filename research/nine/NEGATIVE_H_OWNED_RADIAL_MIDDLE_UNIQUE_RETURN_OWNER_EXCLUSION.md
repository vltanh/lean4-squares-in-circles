This excludes both signs of g_j and both signs of g_n wherever physically available. A zero axis component is an owned BC interface and belongs to Section6.1; BC cannot be B-owned because p,q>0, and the actual B theorem already made it a singleton. Thus no physical BC feature is omitted.

## 2. A complete normal-channel moment lemma

Let A and B be two distinct genuine loaded degree-two units. Suppose

    A-U_1-...-U_m-B, m>=1,

where every U_i is genuinely unloaded and has exactly its two displayed

magnitude gamma, the same directed normal g from A toward B, and the
unchanged endpoint applications P in A and Q in B with

    Q-P=L*g, L=sum_i w_i>0, w_i>=1.                        (6)

Here each w_i is the actual projected width of the real U_i along g.
This is the full original normal-channel identity. All shared means
retain their actual values. No direct physical A-B edge is asserted.

Assume the two nonzero actual resultants F_A,F_B are not parallel.
Then

    sign det(P,g)=sign det(F_A,F_B).                       (7)

In particular det(P,g) is nonzero. This conclusion includes every
finite/parallel form at A and B.

To prove it, use Section1 at both true loaded2 bodies.

If both have finite form, their actual rays are

    q_A=t_A F_A=P-alpha*g,
    q_B=t_B F_B=Q+beta*g,
    t_A,t_B>0, alpha,beta>=0.

Thus, with D=L+alpha+beta>0,

    q_B-q_A=D*g,
    t_A*t_B*det(F_A,F_B)=D*det(P,g).                       (8)

This is an exact equality of original lines and points; it includes
either zero ray, a coincident pair of applications at either endpoint,
and exterior two-circle concurrences.

If A has parallel form and B finite form, F_A=-c_A*g, c_A>0, and
q_B=Q+beta*g. Consequently

    t_B*det(F_A,F_B)=c_A*det(P,g).                         (9)

If A is finite and B parallel, the latter's actual outward normal
toward the channel is -g, so F_B=c_B*g, c_B>0, and

    t_A*det(F_A,F_B)=c_B*det(P,g).                         (10)

If both are parallel, F_A=-c_A*g and F_B=c_B*g are opposite. This
contradicts the stated nonparallel resultant premise. Equations8--10
therefore exhaust the entire domain and prove (7). No finite concurrence
is manufactured for a parallel force pair.

The same proof also covers m=0 when an actual direct contact is present
and both bodies have true loaded degree two. In that case L=0 and the
finite/finite coefficient alpha+beta cannot vanish: otherwise q_A=q_B,
contradicting the two nonparallel resultants. This optional direct-contact
extension is not needed for the original L0135 caller below.

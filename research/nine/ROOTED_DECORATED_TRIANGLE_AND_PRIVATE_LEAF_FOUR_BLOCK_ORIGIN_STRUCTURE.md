degree four. K keeps its full arbitrary outside system and original
circle load. The blocks either have disjoint vertex sets or share
their possible articulation H=K. The two genuine leaves are distinct
actual units and are outside both blocks.

**Theorem.** The blocks must share H=K, and

    O belongs to int H;
    every other actual closed unit avoids O.                 (1)

In particular the entire disjoint layout is impossible. Both genuine
leaves are S, with

    d=sqrt(R^2-1/4)-1=1+epsilon,
    0<epsilon<1/60,                                         (2)

and their distinct actual roots A,X are physically clear and meet
the closed epsilon-disk. H is physically clear as a consequence of
(1), not as an omitted initial circle-load assumption.

No genuine terminal four-block is assumed anywhere in this theorem.
In particular the force X-L_X is never discarded to make Q terminal.

## 6. Disjoint roots are impossible and the shared root contains O closed

First suppose T and Q are disjoint. All Q bodies avoid O by (3).
Equation (5) also excludes closed origin containment in H or A.
B and both genuine leaves are physical boundary units and already
avoid O. Equation (3) covers every remaining actual unit. Hence
no actual unit contains O.

In the shared case, (5) excludes O from the closed A. The private

Consequently O belongs to closed H, and no other actual unit

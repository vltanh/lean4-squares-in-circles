## 2. The original full H4 forces one strict B slope ordering

Write the unchanged actual H vectors as

    F_HA=(-U,-V), F_HB=(x,-y),
    F_HC=(r,s), F_HD=(0,gamma),
    U,V,x,y,r,s>0.

The actual four applications are (0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(p,1). Thus the
full force and torque equations give exactly

    U=x+r, V=r+q*gamma,
    y=s-r+p*gamma.                                  (2)

The equality q*X=eta+gamma*b implies

    q*(a+b)-1=gamma*b^2/X>0,
    q>1/(a+b)>=1/sqrt(2)>1/2,
    q>p.                                            (3)

Furthermore

    eta+gamma*b-Y_C
      =eta*(1+a-b)+gamma*b*(1-b)>0.

Since q<1, it follows that

    0<Y_C<eta+gamma*b=q*X<X.

The original HC force is parallel to (X,Y_C), so r>s. Equation
(2) consequently gives

    0<y<p*gamma.                                    (4)

We will show from the complete real A3 that u>v. Since its actual
HA force is a positive multiple of (-u,-v), this will imply U>V
and, by (2)--(4),

    x>q*gamma>p*gamma>y.                            (5)

No B, C or E equation was used to obtain these consequences of H4.

## 3. Complete actual AB feature cover at the unloaded A3

Let Gamma>0 be the original AH magnitude, so the actual A-to-H
vector is -Gamma*n at its unchanged point (0,0). The physical
triangle A-H-B and this A-owned axis imply that the original AB
normal has n component at most zero.

Its j component cannot be negative. Such a normal would support A
on its side with tangent coordinate -kappa, where every actual point
has world x coordinate -s_A*u-kappa*v<0, 0<=s_A<=1. This contradicts
the actual AB contact with the whole B in x>0.

There are exactly two remaining alternatives.

### 3.1. The parallel AB alternative

If its j component is zero, its unit direction is -n. Write its
original magnitude as beta>0 and its actual application as t_B*j
on the n=0 side. Since that point belongs to B,

    t_B*v>0, hence t_B>0.

The complete original A3 force then makes AE equal to
(Gamma+beta)*n. Its actual application is on the opposite side,
at n+t_E*j with the full original t_E interval retained. Torque
about the actual AH point in the frame (n,j) gives

    beta*t_B-(Gamma+beta)*t_E=0,
    t_E=beta*t_B/(Gamma+beta)>0.

The actual AE point therefore has world height -v-u*t_E<0.
This contradicts the whole-unit statement E subset {y>0}.
Thus every parallel AB application and shared-mean endpoint is
excluded without deleting either of the two parallel forces.

### 3.2. The complete positive-j alternative

The original AB vector has the form

    -rho*n+sigma*j, rho>=0, sigma>0.

It supports A on its actual positive-j side. If rho>0, the contact
is its n=0 endpoint; if rho=0, the entire side mean remains. In
both cases write its unchanged application as

    Z=s_A*n+(1-kappa)*j, 0<=s_A<=1.                 (6)

The complete A3 force now gives

    F_AE=(Gamma+rho)*n-sigma*j.

Both components are strict. Thus its original A support is exactly
the opposite vertex n-kappa*j, whose world height is kappa*u-v.
Because that actual point belongs to E,

    kappa*u-v>0, hence u>v.                        (7)

This proves the assertion needed for the H4 inequality (5). No
three-force equation was assigned to a loaded body: A is the
original unloaded end of true degree three.

## 4. The same actual AB support forces the opposite B slope

From (7), kappa>v/u. Since u<1 and u^2+v^2=1,

    (1-kappa)*(u+v)
      <(1-v/u)*(u+v)
       =2*u-1/u<1.                                (8)

For the last inequality, 2*u^2-u-1=(2*u+1)*(u-1)<0.

The actual point Z in (6) has world coordinates

    Z_x=-s_A*u+(1-kappa)*v,
    Z_y=-s_A*v-(1-kappa)*u.

In particular 1-Z_x>0 and -Z_y>0. Equations (7)--(8) give the
strict comparison over the entire original side interval:

    (1-Z_x)-(-Z_y)
      =1+s_A*(u-v)-(1-kappa)*(u+v)>0.              (9)

The actual unit B is supported at its original H point (1,0) with
the original vector (x,-y). Since Z belongs to this same actual B,

    x*(Z_x-1)-y*Z_y>=0,
    x*(1-Z_x)<=y*(-Z_y).

By positivity and (9), this forces x<y. It contradicts (5).

This covers both the strict-vertex AB alternative and its entire
owned-side mean alternative. No point or normal of B is replaced.
Sections 3.1--3.2 exhausted every possible original AB feature, so
the declared full interface is excluded.

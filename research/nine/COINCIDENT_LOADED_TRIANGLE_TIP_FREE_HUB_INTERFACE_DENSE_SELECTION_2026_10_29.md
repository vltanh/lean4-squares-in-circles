## 4. Two physical degeneracies are impossible without selection

First, eA and eD cannot be parallel in a surviving positive
feature. Identical inward diagonal directions would put their

contradict disjoint open interiors. Thus parallel diagonals
would require eD=-eA.

Adding both complete hub equations and the original V equation

    b*eA+c*eD+k*eV=0.                              (4)

If eD=-eA, equation (4) would make eV parallel to eA. But
each direction on the eA line lies strictly inside one of the

inside V's tangent quadrant. That is impossible for three

    delta=det(eA,eD)!=0.                           (5)

Second, nA and nD cannot be parallel in a positive feature.
They are both inward support normals of the actual V at P,

contradiction. Thus

    Delta=det(nA,nD)!=0.                           (6)

shared AV/DV mean endpoints.

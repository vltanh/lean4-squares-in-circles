## 1. Complete unchanged physical statement

Let U,V,W,T be distinct actual contained closed unit squares with pairwise disjoint open interiors, about the same circle origin.

Retain the following original physical and positive-force data:

1. U is unloaded and its entire positive pair neighborhood is V,W,T. All three original magnitudes are positive.
2. V is loaded and its entire positive pair neighborhood is U,W.
3. W and T physically touch the containing circle. Their true degrees, circle coefficients, other forces, and all other packing squares are unrestricted.
4. The original UV contact is a singleton at a vertex P of each square. Its unchanged normal is strict in both actual vertex cones.
5. V has a unique physical circle corner, opposite P.
6. The actual VW intersection has positive length. Its original aggregate mean is arbitrary on the whole closed shared segment.

**Theorem.** These original data are impossible.

The conclusion covers every frame, every original VW mean including either endpoint, every initial physical W/T circle mask, and every other original force. It has no prescribed hub frame, no U-axis premise, no artificial leaf, and no completion-pocket hypothesis.

The contradiction proves a little more quantitatively: the full original equations force the actual UT application P_T to have

    |P_T|<R-sqrt(2).

Since P_T belongs to the physical-boundary unit T, its actual circle corner and the unit diameter give the reverse weak inequality.

## 2. Exhausting the four actual shared-return directions

The actual positive-length VW intersection makes W parallel to V. Its shared side is one of the four sides of V. The two sides opposite P cannot occur because U would then miss W. Here is the full unit-reach argument.

At a U vertex with an outward normal having two strictly positive coordinate components, both actual inward axes of U have strictly negative scalar product with that normal. At most one such axis has positive first coordinate: two perpendicular vectors with positive first coordinates would put one strictly in the first quadrant. Any positive first-coordinate axis has first coordinate strictly less than one, since the unit East vector has positive dot product with n and cannot point into U. Thus

    max_(x in U) x_1 < P_1+1=a.

The same argument gives max_U x_2<P_2+1=b. Hence U cannot meet a square wholly beyond V's right side x=a or top side y=b.

The only remaining shared sides are V's left side or its bottom side. Exchange the two global own coordinates if needed so that it is the bottom side. This is a rigid reflection of every actual unit and original equation, not a new selection of a force.

## 4. The actual U/W contact fixes the entire hub frame

For detail, the axis with positive first coordinate must have negative second coordinate, because a nonnegative second coordinate has positive dot product with n. Its perpendicular inward axis must have negative first and second coordinates; the alternative perpendicular direction has both positive components and is forbidden. A zero component would either violate strictness or leave U wholly to the left of P, missing W. This also excludes the parallel U frame directly.

The unique rightmost U vertex is P+u. Its height b-1-s lies strictly between b-2 and b-1. Its first coordinate is a-1+c<a<l+1. If it were strictly greater than l, it would be in the open interior of W, contrary to the disjoint interiors. If it were less than l, the whole U would miss W. Therefore

    l=a-1+c,

The actual UW point is in the relative interior of W's left side. The original U-to-W normal is necessarily East. Its unchanged positive magnitude will be denoted nu; it need not equal the original VW magnitude nu_V.

## 5. The full U3 force and torque force its actual third side and mean

Thus the third support is the entire side with outward normal v:

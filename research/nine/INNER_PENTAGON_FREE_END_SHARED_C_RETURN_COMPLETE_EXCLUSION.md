# A free unloaded pentagon end cannot have a shared C return beside four boundary units

Date: October 28, 2026 UTC. Author: pentagon_uniform continuation.

**Status: new complete proposed original-feature theorem, frozen for
separate independent and root review.** It excludes the full free-HD,
positive-length CD interface whenever A,B,C,E physically touch the
circle. Every original shared mean, the coincident lower endpoint, all
E frames and all physical circle masks are retained. No whole original
fine or coarse family is declared closed.

## 1. Original scope and the whole shared-return normal form

Retain the no-chord H4_D0 graph

    HA, HB, HC, HD, AB, BC, CD, DE, EA,

all nine actual contained units, and the original circle window

    79/40<=R<=sqrt(1105)/16.                              (1)

H and D keep their complete unloaded degrees four and three; C and E
keep their complete loaded degrees three and two. Assume A,B,C,E are
physically boundary. This covers H4_D0.L0124 and the physical-boundary-A
mask of H4_D0.L124, with A's zero total coefficient in the latter
retained. Every original equation, physical contact, mask and completion
unit remains.

Assume the original HD contact is free under the strict point-normal
convention, and CD has a positive-length actual shared side. A free
HD has one actual vertex of each unit, and its original common normal
strictly exposes both. Normalize the actual D frame so

    D=[0,1]^2,
    P_H=(0,0), D->H=(-a,-b), a,b>0.                     (2)

The physical D-H-C triangle and D-owned CD axis make their normals'
dot product nonnegative. Thus the CD shared side is either West or
South. Reflect across the diagonal of D if necessary to take West.
Then, with the full original positive magnitude mu,

    D->C=(-mu,0), mu>0,
    C=[-1,0] times [r,r+1], -1<r<1,
    P_C=(0,s), max(0,r)<=s<=min(1,r+1).                 (3)

This is the complete actual positive-length CD interval. The original
mean s and both its closed endpoints remain.

The full original D force equation makes its E vector

    D->E=(a+mu,b),
    P_E=(1,1).                                          (4)

The point in (4) is forced by the strict northeast D normal. No E
ownership follows or is assumed. The complete D torque about (0,0)
is mu*s+b-a-mu=0. Hence, writing n_E=(p,q) for its actual unit
normal into E,

    b=a+mu*(1-s),
    p,q>0, p^2+q^2=1,
    q/p=b/(a+mu)<=1.                                   (5)

Only s>=0 is needed for the last inequality. This includes s=0,
where q=p, and s=1, where b=a. No optional generic-normal avoidance
or mean separation is used at either endpoint.

## 2. The entire C slider is above the free HD vertex

We prove

    r>=0.                                               (6)

If -1<r<0, then P_H=(0,0) lies in the relative interior of C's
right side. Its interior tangent sector is the whole left half-plane,
of angle pi. D's tangent sector at this same point is the northeast
quadrant, of angle pi/2. The tangent sector of the actual H vertex,
of angle pi/2, would have to fit into the sole remaining southeast
quadrant. Equality of these angles forces H's inward axes to be East
and South there.

But (2) is an actual minimum support of H in the southwest direction
(-a,-b). The East inward axis would have strictly negative dot product
with this minimum-support normal, which is impossible. Thus r<0
cannot occur. The endpoints r=-1 and r=1 are not positive-length CD
contacts and were never in (3). The actual endpoint r=0 remains.

Consequently C's entire top right corner

    T_C=(0,r+1)

has positive local height. This is an actual point of C; it is not a
replacement for the original CD mean P_C.

## 3. The full actual HC normal has strictly positive vertical component

Let N be the original unit normal from H into C, at its unchanged
actual point or full shared-side mean. The actual supporting
half-planes, applied to H's point P_H=(0,0) and C's point T_C, give

    N.P_H<=N.S<=N.T_C,
    (r+1)*N_y>=0.

Since r>=0, this implies N_y>=0. It is strict. If N=East, the common
line would equate max_H x with min_C x=-1, whereas H contains (0,0).
This is impossible.

If N=West, the common line would give min_H x=max_C x=0. Thus both
actual inward axes v,w at the H vertex (0,0) would have nonnegative
x components. Its minimum-southwest support (2) requires

    a*v_x+b*v_y<=0, a*w_x+b*w_y<=0.

Since a,b>0 and both axes are nonzero, each y component would be
strictly negative: a nonnegative y component together with a
nonnegative x component cannot satisfy its displayed inequality.
Then v.w=v_x*w_x+v_y*w_y>0, contradicting their orthogonality.
Both horizontal possibilities are excluded, so

    N_y>0.                                               (7)

This sign retains every HC owner and original mean, including contacts
at the common vertex when r=s=0. It uses no shortened H4 or C3 equation.

## 4. Actual occupied-rim order, including the coincident s=0 endpoint

If s>0, the D-center ray to P_C is strictly before the southwest ray
to P_H when traversing the left half-plane counterclockwise. The
P_E ray points northeast. Thus the H spoke lies in the counterclockwise
sector from the C rim ray to the E rim ray. Because H lies in the
occupied rim, its positive counterclockwise orientation is

    D -> C -> B -> A -> E -> D.                           (8)

The endpoint s=0 is also included. Equations (3), (6) force r=0.
At the coincident point (0,0), C and D occupy the northwest and
northeast quadrants and together fill the upper half-plane. The actual
H tangent sector therefore lies in the lower half-plane. Its whole
unit is on that side because its two inward axes generate the unit.
Its center is strictly below the common point, while the C and D
centers are above it on the left and right.

Accordingly the actual C--D strand, traversed from D to C through
their lower shared endpoint, has the H sector on its left, namely
the lower side. The compatible local three-sector routing preserves
this order: its C and D strands enter from the upper left and upper
right, and its H strand enters from below. This is exactly the weak
limit of the sector in the preceding paragraph. The occupied-rim
orientation remains (8). No distinct-application premise is imposed
on this s=0 endpoint and neither original mean is moved.

The occupied-rim simultaneous physical circle accesses give boundary
order C,B,A,E. The actual corner-to-center deviations less than pi/12
and boundary center separations greater than pi/5 transfer it to the
same center order. The angular-neighbor rule makes CB,BA,AE their
counterclockwise minor traversals. Thus the actual center advance
along C-B-A-E satisfies

    3*pi/5<Delta<6*pi/5, Delta<2*pi.                    (9)

The complete boundary-contact moment theorem gives the original signs

    det(P_CB-O,g_CB)>0,
    det(P_EA-O,g_EA)<0.                                 (10)

All full physical contact means and A's zero-load physical-boundary
mask remain. No positive load at A is used in this order argument.

## 5. Original-circle support and height bounds

Write the original circle center in local D coordinates as O=(xi,zeta),
and set d_R=sqrt(R^2-1/4)-1<61/60. The universal center and halfwidth
bounds give every actual minimum support height at most d_R.

C's West support has height xi, so

    xi<=d_R.                                             (11)

E's actual minimum-n_E support has height

    a_E=p*(1-xi)+q*(1-zeta)<=d_R.

Its genuine E2 origin torque and (10) give

    det(P_E-O,n_E)=q*(1-xi)-p*(1-zeta)<0,
    1-zeta>(q/p)*(1-xi).                                (12)

Consequently a_E>(1-xi)/p, and hence

    xi>1-p*d_R>=1-d_R>-1/60>-1/2.                      (13)

We also have the strict original-origin bound

    zeta<1.                                              (14)

If xi<=1, (12) proves it directly. Otherwise suppose xi>1 and
zeta>=1. From (11), (12), and q/p<=1 in (5),

    0<xi-1<=d_R-1<1/60,
    0<=zeta-1<(q/p)*(xi-1)<=xi-1<1/60.

The actual boundary point P_E=(1,1) would then have norm less than
sqrt(2)/60 about O. But every actual point of a physical boundary
unit has norm at least R-sqrt(2)>11/20 in (1), by comparison with
one of its circle corners and the unit diameter. This contradiction
proves (14). If d_R<=1, the supposition xi>1 is already impossible.
Thus all closed radius and physical-mask endpoints are covered.

## 6. The two complete endpoint resultants point upward

Use the actual C maximum-x point T_C=(0,r+1). About O it is

    T_C=(-X_C,h_C),
    X_C=xi>-1/2,
    h_C=r+1-zeta>0,
    C subset {x<=-X_C}.                                 (15)

This auxiliary support point leaves the original mean P_C=(0,s)
unchanged in the full C equation

    F_C-mu*West-mu_CH*N+mu_CB*g_CB=0.                    (16)

Its two incoming vertical components are zero and the strict positive
N_y from (7), and its outgoing CB moment is positive by (10).
The arbitrary-degree full-star consequence of
`BOUNDARY_SUPPORT_POINT_HALF_PLANE_MOMENT_MONOTONICITY.md` therefore
applies at the actual support (15) and the complete equation (16).
It gives

    (F_C)_y>0.                                          (17)

The arbitrary-frame center lemma from the full-radius path theorem
now puts the actual C center in the closed upper left quadrant, with
strictly negative x coordinate. In particular one may take

    pi/2<=arg(c_C)<=pi.                                 (18)

For E, its true two-force properness theorem applies because the actual
neighbor A is physically boundary. With all original means and masks,
it gives q_E=t_E F_E, t_E>0, and

    q_E=P_E-O+ell*n_E, ell>=0.

By (14), its vertical coordinate is strictly positive. Therefore

    (F_E)_y=(1-zeta+ell*q)/t_E>0.                       (19)

No E side ownership or interior placement of q_E is asserted. C remains
a full loaded degree-three body and H remains a full unloaded degree-four
body throughout.

## 7. A real auxiliary left support of E, for every original owner

The unchanged actual E has a minimum-x support point Q=(1-M,y_T)
in local D coordinates with

    0<=M<=q<1, y_T>=1.                                  (20)

To prove this at a vertex P_E, take the two actual inward orthonormal
axes whose dot products with n_E=(p,q) are nonnegative. At most one
axis can have negative x component: otherwise West would be a positive
combination of them, contradicting n_E.West<0. Any inward unit axis w
with w_x<0 satisfies q*w_y>=-p*w_x>0. Thus w_y>0 and -w_x<=q,
using p^2+q^2=1. Add this negative-x axis, if present, and zero on
the other axis. If neither decreases x, take Q=P_E. These choices
are actual minimum-x points and give (20).

For an owned-side application retain the complete original pose

    E=P_E+[0,1]n_E+[-rho,1-rho]J n_E,
    J n_E=(-q,p), 0<=rho<=1.

Its actual left corner Q=P_E+(1-rho)J n_E gives
M=(1-rho)q and y_T=1+(1-rho)p, again (20). This covers all original
side means and endpoints. The new point is only a geometric support
on E; P_E remains the original force application in every equation.

## 8. The E left-support coordinate exceeds -1/2

In the original-circle chart define

    X_E=1-M-xi,
    h_E=y_T-zeta>0.

We prove X_E>-1/2. Suppose instead X_E<=-1/2. Equations (11), (20)
give X_E> -d_R> -61/60. The actual horizontal halfwidth of E is
between 1/2 and 1/sqrt(2), so its center satisfies

    -31/60<(c_E)_x<1/4.                                 (21)

The lower inequality uses X_E>-61/60; the upper uses
-1/2+1/sqrt(2)<1/4. The actual support point Q has strictly positive
height h_E. If (c_E)_y<=0, its distance at most 1/sqrt(2) from Q
would give |(c_E)_y|<1/sqrt(2). Together with (21), that would imply

    |c_E|^2<(31/60)^2+1/2=2761/3600<25/16,

contrary to |c_E|>5/4 for a boundary unit. Hence (c_E)_y>0.
If its x coordinate is positive, (21) and |c_E|>5/4 give cosine
less than 1/5<1/2; if its x coordinate is nonpositive the angle is
already at least pi/2. Thus in every case

    pi/3<arg(c_E)<pi.                                   (22)

Together (18), (22) place arg(c_E)-arg(c_C) strictly between
-2*pi/3 and pi/2. The actual positive center advance less than 2*pi
from C to E is consequently either less than pi/2 or greater than
4*pi/3. Equal endpoint representatives cannot yield a distinct lift
in this interval. Both possibilities contradict (9).

This excludes the entire closed supposition X_E<=-1/2 and proves

    X_E>-1/2.                                            (23)

No assertion about an original E contact mean was used in this angular
endpoint argument.

## 9. Exact complete short-path contradiction and surviving scope

Use the two auxiliary actual support points T_C and Q on the unchanged
boundary path C-B-A-E. Its left and right supports are (15), (20),
with positive heights, coordinates X_C,X_E>-1/2, and exact span

    X_C+X_E=xi+(1-M-xi)=1-M<=1.

Their full actual circle resultants have positive vertical components
by (17), (19), and their real counterclockwise center lift was proved
in Section 4, including the coincident s=0 case. The complete theorem
`SHORT_BOUNDARY_PATH_ARBITRARY_FRAMES_FULL_RADIUS_OPPOSITE_SUPPORT_EXCLUSION.md`
therefore gives X_C+X_E>1, a contradiction.

This excludes the full positive-length CD return under a free HD,
including every original s,r, every coincident endpoint, every free or
owned DE normal and all physical circle masks. It applies to L0124
and to L124's physical-boundary-A mask without assigning a positive
load to A. Free-HD features with CD not a positive-length shared side
remain outside this source, as does the physical-clear-A mask of L124.
No whole original graph/load or coarse family is declared closed.

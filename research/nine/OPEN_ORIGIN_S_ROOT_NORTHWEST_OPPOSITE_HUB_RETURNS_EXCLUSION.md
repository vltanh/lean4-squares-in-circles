The proof uses the complete real X3 and H3 and their unchanged

## 2. The original HA normal is the same actual A axis as XA

Apply the general fixed-axis off-line selector to actual H3.
The fixed first normal is HX East at P. The two other points
P+u and P+v have vertical displacements beta and -alpha,
both nonzero. They are distinct physical singletons. Thus
the compatible pre-extraction selection removes a free HA
normal, and its strict H-vertex feature makes A its owner.
The selector also makes C own HC, though that consequence
will not be needed.

This selection is on actual fixed physical points. It does
not add a leaf at H, shorten H3, or select a value of a
shared application. The prelist is made before multipliers
are extracted, exactly as in the cited general theorem.

Let w=(1,z)/s, the other A own axis. Put

    U=u.m=(alpha*z+beta)/s>0,
    V=v.m=(beta*z-alpha)/s.

Equation (2) gives

    F_HA.m=p*(U-V)+lambda*alpha*U>0,                (3)

because

    U-V=[alpha*(1+z)+beta*(1-z)]/s>0.

The only A side axis with positive dot product with m is
m itself. Consequently the original unit normal from H
into A is exactly m. Write F_HA=eta*m, eta>0. Its strict
H cone gives

    V<0, hence beta*z<alpha,
    eta*(U+V)=lambda*alpha.                        (4)

The real points R,Q both belong to A's minimum-m side.
Their equal m projections give the exact geometric identity

    alpha*z=1-beta-b+t.                            (5)

Since t<b, it follows that

    z<(1-beta)/alpha=alpha/(1+beta).                (6)

No point of A or normal has been replaced in deriving (5).

## 3. The full-face lemma forces alpha<beta

    z<sqrt(2)-1<5/12.                              (7)

## 4. The original weak HC moment forces the opposite slope bound

Put

    K=beta-alpha+(alpha+beta)*z.

By (4), K=s*(U+V)>0 and eta/lambda=alpha*s/K.
The actual point S is

    S=(-a-beta,-t-alpha).

Using full force F_HC=-lambda*(1,0)-eta*m, its original
origin moment is exactly

    S cross F_HC
      =-lambda*(t+alpha)
         +(eta/s)*[a+beta+z*(t+alpha)].

The supplied nonpositive moment and (4) therefore give

    alpha*[a+beta+z*(t+alpha)]
                   <=(t+alpha)*K.                 (8)

Expanding (8) and substituting the actual side identity
(5), together with alpha^2+beta^2=1, gives

    t*(2beta-alpha+beta*z)
                   >=1-beta+beta*b+a*alpha.        (9)

This identity can also be checked before substitution:
the right side minus the left side of (8) is
t*(beta-alpha+beta*z)+alpha*beta*z-alpha^2-a*alpha.

By (4), the coefficient on the left of (9) is strictly
less than 2beta. Its right side is strictly greater
than beta*b. Hence

    t>b/2.                                         (10)

The unchanged genuine X3 formula (1) now gives

    z>[1-a]/[2(1-b+t)]>(1-a)/2>59/120.              (11)

Here 1-b+t<1 because t<b, and a<=epsilon<1/60.
Equations (7),(11) contradict each other:
59/120>5/12.

This excludes the entire declared Northwest/opposite-return
interface, including every original A tangent-side position,
all physical masks and all individual zero circle weights.

## 2. A uniform single-positive-moment axis obstruction

We prove the local geometric fact needed for this complete fan
domain. Let U be an actual unloaded unit, all of whose positive
forces are pair forces. Suppose one actual neighbor E contains

**Axis obstruction.** The actual U-to-E normal cannot be an own side
axis of U.

Suppose otherwise. Use a positively oriented own frame of U in
which that normal is -e_x. Since E is on the outward side of this
true support, while it contains D_epsilon openly, the unchanged

    U=[a,a+1] x [b,b+1], a>epsilon,
    E subset {x<=a}, h_E(e_x)=a.

Write its actual E application as p_E=(a,y_E). Its positive moment
is det(p_E,-e_x)=y_E, so y_E>0 and b+1>0. Also

    a+1>d.                                           (3)

No actual neighbor can use the right-side axis +e_x: the neighbor
would lie beyond its actual support a+1>d, whereas every contained
unit permits inner support at most d.

At the upper-right corner p=(a+1,c), c=b+1>0, an outward normal has
form n=(u,v), u,v>=0, u^2+v^2=1. A negative moment would require
(a+1)v<cu. For v>0 this gives

    p.n>(a+1)(u+v^2/u)=(a+1)/u>=a+1>d.

For v=0 the actual support is a+1>d directly. Hence no negative-
moment contact can use that complete upper-right corner cone.

The entire remaining support-feature cover is elementary and retains

* The top side has determinant a coordinate x>0, so its moment is

* The upper-left corner has normal (-u,v), u,v>=0 and determinant
  a v+c u>=0, so it has no negative moment.
* At the left side the only normal is -e_x, with determinant y.
* The bottom side and both lower-vertex normal cones have nonpositive
  vertical normal component. These include every negative moment

The right side and upper-right cone have already been excluded for
negative moments. Thus every other original U force has vertical

U's full force equation makes their vertical sum zero, so each other
force must have zero vertical component. Its normal must therefore

leaving only -e_x for every force, including U-to-E. Their positive

The argument also covers b=0, b<0 and every original shared-side mean.

**Adopted at these exact pins for the entire stated physical domain.**
Root authored the theorem; this record is author adoption, not an
independent review. Pentagon completed a fresh independent read of
the whole theorem and all five direct sources, including both partial
shared-side charts, every original normal and mean, physical masks,
zero coefficients, the closed radius threshold and the literal
original graph joins. No repair was required.

For R^2>=13/4, a true positively loaded degree-two unit V with
neighbors U,W and physical-boundary W cannot have its unchanged
U-V application P in W. The actual U and W retain arbitrary
degrees and loads, and no force equation at either is used.

The existing coincident-star theorem handles a singleton V-W
intersection, and the existing local full-side torque theorem handles
a full shared side with P at an endpoint. A point interior to the
shared segment leaves no tangent sector for U. The new argument
exhausts the two remaining partial-side possibilities.

If P is V's vertex, the real tangent sectors force the true V pair
normals West and South. Its full northeast circle resultant forces
a,b>=-1/2, including the physical two-corner masks. The original
mean and torque force b>0. The actual boundary W then lies strictly
inside the circle, by one comparison for a>=0 and another for
-1/2<=a<0; the latter includes a=-1/2 and R^2=13/4.
If P is V side-interior, both original pair normals are South and
the full circle resultant forces the actual centered radial-S V.
Its real neighbor W again has every corner strictly inside the
same circle. All original side means and coefficient endpoints remain.

This removes the entire coincident hub-application feature in all
twelve original interface rows of the earlier strict-vertex source,
with that source's strict-hub-normal restriction removed. It also
applies literally to lower-house U=A3,V=T2,W=D3, without any
near-port, root-frame, support-owner or concurrence premise.

The conclusion concerns the original U-V application. It does not
rule out another physical triple point elsewhere on a shared U-V
side, prove that every contact is a singleton, or provide an
unproved fixed-point selector premise. It does not exclude any
whole noncoincident original triangle family by itself.

No original fine or coarse checkbox changes from this result. The

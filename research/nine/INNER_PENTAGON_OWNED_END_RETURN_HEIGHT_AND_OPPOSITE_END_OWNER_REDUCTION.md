## 1. Exact actual domain and three conclusions

    H=[0,1]^2, n_HD=North,
    n_HA strictly Southwest, max_A y<1,
    n_HC strictly Northeast or East,
    P=(p,1), 0<p<1, q=1-p in(0,1),
    D=P+[0,1]u+[0,1]v,
    u=(a,b), v=(-b,a), a,b>0, a^2+b^2=1.

Here q is the fixed geometric distance 1-p, not a force parameter. Write
gamma>0 for the original HD magnitude and eta>0 for the original D3
return parameter. Put

    R=P+u, L=P+v,
    X=eta*(a+b)+gamma*a*b>0,
    Y_C=eta*(b-a)+gamma*b^2,
    Y_E=eta*(a-b)+gamma*a^2,
    Y_C+Y_E=gamma.

The actual D vectors toward C and E are respectively (X,Y_C) and
(-X,Y_E), with their original physical singleton applications R and L.
In the common selected certificate C owns CD and E owns DE; R and L
are strict relative-side-interior points of C and E. The same normals
and points are retained throughout this manuscript.

**Theorem.** The following complete necessary conclusions hold.

1. For both original HC types, the entire actual E satisfies y>0.
2. In the strict Northeast HC branch, A owns HA and its original H
   application (0,0) is in the relative interior of its actual side.
3. In that strict Northeast branch, the C feature cover in Section 3

These statements require no radius or origin-clearance premise. They

## 3. Complete C feature cover in the strict Northeast branch

The actual HC point is S=(1,1). Let

    N_C=sqrt(X^2+Y_C^2), n=(X,Y_C)/N_C,
    j=Jn=(-Y_C,X)/N_C.

Because C owns CD, its complete actual pose relative to the unchanged
CD application R is

    C=R+[0,1]n+[ell_C,ell_C+1]j, -1<ell_C<0.                 (2)

No value of ell_C is selected. The two coordinates of the actual HC
point S in this frame are exactly

    U=(S-R).n=[q*X-eta-gamma*b]/N_C,
    V=(S-R).j=-[q*Y_C+eta]/N_C.                              (3)

Since S belongs to C, U>=0. Also U<1, because q<1, X<=N_C and
eta+gamma*b>0. Moreover V<0 throughout the entire domain: indeed

    q*Y_C+eta=eta*[1+q*(b-a)]+q*gamma*b^2>0,

using 1+q*(b-a)>1-q>0.

Let N be the original unit normal from H into C. Its world components
are strictly positive. S is an actual minimum-N support point of C.
The inequalities U<1 and V<0<ell_C+1 imply

    N.n>=0, N.j>=0.                                         (4)

A negative first component would force U=1; a negative second
component would force V=ell_C+1>0. Both are impossible.

### 3.1. The entire Y_C>=0 alternative

Here N.n>0, so U=0. If N.j were positive, S would be the actual
corner R+ell_C*j, with both inward axes n,j having nonnegative world
y components. The whole C would then have y>=1. This contradicts
the actual BC contact and max_B y<1 from (1).

Therefore N.j=0 and N=n. Thus C owns HC on exactly the same side
axis as CD. Since N is strictly Northeast, necessarily Y_C>0; the
case Y_C=0 is completely excluded. The HC contact is a singleton
at an H vertex, so the strict singleton convention puts S in the
relative interior of that actual C side as well. Consequently

    q*X=eta+gamma*b,             |S-R|<1.                    (5)

The actual R and S are distinct because their heights differ by b>0.
The strict chord bound in (5) holds between two true interior points
of the same unit side, without replacing either application.

### 3.2. The entire Y_C<0 alternative

Now j has two strictly positive world components, so N.j>0 and
V=ell_C. There are exactly two cases.

If N.n>0, then U=0 and S is the actual C corner R+ell_C*j. Both
own components of N are positive. The contact is free at the two
H/C vertices, and again q*X=eta+gamma*b. Both inward C axes have
positive x components, so S is the unique leftmost C vertex.

If N.n=0, then N=j. C owns HC on the side perpendicular to its
original CD side. The actual H vertex lies in the relative interior
of that C side, hence 0<U<1; its original full side coordinate is
retained. Equation (3) still holds unchanged.

Equations (4) exhaust all possible signs, so there is no other HC
owner or contact feature in this branch.

## 4. The entire actual E lies strictly above y=0

The unchanged DE support point is L, whose world height is 1+a.

If Y_E>0, the actual minimum support of E with normal (-X,Y_E)
and the strict unit support-span lemma give min_E y>a>0. If
Y_E=0, the full vertical width at that support is at most one,
so min_E y>=a>0. This includes all actual side means, although
the current selected application is a strict side-interior point.

It remains to consider Y_E<0. Then b>a and

    Y_C=gamma-Y_E>gamma>0.                                  (6)

The East HC branch has Y_C<=0 by the original complete end reduction,
so (6) necessarily belongs to the strict Northeast branch. Section 3.1
applies. Since S-R=(q-a,-b), its actual side chord in (5) gives

    |S-R|^2=q^2-2*a*q+1<1, hence q<2*a.

The exact equality in (5) gives

    q*(a+b)-1=gamma*b^2/X>0.

Therefore

    a>1/[2*(a+b)]>=1/(2*sqrt(2))>1/3.                       (7)

Let N_E=sqrt(X^2+Y_E^2) and let n_E=(-X,Y_E)/N_E. The actual E
side through L has inward axis n_E and tangent
Jn_E=(-Y_E,-X)/N_E. Its tangent interval has length one and contains
zero strictly in its interior. The entire downward span from L is
therefore strictly below

    (X+|Y_E|)/N_E.                                          (8)

This keeps its complete original interval. Its normal coordinate
has length one and its downward tangent length is strictly below one.

Write r=|Y_E|/X. The exact original force formulas imply

    0<r<(b-a)/(a+b)<1.                                     (9)

For example, cross multiplication of the middle strict inequality
leaves the positive reserve gamma*a. The function
(1+r)/sqrt(1+r^2) strictly increases for 0<r<1. At the upper
comparison value in (9) it equals sqrt(2)*b. Thus (8) is strictly
below sqrt(2)*b, and

    min_E y>1+a-sqrt(2)*b>0.                                (10)

For the last strict inequality, both compared quantities are positive,
and their squared difference is
(1+a)^2-2*b^2=3*a^2+2*a-1=(3*a-1)*(a+1)>0 by (7).

This covers every sign of Y_E and both actual HC types, including the
whole original East-HC mean interval. No E circle force, tangential
moment assumption or fictitious transmitter equation was introduced.

## 5. The opposite end A owns its original HA contact

Assume now the strict Northeast HC branch. The actual AB contact and
min_B x>0 supply an actual point of A with positive x. The actual AE
contact and Section 4 supply an actual point of A with positive y.

The HA application is the original H corner (0,0), with strict
Southwest inward-to-A normal n_A. Suppose this point were a vertex
of A. Write its two orthonormal inward axes as r,s. Their dot products
with n_A are nonnegative.

To generate a point with positive x, at least one of r,s has positive
x. Since both components of n_A are negative, that axis then has
strictly negative y. Likewise an axis generating positive y must have
strictly negative x. They cannot be the same axis. If they are the
two different axes, both terms of their dot product are strictly
negative, contradicting orthogonality.

Thus the actual HA point is in the relative interior of an A side.
A owns the original HA normal. This conclusion is geometric; it
does not use a generic choice at A or depend on whether A is loaded.

Write its actual unit axis as n_A=(-u_A,-v_A), u_A,v_A>0, and put
j_A=(v_A,-u_A). There is an original side parameter kappa in(0,1)
such that the entire actual A is

    A=[0,1]n_A+[-kappa,1-kappa]j_A.                          (11)

In particular its two positive coordinate extents satisfy the exact
full-side identity

    max_A x=(1-kappa)*v_A,
    max_A y=kappa*u_A,
    (max_A x)/v_A+(max_A y)/u_A=1.                          (12)

The parameter is an actual physical side position, not a new choice of
mean. The real AB and AE contacts must both fit this same interval.

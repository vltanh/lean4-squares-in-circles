# An opposite-side upper extra supplies a high left-or-right shoulder

November 2, 2026. Complete hand consequence; no new finite table, Lean,
Lake, or CI. This is a resource lemma, not an exclusion of side extras.

Keep the complete original 010/11 caller and upward routing. Reflect
horizontally so the original bad-floor selected middle P has xP>=0.
Then the original right shoulder R is high: `yR>=3/5`. Indeed its
original cone gap gives `xR>707/1000`, whereas a low routed shoulder
would have `xR<121/200` by the endpoint inset computation.

Suppose an original extra S lies in the opposite closed side strip
`-13/12<xS<=-29/60` and above its own main B. We prove that at least
one of the two distinct original left-side units L,S has center height
at least3/5. The original selected L is allowed to be central.

Assume instead yL,yS<3/5. The uniform 3/4 clearance lemma and
`yB>-6/5` put both heights above `-9/20`, so both absolute heights are
less than3/5. Their half-unit disks avoid the original left endpoint
inset. The already proved localization therefore gives

    |xL|,|xS| <121/200.

The two original selected cone gaps and outsider strip give

    |xL|>beta=124/375,

whereas `|xS|>=29/60>beta`. Thus both horizontal coordinates lie in
the same reflected interval `(beta,121/200)`. The actual main B has

    5/13-1/60 < |xB| <31/60.

The largest possible distance from that main interval to the low
shoulder interval is less than6/25: its two endpoint differences are

    121/200-(5/13-1/60)=1849/7800<6/25,
    31/60-124/375=93/500<6/25.

The original center disks and the already known above-B order give

    yL-yB>97/100,       yS-yB>97/100,

because `1-(6/25)^2-(97/100)^2=3/2000>0`. Therefore both heights
belong to `(-23/100,3/5)`, an interval of length83/100. Their mutual
horizontal gap is less than

    121/200-124/375=823/3000<7/25.

Their squared center distance is consequently less than
`(7/25)^2+(83/100)^2<1`, with exact reserve2327/10000. This contradicts
their disjoint open half-unit center disks.

We have proved the claimed resource: in the presence of an opposite-side
extra above B there are distinct original high units on both horizontal
sides, namely R and at least one of L,S. Their center signs and heights
meet the shoulder assumptions of `WIDENED_JOINT_FIVE_SQUARE_CAP.md`.
If a further distinct original central unit U has whole floor at least
1/10, those three units together with the original endpoints give that
cap's complete caller and contradict `Dmain>2`.

Distinctness of U from the chosen high shoulder is an explicit caller
obligation. In particular this resource lemma alone does not license
using a central selected L simultaneously as the left shoulder and as
the replacement central unit.

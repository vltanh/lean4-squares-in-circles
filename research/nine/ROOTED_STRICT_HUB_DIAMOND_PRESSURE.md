## 1. Exact statement and unchanged full forces

There are nine actual interior-disjoint unit squares in D_R, where

    79/40 <= R <= sqrt(1105)/16.

Use the common compatible selected physical certificate. Suppose
H,K,A,B have the five actual positive edges

    HK, HA, HB, KA, KB,

with K of true positive degree three and A,B of true positive degree
two. The actual root H may have arbitrary additional positive forces
and any degree. No positive force at a private vertex is suppressed.
All physical contact means, one- and two-corner circle masks,
individual zero corner weights and the other five squares remain.

Write the original circle resultants as

    F_T = 2 sum_z lambda_(T,z) z,   lambda_(T,z) >= 0,

and put S=F_A+F_K+F_B. Then

    c_H dot S > 0.                                      (1)

There is no origin-containment premise, no separate minimization
premise and no hypothesis about the pressure of an outside branch.

The true private-degree and load argument is the one in
DIAMOND_ROOT_PRESSURE_AND_MIXED_BOUQUET_CLOSURE.md, Section 1.1.
A and B are loaded because an unloaded two-force square separates
its neighbors by its positive width, whereas H and K touch.
NO_UNLOADED_DIAMOND_HUB.md forces K loaded, keeping its full three
forces and both actual neighbor contacts HA,HB. If H were physically
boundary, H,K,A would be a forbidden physical boundary triangle.
Thus H is physically clear and its circle force is zero.

Let n,a_H,ell be the actual H-to-A, H-to-K and H-to-B normals, with
their unchanged positive magnitudes mu,eta,nu. Summing the three
actual private equations gives

    S = mu n + eta a_H + nu ell.                       (2)

The internal AK,KB forces cancel at their actual common applications.
Their moments cancel too. Distinct parallel HA and HK forces remain
at their distinct actual points for every torque equation.

Suppose, for contradiction, that

    c_H dot S <= 0.                                    (3)

This weak inequality is the only replacement for the older proof's
strict contrary pressure. Sections 2--6 below check the whole
original cover under precisely (3).

## 2. A strictly negative unique endpoint still exists

The accepted physical boundary path theorem
BOUNDARY_THREE_PATH_RESULTANT_CONE.md, Section 1, applies to A--K--B
without a degree-two equation at K. It gives

    F_K = alpha F_A + beta F_B,   alpha,beta > 0,        (4)

and proves that F_A,F_B are linearly independent. Indeed their actual
direction difference belongs to (0,29*pi/30); individual radial
weight endpoints are included. Consequently

    c_H dot S
      = (1+alpha)c_H dot F_A+(1+beta)c_H dot F_B.        (5)

The quarter-disk obstruction
CHORDED_HEXAGON_QUARTER_DISK_COMPLETION_OBSTRUCTION.md, Theorem 2,
says that no actual unit in this same nine-packing can contain the
closed disk D_(1/4). In particular c_H is nonzero: a unit centered
at O contains that disk.

If both endpoint projections in (5) were nonnegative, (3) would
force both to be zero. Their linear independence would then give
c_H=0, a contradiction. Thus one endpoint has strictly negative
projection. Reflect the actual rooted diamond if necessary and
call it A.

RADIAL_BOUNDARY_NEIGHBOR_PRESSURE.md makes every physical circle
corner of a two-corner neighbor strictly positive at c_H. Its bound
holds for each corner separately and so permits a zero individual
weight. The selected negative endpoint therefore has one physical
circle corner z_A and

    c_H dot z_A < 0.                                   (6)

The complete physical masks, in the order (K,A,B), are exactly

    SUS, UUS, SUU, UUU,

with U meaning one physical circle corner and S meaning two. No
positive-body or circle-weight count has been substituted for these
physical masks. Reflection exchanges only A and B, retaining K3.

### 6.1. Positive c_n

If c_n>0, the strictly negative A corner gives c_j>0. Equation
(3) and (9) then imply

    c_H dot ell <= -(mu+eta)c_n/nu < 0.             (10)

These are precisely the signs needed in Sections 2--2.2 of the
cited source. The whole proof there then excludes both B masks:
the actual triangle side-normal constraint gives ell dot n>=0;
the -j axis is excluded by real K/H support; hence HB is the
fixed lower H vertex. The KB side alternative misses that point,
and the remaining fixed K vertex has squared distance greater
than one from it. Unique-B fixed-tip selection or the complete
radial-B vertex-norm screen makes KB B-owned. The physical
triangle normal inequality then contradicts the two actual
B-side support points, including every perpendicular/mean
endpoint. No further root equation is used.

### 6.2. Nonpositive c_n, including zero

If c_n<=0, Sections 3.1--3.3 of the same present source are a
purely physical whole-H bound after the unchanged A2 equation.
They do not use an HB feature, a B mask, a root pressure sign,
or any force from an outside branch. They prove

    |Z| squared < 13/16 for every Z in H.             (11)

For clarity, their full numerical input is R>=79/40, which gives
(R-sqrt(2)) squared>5/16. The two real boundary contacts P,V
have opposite j signs, common n coordinate N in (0,1/2], and
norm at least R-sqrt(2). Writing
b_0=sqrt((R-sqrt(2)) squared-N squared) gives
J>=b_0 and r_0-J>=b_0. The actual strict side offsets bound
every H point by

    |Z| squared < (1-N) squared+(1-b_0) squared.

Their exact identity

    (N b_0) squared-k_0 squared/4
       =(N squared-k_0 squared)(1/4-N squared)>=0,
    k_0=sqrt((R-sqrt(2)) squared-1/4)>1/4,

gives (11) on the complete interval, not a finite pose cover.

Let (x,y) be the actual coordinates of c_H in H's own orthonormal
axes and put rho=sqrt(x squared+y squared). The farthest actual
H vertex has squared norm exactly

    rho squared+1/2+|x|+|y|
       >= rho squared+rho+1/2.                     (12)

By (11), rho squared+rho<5/16. Since this increasing expression
equals5/16 at rho=1/4, it follows that rho<1/4. In particular

    |x|<1/4 and |y|<1/4.

Thus H contains the closed disk D_(1/4), contrary to the
same-nine quarter-disk theorem. This excludes the entire
nonpositive-c_n row, including c_n=0, independently of whether
B is unique or radial.

The older proof used (11) only to exclude radial B and then
continued through two lengthy unique-B return families. At this
nine-square caller, neither late family is needed. No historical
source is deleted and no theorem at a different scope is retired.

## 7. Completion, selection and exact caller scope

Sections 2--6 exhaust every original positive load, physical
circle mask, HA/AK/HK/KB/HB owner and mean, and both radius
intervals, under the weak contrary hypothesis (3). It is
therefore impossible, proving the strict pressure (1).

The finite compatible selection is exactly the arbitrary-root
one already audited in
DIAMOND_ROOT_PRESSURE_AND_MIXED_BOUQUET_CLOSURE.md, Section 4:
prelist all ordered physical rooted diamonds and both endpoint
orders. Every fixed-tip or shared-block condition uses genuine
A2/B2 or K3 equations and actual applications, not a specified
degree, outside-force direction or petal geometry at H. Existing
coincident and variable-mean exceptions remain. No stronger
post-extraction selection is introduced by the equality argument.

The theorem applies to an actual genuine terminal hub-diamond
branch of a larger positive component whenever its three private
degrees are exactly K3/A2/B2. Its original S is the sum of that
branch's original circle loads, also the true sum of its three
forces at H. Arbitrary other H branches remain in the full H
equation. This supplies a strictly positive summand when a caller
has independently established signs for all of its other actual
branch sums.

It does not assign positive pressure to a general decorated
diamond: a private extra leaf, connector or chord changes the
stated true degree and requires a separate theorem. It does not
by itself close an entire C2.44 attachment cell or alter a ledger.

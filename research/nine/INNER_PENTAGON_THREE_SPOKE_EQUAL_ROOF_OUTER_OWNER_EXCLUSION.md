## 2. The exact two incoming weights and their actual weighted point

    theta=mu/(mu+nu)=t Delta/[a(1-p)+t Delta],

Pbar belongs to T's original minimum-alpha face, by convexity.

    F_T-(mu+nu)alpha+lambda g=0,

where Q is the original TU application and F_T is T's complete
positive circle resultant. Equation (5) retains both original

The weighted normal line through Pbar in direction alpha has

It is a force line, not the supporting side of T.

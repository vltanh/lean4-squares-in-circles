## 3. Classifying H's actual genuine S leaf

Side/vertex endpoint descriptions with an axis normal are already
included in the owned-axis cases, so this cover adds no genericity.

## 6. Two adjacent bottom-right/left root frames leave only a repeated-leaf cap

We are left with (3), where 0<V,k<1/3 and |x|<=epsilon. If both
V>epsilon and k>epsilon, the two actual adjacent rectangles A,H
cover the full radius-epsilon disk. A disk point with first coordinate
less than x is in int A; one with first coordinate greater than x is
in int H. At first coordinate exactly x, the point is in the relative
interior of both genuine sides, and A,H together cover a neighborhood
of it. No distinct actual unit C can contain such a point: interior
points of C approach it and would enter int A or int H. Since C
does contain its actual epsilon e_C, at least one of V,k is <=epsilon.

Suppose first V<=epsilon. Equation (3) gives

    x<=1-sqrt(d^2-epsilon^2)=1-sqrt(1+2 epsilon)<0,
    x/epsilon<=-2/(sqrt(1+2 epsilon)+1)
               <-120/121<-59/60.                    (4)

contains [-epsilon,epsilon] strictly. Its actual side separation

    (e_C)_x<=x/epsilon<-59/60,
    (e_A)_x=(x-1)/d<-1/d<-60/61<-59/60.               (5)

If instead k<=epsilon, the symmetric calculation gives

    x>=sqrt(1+2 epsilon)-1>0,
    x/epsilon>120/121>59/60,
    V>epsilon.

Now A's transverse interval strictly contains [-epsilon,epsilon],

    (e_C)_x>59/60,
    (e_H)_x>60/61>59/60.                              (6)

This contradicts the disjoint inscribed open disks of those two

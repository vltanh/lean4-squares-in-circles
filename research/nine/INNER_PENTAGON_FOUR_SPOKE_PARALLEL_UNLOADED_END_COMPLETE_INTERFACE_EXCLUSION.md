## 2. All-parallel forces reduce to an actual D side axis

The physical H--C contact first forces n_DH=n_DC. Indeed if these two
parallel unit normals had opposite signs, the actual H and C would

normal as n. Full positive D force balance then gives

    n_DE=-n, mu_E=mu_H+mu_C, mu_H,mu_C>0.              (1)

Suppose n is not an own axis of D. It strictly exposes one D vertex P,
so both physical DH and DC intersections are the singleton P. At
either neighboring unit this is either a side-interior point, whose
local tangent half-plane has angle pi, or a vertex. In the vertex case
the retained strict point-normal convention strictly exposes that
neighbor's vertex too, so both inward edge rays have strictly positive
scalar product with n. Its closed right-angle tangent sector, apart
from its apex, lies strictly inside the n-positive open half-plane.

If either neighbor has a side-interior point, its inward half-plane
overlaps the nonempty inward sector of the other neighbor. If both
are vertices, their two angle-pi/2 sectors cannot have disjoint angular
interiors while both are strictly inside one angle-pi half-plane:
disjoint sectors of total angle pi would have to reach both boundary
rays of that half-plane. In every case the actual open H and C would
overlap near P. This is impossible.

Thus n is an actual own axis of D. Rotate the coordinates so that

    D=[0,1]^2, n_DH=n_DC=East, n_DE=West.

The actual circle origin has coordinates O=(X,Y) in this physical D
chart. It has not been moved to the center of D. Write the unchanged
applications or complete shared means as

    P_H=(1,h), P_C=(1,c), P_E=(0,e),
    0<=h,c,e<=1.

The same selected far pair has |P_C-P_E|>1, as proved in Section 9.
Consequently c!=e. Full D torque, with all three original terms, is

    (mu_H+mu_C)e=mu_H h+mu_C c.                       (2)

Hence h!=c. Reflect the whole chart vertically if necessary so that

    0<=h<e<c<=1.                                     (3)

In particular the D/H and D/C original mean points cannot coincide.
No shared side has been converted to a singleton, and no mean has
been selected. The complete actual CD and DE contact sets also have
pairwise distance greater than one. Being intervals on the opposite
vertical D sides, their entire height intervals are disjoint and the
C interval is strictly above the E interval, consistently with (3).

The D boundary arc through H is the occupied lower arc. The actual

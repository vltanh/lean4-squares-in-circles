# Every physical triangle with two boundary units has zero origin winding

Date: October 28, 2026.

**Status: complete proposed degree-free geometric theorem, with a
whole-domain moment consequence for the original D0235.L0134 high-radius
interface; frozen for independent review.** No original graph/load

Keep all nine actual closed unit squares with pairwise disjoint open
interiors inside the same circle about O, with

    79/40 <= R <= sqrt(1105)/16,
    d=sqrt(R^2-1/4)-1,
    epsilon=max(d-1,0)<1/60.                         (1)

A boundary unit means an actual unit touching this circle. It need

full shared-side mean, physical circle mask, zero individual corner

## 1. Three strict bounds from the complete nine-unit packing

The quarter-disk completion theorem excludes any actual unit containing
the closed disk D_(1/4). If the actual center c of a unit satisfied
|c|<=1/4, then in either of that unit's own orthonormal axes its center
coordinate would have absolute value at most 1/4. Its two closed unit
intervals would therefore contain [-1/4,1/4]. The whole closed disk
D_(1/4) would be contained in that unit, a contradiction. Consequently

    |c|>1/4 for every one of the nine actual units.    (2)

The complete boundary angular-order theorem gives, for the center a
of a boundary unit A,

    |a|>5/4,
    angular shadow of A has length <pi/3.            (3)

For any actual point p of A choose an actual circle corner z of A.
The unit diameter gives

    |p| >= |z|-|z-p| >= R-sqrt(2)>11/20.             (4)

The strict rational reserve in (4) follows from
sqrt(2)<57/40 and R>=79/40. Finally, for two touching boundary units
A,B with centers a,b, the same angular-order theorem gives their
minor center gap

    0<gamma<2pi/5.                                  (5)

All four statements concern the unchanged physical units. In
particular (2) uses the eight other real units even when they carry
zero forces or are absent from the selected positive component.

## 2. Every boundary-contact bent edge can be straightened away from O

Let K touch a boundary unit A. Let c,a be their actual centers and
p any actual point of their common contact set. The point may be an
original side mean, either closed endpoint, or any other actual point
of the common physical face. Then

    O is not in conv(c,p,a).                         (6)

Translate the coordinate chart so O=0. If (6) failed, the coefficient
of c in a convex representation of zero would be positive, because
the segment [p,a] lies in the origin-free convex A. Therefore there
would be q in [p,a] on the ray in direction -c. The entire segment
[p,a] lies in A, so (3) gives

    angle(p,-c)=angle(p,q)<pi/3.

Using (2), (4) and cos(pi/3)=1/2,

    |p-c|^2
      =|p|^2+|c|^2+2|p||c|cos(angle(p,-c))
      >(11/20)^2+(1/4)^2+(11/20)(1/4)
      =201/400>1/2.                                 (7)

But p belongs to the real unit K, so its distance to K's actual
center is at most the circumradius 1/sqrt(2). This contradiction
proves (6), including all closed boundary cases.

The polygonal path c--p--a can consequently be homotoped, with its
two centers fixed, to the straight segment c--a within the filled
triangle conv(c,p,a). Every point of this homotopy avoids O. This
is a homotopy of a drawn path only: it moves no square, force,
normal or original application.

## 3. The complete triangle theorem

Let K,A,B be three distinct actual units touching pairwise, with A
and B boundary units. Their actual centers are c,a,b. Then

    O is not in conv(c,a,b).                         (8)

Indeed, (5) puts the cone generated by a,b inside an angle gamma<pi.
If zero belonged to this center triangle, then -c would lie in that
cone. At least one endpoint z in {a,b} would therefore satisfy

    angle(z,-c)<=gamma/2<pi/5,
    cos(angle(z,-c))>4/5.

The last comparison follows from
cos(pi/5)=(1+sqrt(5))/4>4/5. Equations (2)--(3) would give

    |z-c|^2
      > (5/4)^2+(1/4)^2+2(5/4)(1/4)(4/5)
      =17/8>2.                                      (9)

This contradicts the center-distance bound sqrt(2) for two touching
unit squares. The argument includes zero coefficients in the center
triangle representation and degenerate center triangles.

Choose any actual contact points p_KA,p_AB,p_BK. The entire physical
center/contact cycle

    c--p_KA--a--p_AB--b--p_BK--c                      (10)

avoids O and has winding number zero about O. To see this, every edge
has at least one boundary endpoint, so Section 2 straightens all three
bent edges away from O. The resulting straight center triangle avoids
O by (8). It can be contracted inside its convex hull away from O,
so its winding number, and therefore that of (10), is zero.

This proves the theorem for every original full contact mean and every
weak contact endpoint. The path (10) may have coincident contact points
or strands; winding and the displayed homotopies remain well-defined
because their compact images avoid O. If the compatible physical
embedding routes such a cycle to a nearby simple Jordan cycle, choose
its finite routing neighborhoods smaller than this positive clearance.
The routed cycle has the same zero winding.

**Theorem.** In the complete nine-unit radius window (1), every actual
physical contact triangle with at least two boundary units has zero
winding about O, for all actual contact-point choices. The result is
independent of all three true positive degrees and all force equations.

## 4. A positive-moment boundary triangle forces its third unit near O

Consider such a triangle in the retained selected physical graph. Give
each edge its original directed vector f at its original application p.
Suppose the three edges admit a cyclic orientation for which

    det(p-O,f)>0 on all three edges.                 (11)

Then the remaining unit K must meet the closed disk D_epsilon.

The two boundary units avoid that disk by (4) and epsilon<1/60. If K
also avoided it, the complete degree-free positive-moment cycle theorem
would give positive winding about O. That theorem applies to the three
original ports even when the three bodies have additional positive
forces or arbitrary physical circle masks. Its conclusion contradicts
Section 3. Hence K meets D_epsilon, as asserted.

For later zero-moment endpoints, the following full geometric fact is
also useful. If an actual contact K--A with boundary A has zero origin
moment, then K meets D_epsilon. Here is its complete proof.

Every actual unit center satisfies |c|<=d+1/2, by the farthest-corner
identity |c|^2+1/2+|c.u|+|c.v| and |c.u|+|c.v|>=|c|. A unit contained
in the half-plane e.x>=t, for unit e, has center e-coordinate at least
t+1/2. Thus any such actual supporting height satisfies t<=d.

The contact application p is nonzero by (4). Write p=t e, t>0. Its
zero moment says its actual normal is parallel to e. The outer unit is
in e.x>=t and the inner unit I is in e.x<=t, with p an actual maximum-e
support point of I. In particular t<=d<61/60. If O belongs to I, it
already meets D_epsilon. Otherwise an own-axis interval of I misses
zero. Reflect that axis to write this interval [a,a+1], a>0. Because
p=t e and p lies in this positive interval, e has positive component
along that axis; the support condition gives p.u=a+1. Therefore

    a+1<=t<=d, so a<=d-1<=epsilon.                  (12)

If the second own interval also missed zero, the same argument, after
reflecting that axis, would give both absolute coordinates of p greater
than one. Then t>sqrt(2)>d, impossible. Thus the second interval contains
zero, and the actual point (a,0) of I has norm at most epsilon. In every
case I meets D_epsilon. A boundary unit cannot be I by (4), so I=K.
This proves the claim for the full actual mean, every support owner,
all axis-normal endpoints and all true degrees. When d<=1, (12) simply
rules out the origin-free alternative.

## 5. Exact unchanged D0235.L0134 moment reduction

Retain the original fine family

    M6.B6.HEXAGON.D0235.L0134.

Its complete rim is 01,12,23,34,45,50, its positive chords are 02,35,
and its full positive circle-load set is {0,1,3,4}. The unloaded units
2 and 5 have true positive degree three. Loaded 0 and 3 also have true
degree three; loaded 1 and 4 have true degree two. All nine units and
all original additional physical contacts remain.

Orient the actual exterior rim counterclockwise. The boundary units
have the induced circular order 0,1,3,4. This uses the unchanged
occupied plane rim and the disjoint physical radial accesses through
the boundary units. The actual contacts 01 and 34 are the respective
counterclockwise minor gaps: the angular-neighbor rule excludes the
reverse minor arc, which would contain the other two boundary centers.
The full boundary-contact moment-orientation theorem consequently gives

    m_01=x>0,    m_34=y>0,                            (13)

where m_ij=det(p_ij-O,f_ij), with f_ij the original vector directed from
i to j, and m_ji=-m_ij. No force magnitude or mean is changed.

The unchanged origin torque equations at the true-degree-two units 1
and 4 give m_12=x and m_45=y. All original circle atoms have zero
origin moment. The full torques at 2,3,5,0 therefore give exactly

    m_23=m_50=r,
    m_02=r-x,
    m_35=r-y.                                       (14)

For example at 2, -m_12-m_02+m_23=0; at 0,
m_01+m_02-m_50=0. At 3 and 5 the two remaining equations give the
second equality in (14). None of these true-degree-three bodies has
been replaced by a two-force transmitter.

If r<x, the actual triangle 0->1->2->0 has strictly positive moments
x,x,x-r. Section 4 forces unit 2 to meet D_epsilon. If r=x, the actual
edge 02 has zero moment and the zero-moment part of Section 4 gives

    r<=x implies unit 2 meets D_epsilon.             (15)

Likewise, for the actual triangle 3->4->5->3,

    r<=y implies unit 5 meets D_epsilon.             (16)

Both implications hold throughout the complete original radius window.
They use the entire original force/moment certificate and no shortened
degree hypothesis.

In the high-radius domain

    sqrt(17)/2<R<=sqrt(1105)/16,                      (17)

the selected actual four-ring 0--2--3--5--0 has boundary opposite units
0,3. The complete high-radius two-near four-ring theorem forbids both
opposite units 2,5 meeting D_epsilon. Equations (15)--(16) yield

    r>min(x,y)>0.                                   (18)

This removes the entire continuous high-radius original moment domain
r<=min(x,y), including both zero-chord boundaries and every physical
mask/owner/mean. It is a necessary restriction on the surviving original
family, not its complete exclusion. In particular the remaining domain
r>min(x,y), the lower/critical-radius continuation, and the actual

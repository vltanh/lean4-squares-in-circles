## 2. Zero cycle moment puts O in two actual triangle units

Suppose instead the common directed cycle moment is zero. At any
body with a nonparallel pair, both of its actual normal lines pass
through O and therefore concur at O.

At most one body has a parallel cycle pair. Two parallel pairs
would make all three edge directions collinear. At any one body
the two parallel outward normals must have the same sign, because
opposite supports of that unit separate its two touching neighbors.
Three cyclically directed edge signs cannot satisfy this same-
outward-sign condition at all three bodies. Equivalently this is
the complete parallel exclusion in the external-resultant theorem.

Choose two of the nonparallel bodies, say T_i,T_j. They have their
genuine concurrences at O. The radius-free physical proper-port

Thus a+b=0, so a=b=0 and p=O. Both actual closed units T_i,T_j
contain O.

This contradicts O in int E and pairwise interior disjointness:

their open interiors overlapping nearby. The contradiction covers

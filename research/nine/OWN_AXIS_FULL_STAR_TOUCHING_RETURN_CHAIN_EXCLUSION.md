## 1. A complete physical two-return direction theorem

Let H,B,C be distinct actual closed unit squares with pairwise
disjoint open interiors and all three physical contacts HB,HC,BC.
Let e be an own unit side axis of H. Let n_B,n_C be any actual
unit common supporting normals directed from H toward B,C,
respectively, at their original physical applications or full
side means. Suppose positive scalars b,c,Lambda satisfy

    b*n_B+c*n_C=-Lambda*e.                         (1)

**Theorem 1.** Both actual normals then have strictly negative
own-axis projections:

    n_B dot e<0, n_C dot e<0.                     (2)

No containing circle, force balance at B or C, positive-degree
restriction, load, contact owner or distinct-application premise
is part of this physical statement. The coefficients in (1) are
simply positive scalars. In a force caller they may be the unchanged
original force magnitudes.

Rotate the coordinate frame so e=East and H is axis-aligned.
Suppose n_B has nonnegative x component. The full x component of
(1) gives (n_C)_x<0, and its y component is exactly

    b*(n_B)_y+c*(n_C)_y=0.                       (3)

If (n_B)_y=0, the unit normal n_B is East, while (3) makes n_C
West. East is an own H axis, and the physical H-B-C triangle
requires n_B dot n_C>=0. Their actual scalar product is -1,
a contradiction. Every East or West side mean is covered.

If (n_B)_x=0, then n_B is North or South. Equation (3) makes
(n_C)_y nonzero with the opposite sign. Consequently
n_B dot n_C<0, again contradicting the owned-axis physical
triangle theorem with the unchanged n_B.

The only remaining case has (n_B)_x>0 and (n_B)_y!=0.
If (n_B)_y>0, n_B strictly exposes H's Northeast vertex, and
(3) together with (n_C)_x<0 makes n_C strictly Southwest.
These are opposite H vertices. If (n_B)_y<0, the two strict
features are instead Southeast and Northwest, again opposite.
The complete purely physical opposite-vertex triangle obstruction
forbids the actual B-C contact in either case.

These rows exhaust every n_B with nonnegative x component and
prove n_B dot e<0. Interchanging the names and coefficients of
B,C in the same argument proves n_C dot e<0. This proves (2),
including all own-axis limits, every actual side-mean endpoint,
and every originally permitted free normal.

## 2. Arbitrary-degree full-star consequence with an actual chain

Let H now carry an original compatible physical force certificate
at any actual positive degree. Retain distinct positive neighbors
B,C and another actual positive neighbor A. Suppose

* B and C physically touch;
* A and B physically touch;
* the original H-to-A unit normal is an own H axis e;
* the literal sum of every remaining original H force satisfies

      G=F_H+sum_{V in N(H), V not B,C} F_HV
       =Lambda*e, Lambda>0.                       (4)

Here F_H is H's complete original circle resultant, possibly zero.
Every actual pair force in N(H) appears exactly once either in
G or as F_HB,F_HC. The notation G denotes only the finite sum of
those unchanged vectors. It is not a physical neighbor, a new
application, a replacement support or a three-force certificate.
All original torque terms remain at their original applications.

**Theorem 2.** Such a complete original equilibrium is impossible.

Indeed its full original vector equation is

    F_HB+F_HC=-G=-Lambda*e.

The actual H-B-C triangle and the original positive magnitudes
therefore satisfy Theorem 1, which gives n_HB dot e<0. But the
actual H-A-B triangle, with its original owned H-to-A axis e,
gives n_HB dot e>=0 by the physical owned-axis theorem. This is
a contradiction. No force or moment equation at A,B,C is used.

The circle load and true degree of H are arbitrary provided the
entire original sum (4) really has that positive own-axis value.
In particular an omitted force or a proposed virtual return cannot
be used to manufacture (4). No circle containment bound is used
by this local full-star consequence.

## 3. An entire radius-free end-pair obstruction at an unloaded H4 fan

Let an actual unloaded unit H have exactly four distinct original
positive neighbors X,A,B,C. Suppose the actual physical contacts

    XA, AB, BC

are all present. The neighbor degrees, their circle loads, their
other positive forces and every other actual packing unit are
arbitrary. All original applications, means and selected normals
at H are retained.

**Corollary 3.** The original HX and HA normals cannot both equal
one own H axis e. Likewise the original HB and HC normals cannot
both equal one own H axis.

For the first assertion, the entire original remaining sum is

    G=F_HX+F_HA=(lambda_X+lambda_A)*e,
    lambda_X,lambda_A>0.

This is exactly (4), because H is unloaded and its full positive
neighborhood is X,A,B,C. The actual A-B and B-C contacts supply
Theorem 2, which gives a contradiction. No assumption about XA
is needed for this first implication; that extra physical contact
is retained and is useful for the symmetric formulation.

Reverse the actual path X,A,B,C to C,B,A,X. The same theorem
then excludes the original HB and HC normals being the same
own H axis, using the real BA and AX contacts and the complete
remaining sum F_HB+F_HC. This is a graph relabeling of the whole
actual star, not removal of a physical edge or original force.

Consequently both endpoint pairs of the four-spoke physical fan
are excluded at once, throughout every radius and physical mask,
whenever their two original normals coincide on an own H axis.
The other positive degrees and force equations are unrestricted.
Additional zero-force physical contacts do not weaken the result.

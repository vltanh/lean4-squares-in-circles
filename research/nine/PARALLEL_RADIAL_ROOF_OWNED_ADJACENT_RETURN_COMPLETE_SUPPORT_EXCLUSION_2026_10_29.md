## 3. Two real support inequalities and the full roof inequality

For every contained unit with center c, its farthest-corner squared
norm is at least |c|^2+|c|+1/2. Hence |c|<=d+1/2. A unit's
half-width in any unit direction is at least1/2. Its actual minimum
support in every unit direction is therefore at most d. This is a
physical containment theorem for the unchanged unit, with no degree,
load, side-owner or physical circle premise.

The original incoming unit directions of B and C are respectively

    nB=(-s-lambda,l)/LB, LB=sqrt(1+2*lambda*s+lambda^2),
    nC=(s-r,-l)/MC, MC=sqrt(1-2*r*s+r^2).           (11)

Both denominators are positive because l>0. Applying the support
bound to the real points (7),(9) gives the exact necessary inequalities

    1+lambda*(l+s)+l*yA<=d*(LB+s+lambda),          (12)
    K+r*S-l*yD<=d*(MC-s+r).                        (13)

Their right-hand factors are positive: LB>|s+lambda| and MC>|r-s|.
Adding them and using l*(yA-yD)=T gives

    N:=1+K+T+lambda*(l+s)+r*S
       <=d*(LB+MC+lambda+r).                       (14)

This sum uses actual B,C, both original forces at V and the full
unchanged AD stars. It does not replace the two external squares
by independent hypothetical supports.

The upper actual roof height yA<1/2 in (10) is exactly

    G(alpha,beta):=(T-K)*(2*T-l)*(l-s)-L*(l-L)<0.   (15)

Every factor multiplied to obtain (15) is positive. Also the other
roof height yD>-1/2 gives yA>delta-1/2. Both bounds will be used
at their original full-star scopes.

## 2. Every physical boundary-B contact is on a strict inner side

Every contained unit center has norm at most 3/2 at R^2=17/4, by
the farthest-corner inequality |c|^2+|c|+1/2<=R^2.

If B met A in the relative interior of its east side, the original
normal would be east and c_Bx>=3/2. The center bound forces c_B=(3/2,0)
and the minimum half-width forces B to be exactly E. This contradicts
the distinct actual body labels. The north-side alternative similarly
repeats N.

If B contained A's northeast vertex, a common support normal n=(a,b)
with a,b>0 would give n.c_B>=a+b+1/2>3/2, since |n|=1 and a+b>1.
An axis endpoint instead forces E or N by the same center argument.
Thus the northeast vertex is impossible in every actual normal feature.

The vertices (1,0),(0,1) are in the relative interiors of E's and N's
inner sides respectively. A distinct disjoint unit containing either
point must have the corresponding actual inner-side support. The
radial-S inner-face theorem C7_FORCE_POLYGON.md, Lemma 2.1, makes such
a neighboring unit physically circle-clear. Thus neither can belong to
the boundary B. O cannot belong to B either, since R>sqrt(2).

These exclusions exhaust A's two outer sides and all its vertices.
After interchanging A's two own axes if necessary, the actual AB contact
and normal are therefore

    p_AB=(0,y), 0<y<1,   n_AB=-e_x.                     (2)

This complete physical argument is exactly the local boundary-neighbor

## 3. The original A torque and the actual B point cone contradict

Let n be the actual A-to-H normal. The physical triangle side-normal
lemma applied to the A-owned normal -e_x in (2) gives n_x<=0.
A's full, unchanged force equation is

    alpha e_x+beta e_y+mu n-nu e_x=0,
    alpha,beta,mu,nu>0.

Hence n_y<0. If n_x<0, the actual AH application is A's southwest
vertex O, whose origin moment is zero. The two radial leaf moments
are also zero, while the AB moment is nu y>0. A's genuine torque
equation would fail. Consequently n=-e_y, and the actual H satisfies

    H subset {z:z_y<=0}.                                (3)

Choose the original BH contact point q, including its true shared-side
mean when applicable. It belongs to H and B, so q_y<=0 by (3), whereas
p_AB=(0,y) belongs to B with y>0. Thus p_AB.q<=0. This contradicts
ACUTE_LOADED_CONTACT_GRAPH.md, Theorem A.1: every two points of a
physical circle-touching unit in this radius window have strictly
positive scalar product. Every original B physical corner mask and
every individual zero weight is included in this geometric theorem.

The critical case is excluded, proving the local theorem over the
whole radius interval.

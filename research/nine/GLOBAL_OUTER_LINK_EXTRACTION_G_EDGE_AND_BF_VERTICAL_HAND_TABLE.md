## 3. B and F overlap horizontally through their original insets

The inherited original left-cap bound gives

\[
 x_F\ge h_F-\frac12-\frac{92}{27}(x_C-h_C)
       >-\frac{115}{432}.
\]

Actual BC and `x_C-h_C<5/64` give `x_B<-27/64`. Hence

\[
                   x_F-x_B>\frac{269}{1728}>0.               \tag{8}
\]

Actual FG, (1), `h_F>=1/2`, and `x_B>-31/60` give

\[
 x_F-x_B<(49/64)-1/2+31/60=\frac{751}{960}.                  \tag{9}
\]

The accepted bound `h_B<37/64` gives `g_B>16/37`, and
`g_F>=1/(2sqrt(2))>7/20`. The latter strict comparison has squared
reserve `1/8-(7/20)^2=1/400`. Therefore

\[
 g_B+g_F>\frac{16}{37}+\frac7{20}
       =\frac{579}{740}
       =\frac{751}{960}+\frac1{7104}.                       \tag{10}
\]

Equations (8)--(10) prove

\[
                    |x_F-x_B|<g_B+g_F-1/7104.               \tag{11}
\]

Original physical nonoverlap makes the two contained insets separate
vertically. It remains to determine the sign. The completed C/F inset
theorem, proved independently of the present table, gives

\[
 y_F-y_C\ge g_C+g_F,
 \qquad |x_F-x_C|<g_C+g_F-1/25.
\]

If B were above F, its inset separation would imply

\[
 y_B-y_C\ge g_B+g_C+2g_F.
\]

On the other hand, the horizontal decomposition through the same F
and the two strict inset-overlap estimates give

\[
 x_C-x_B
 \le|x_C-x_F|+|x_F-x_B|
 <g_B+g_C+2g_F-\frac1{25}-\frac1{7104}.
\]

This contradicts the unchanged marked BC cone
`|y_B-y_C|<=x_C-x_B`. Thus F is above B. Its inset separation and (11)
prove both parts of (2).

Moreover `|x_F-x_B|<751/960<1`. A unit normal with nonpositive
vertical component has projection on `c_F-c_B` strictly below one,
while its physical support sum is at least one. Thus every usable

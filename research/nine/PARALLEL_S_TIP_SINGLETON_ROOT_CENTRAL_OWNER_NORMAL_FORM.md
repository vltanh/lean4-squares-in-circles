    e_B=-n,
    lambda_B=mu_HB+nu_BK.                      (2)

    T=d e_B-a_B-b_B,
    e_B.a_B>0, e_B.b_B>0.

    |T|=sqrt(2)-d.                             (3)

    R<=|T|+sqrt(2)=2sqrt(2)-d.                  (4)

    R+d>79/40+9/10=23/8>17/6>2sqrt(2),

Thus the entire oblique-leaf/parallel-normal feature is impossible,

### 7.1. Tilted K: the entire vertical-section comparison

Retain the actual tilted chart (9), with k=C/S. If c>=S, then
s<=C. The whole H lies at heights at most s-a<=C-a, while the

    q-2C+a>4/5-7/10=1/10.

This contradicts HK. Thus

    0<c<S.                                    (24)

Let t denote horizontal distance to the left of x=delta. The
open horizontal projections of H and K overlap on a connected
interval. On that interval both squares have nonempty open
vertical sections. Interior disjointness makes their vertical
order constant: an order change would require intersecting open
sections by continuity. Near t=0, H starts at height -a and K
at q, so H is below K throughout the common interval.

Write D=a+q in (0,1]. The upper boundary of H is exactly

    H_top(t)=-a+(s/c)t,              0<=t<=c,
    H_top(t)=s-a-(c/s)(t-c),         c<=t<=c+s.

The lower boundary of the actual K is exactly

    K_bottom(t)=q-k*t,               0<=t<=S,
    K_bottom(t)=q-C+(S/C)(t-S),      S<=t<=S+C.

All divisions are positive. By (24), t=c lies strictly inside
the common projection interval. The established vertical order
therefore gives

    K_bottom(c)-H_top(c)=D-s-k*c>=0.           (25)

Since D<=1, (25) implies the full angular order

    k<=c/(1+s)<c/s.                            (26)

The actual gap K_bottom-H_top has negative slope before c,
positive slope c/s-k between c and S, and positive slope

projection. Thus its unique minimum is at t=c. The same formulas
and continuity include the projection endpoints; t=0 has gap D>0.
Since HK is physically nonempty, that minimum must be zero.
Otherwise every common vertical section, including its endpoints,
would have a strictly positive gap and the squares could not touch.
Consequently

    a+q=s+k*c,
    H intersect K={W}, W=P+u,
    W=Q+(c/S)v_K,

This is the actual physical intersection, not a chosen mean.

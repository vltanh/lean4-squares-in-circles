## 1. Complete actual motif and exact star hypotheses

disjoint open interiors. Retain all of its actual units, physical
contacts, selected normals, original positive force magnitudes,
applications and full shared means. Choose six distinct units

    H,A,B,C,D,E.

Assume that H,A,D are genuinely unloaded and that their complete
original positive neighborhoods are exactly

    N(H)={A,B,C,D}, N(A)={H,B,E}, N(D)={H,C,E}.        (1)

All original terms in these true H4,A3,D3 stars have positive
magnitude. Their full original force and torque equations hold.
In particular the eight pair contacts

    HA, HB, HC, HD, AB, AE, DC, DE                     (2)

are positive and physically nonempty. Assume also the actual
physical contact

    B intersect C is nonempty.                       (3)

That contact need not carry a positive force. No force at BC
will be used. The original true degrees, loads, other positive
neighbors, physical masks and complete equations of B,C,E are
arbitrary and remain unchanged. Other actual units may attach
positively to any of B,C,E and retain every original force.
Additional zero-weight physical contacts at H,A,D also remain.

Require the same common compatible selected-normal conventions
and finite generic preselection as the complete local inputs:
strict original vertex/vertex singleton normals, actual unique
side normals, matched-junction choices, the general distinct-
singleton unloaded-three-force rule, and its fixed-axis off-line
preselection. These are chosen on the full actual packing before
positive multipliers are extracted, as proved by the generic
source theorems. No choice depends on a future shared mean,
load, positive degree or force magnitude.

**Theorem.** No selected original positive force system satisfying
the entire literal motif (1)--(3) exists.

The complete neighborhoods in (1) are indispensable. The theorem
cannot be applied to a larger H,A,D star by deleting positive
terms, or by aggregating several actual neighbors into one unit.
It permits larger degrees at B,C,E precisely because none of
their equilibrium equations is used.

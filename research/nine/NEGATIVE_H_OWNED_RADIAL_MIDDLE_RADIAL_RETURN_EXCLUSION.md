## 2. Negative pressure forces the uniform lower bound c>1/40

## 4. One exact monotonic bound controls the entire radius interval

Regard E,F,N as the functions of d in (7) and
\(N=\sqrt{d^2+2d-1}\). On \(91/100\le d\le1\),
\[
 \begin{gathered}

 E\le\frac35,\qquad N-1<\frac37,\qquad N'<\frac32,\\
 F'=\frac{4(1-4d^2)}{(4d^2+1)^2}\le-\frac{12}{25}.
 \end{gathered}
\tag{25}
\]
Here are full checks of the derivative comparisons.
E' decreases because
\(E''=16(1-12d^2)/(4d^2+1)^3<0\); at 91/100 it is
\(91000000/116229961<4/5\).

\(N'=(d+1)/N=\sqrt{1+2/N^2}<3/2\).

\(4(X-1)/(X+1)^2=-F'\) decreases, since its derivative is
\(4(3-X)/(X+1)^3<0\). Its minimum is \(12/25\) at X=4.

Because \(\sqrt2-1/2>9/10\), (25) yields
\[
 \begin{split}
 f'(d)
 &=E'(N-1)+EN'+(\sqrt2-\tfrac12)F'-1\\
 &<\frac{12}{35}+\frac9{10}-\frac{54}{125}-1
   =-\frac{331}{1750}<0.
 \end{split}
\tag{26}

The final exact margin is \(121/269525>0\).

Equations (10), (17), (24), (27) now give

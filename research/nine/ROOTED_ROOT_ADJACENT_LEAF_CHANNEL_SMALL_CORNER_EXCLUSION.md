## 4. A uniform two-small-support lemma for an actual square

Here is the geometric kernel, stated independently of any load.

The upper-right vertex belongs to both supporting faces, since

y=alpha and n.x=b. Its squared norm is

    |P|^2=(b^2-2*n_y*b*alpha+alpha^2)/n_x^2.         (16)

This is convex in (b,alpha) on the closed square [0,epsilon]^2.
At the four corners its values are 0, epsilon^2/n_x^2 (twice),
and 2*epsilon^2/(1+n_y). Since n_x>4/5, each value is at most
2*epsilon^2. This proves (1) in these cases.

    w_H=(C_H,-S_H), z_H=(-S_H,-C_H),

other possible n-maximizer is the adjacent vertex R=P+w_H.

    S_H/C_H<alpha/x.                                (17)

    rho<sqrt((3/5)^2+(1/60)^2)<2/3.

       > (1/rho-1)*(n_x*x-n_y*alpha)

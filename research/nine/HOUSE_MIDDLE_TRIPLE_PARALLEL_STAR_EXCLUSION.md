## 3. The full D equations give two strict coefficient comparisons

\[
 -\gamma a+\nu r-\kappa l=0,
 \qquad

\tag{6}

\[
                   \nu(r-s)=\gamma a>0,

\tag{7}

## 4. The entire roof support feature forces b>1/3

\[

## 5. The C-owned frame is too narrow to reach B

Because eta is an actual side axis of C, its exact horizontal width is
|eta_x|+|eta_y|. Equations (5),(8) give

\[
\begin{split}
 \operatorname{width}_x(C)
  &=la-mb+lb+ma\\
  &=a(l+m)+b(l-m)\\
  &<\sqrt2\,a,
\end{split}
\tag{14}
\]

using m>l and l+m<=sqrt(2). On the other hand C contains v with
v_x=b and an actual BC contact at x<=-1. Thus

\[
                           \operatorname{width}_x(C)\ge1+b.
\tag{15}
\]

Finally (13) and a^2+b^2=1 imply

\[
 (1+b)^2-2a^2=3b^2+2b-1=(3b-1)(b+1)>0,
                   \qquad1+b>\sqrt2\,a.
\tag{16}
\]

Equations (14)--(16) contradict one another. This proves the whole
parallel-A-star exclusion.

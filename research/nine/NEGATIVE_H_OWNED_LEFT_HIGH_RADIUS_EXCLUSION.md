## 2. A uniform bound on the actual H normal

\[

\[

\[

## 3. Every original H offset has strictly positive pressure

\[

\[

>q-a-b-\frac{5a+b}{2\sqrt{13}}.
\tag{13}
\]

Define

\[

 +\left(1+\frac{1}{2\sqrt{13}}\right)^2
 =\frac52+\frac6{\sqrt{13}}.
\]

\[
c_H\cdot z_A>q-R\sqrt C=R(R-\sqrt C).

\[

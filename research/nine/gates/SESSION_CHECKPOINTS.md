### Proof-state legend (use these terms, not an undifferentiated checkmark)

- **OPEN:** No complete proof of the exact claimed domain.
- **H — hand-written:** Full stated limited theorem has a manuscript, but its prerequisites or proof have not yet passed an independent reconstruction.
- **V — independently reviewed:** Source-level hand proof reconstructed with exact common selected-certificate, original bodies/cuts/means and any tables; does **not** imply a full original parent.
- **J — caller joined:** A reviewed source is proved applicable to a named original physical graph/load/owner domain with its nine real squares; still not enough if sibling domains remain.
- **C — canonical closed:** All original subdomains of one named source parent covered and independently checked; only now may its original checkbox and the 58-row or 2,513-ID source flag change.
- **BLOCKED:** A difficult *research target* has no presently certified terminal inequality. It is **not** a small session gate and carries no completion percentage.

A **proof-ready small task** has a precise input graph/load/force domain, one verifiable output (finite orbit table, particular original-side identity, or one quantified inequality), named prerequisites, and a source-level verification test. It may still be unexpectedly difficult. A large continuous family is recorded as **BLOCKED**, not disguised as an equally small checkbox. **Do not promise one finished gate per session.** Report a genuine result or zero, and never subdivide merely to inflate a numerator.

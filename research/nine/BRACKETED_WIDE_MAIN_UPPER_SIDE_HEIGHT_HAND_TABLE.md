## 1. Signed lower-side height refinement

For a left main M write its folded side-axis frame as
\((a_M,s_Mb_M),(-s_Mb_M,a_M)\), where

\[
 a_M=h+k,\quad b_M=h-k,\quad k=\sqrt{1/2-h^2},
\quad 1/2\le h\le13/20.
\]

The main center satisfies

\[
 h-61/60\le x_M\le1/60-g,\qquad g=1/(4h).        \tag{1}
\]

Let an original physical left-side unit S, with \(-u\le x_S\le-l\),
be below M. The accepted width-dependent theorem already proves

\[
 y_M>f(h),\quad
 f(h)=-2h/3+\max\{0,\min((h-1/2)/6,(27/50-h)/3)\}+1/400.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]

The new signed refinement is

\[
        s_M=+1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
        y_M>f(h)+(h-1/2)/4.                       \tag{3}
\]

No restriction is imposed on S's chirality. At h=1/2 the two main
chirality descriptions coincide, and the added term is zero.

### 1.1. Retain every physical lower-pair owner

Use the full three-parameter root

\[
 (h,t,X)\in[1/2,13/20]\times[0,1]\times[l,u],
\quad X=-x_S,
\]

where S has folded components
\(a=(1+t^2)^{-1/2}\), \(b=ta\), \(p=(a+b)/2\),
\(k_S=(a-b)/2\), and either chirality s=±1.

Reflect vertically. Then the reflected S is above the reflected M,
their chiralities are -s and -1, and the unchanged horizontal displacement
belongs to

\[
 d=[-X-(1/60-g),\ -X-(h-61/60)].                  \tag{4}
\]

If the main interval (1) is empty, the original branch is already empty.
Otherwise its maximal absolute displacement is at most 93/130<1. For
these original frames the exact common owned-axis support sum is

\[
 \sigma={1+|a a_M+s b b_M|+|-sba_M+ab_M|\over2}\ge1.           \tag{5}
\]

The complete upward owner list in the reflected configuration is

\[
 (-a_M,b_M),\quad(b_M,a_M),\quad(-s a,b),\quad(s b,a).        \tag{6}
\]

A zero vertical component is impossible because the horizontal center
gap is below one. A nonpositive vertical component cannot separate for
the same reason. Each remaining owner n supplies the lower bound

\[
 y_M-y_S\ge{\sigma-\max(n_xd_-,n_xd_+)\over n_y}.
\]

Take the minimum over the four owners. Its numerators are positive since
\(\sigma\ge1\) and \(|d|<1\). Retain also the center-distance bound
\(\sqrt{\sigma^2-D^2}\), D=max(|d_-|,|d_+|), and the main-inset
clearance

\[
 g+\min\left\{p,{1/2+(g-D)b\over a},
                  {1/2+(g-D)a\over b}\right\}.    \tag{7}
\]

The zero-b alternative is omitted. Here D-g<=43/130<1/2, so the
inset's horizontal axis and all wrong vertical signs are excluded and
both displayed numerators are positive. Write C for the maximum of
these three independently valid clearances.

### 1.2. The same original S corners supply its lower height

Rotation by a half turn sends the original S to center \((X,-y_S)\)
without changing its folded chirality s. Its four actual signed corner
offsets are

\[
 (p,-s k_S),\quad(s k_S,p),\quad(-p,s k_S),\quad(-s k_S,-p).
\]

Thus

\[
 -y_S\le U_s(X):=
 \min_j\{\sqrt{Q-(X+v_{j,x})^2}-v_{j,y},\rho\}.
\]

For each of s=±1 separately we therefore have

\[
                         y_M\ge C-U_s(X).         \tag{8}
\]

The complete table proves that (8) strictly exceeds the target in (3)
on the entire root, for both original S chiralities. The same S frame
appears in its physical clearance and all four corner inequalities.

The target \(f(h)+(h-1/2)/4\) is decreasing: its piecewise slopes are
-1/4, -3/4, and -5/12. Consequently its upper bound on a rational h-row
is its value at the lower endpoint. This explains the target direction
in both independent interval implementations.

## 2. The upper side original

Return to the original left main B and its original upper side unit H,
with \(x_H=-X\), X∈[l,u]. For each actual main chirality set

\[
 f_-(h)=f(h),\qquad f_+(h)=f(h)+(h-1/2)/4.
\]

Sections 1 and the accepted unsigned envelope give
\(y_B>f_{s_B}(h_B)\). Retain the actual upper H chirality s_H.
Its signed displacement from B is again the interval (4), now with
its original upward order. The complete owner list is

\[
 (s_Ba_B,b_B),\quad(-s_Bb_B,a_B),\quad
 (s_Ha_H,b_H),\quad(-s_Hb_H,a_H).
\]

Use the corresponding support sum (5) with the actual two signs, the
center-distance condition, and (7). Denote the resulting necessary
clearance by C(B,H). The initial height bound is

\[
                         y_H>f_{s_B}(h_B)+C(B,H). \tag{9}
\]

This preserves B's chirality between its lower-pair height theorem and
its upper-pair physical separation. Minimizing that sign independently
in the two pairs is not valid and was too weak in numerical discovery.

### 2.1. The original endpoint improves the height bound

The outer-actual-link endpoint reserve gives for the actual left endpoint

\[
 c_A=g_A-|y_A|\ge c_*+(32/9)V
              \ge c_*+(32/9)\eta_B=:c,
\quad\eta_B=h_B+g_B-1.                            \tag{10}
\]

That original endpoint contains the rectangle

\[
 [-937/480,-503/480]\times[-c,c].
\]

Its inward edge may be improved to \(-\min(503/480,\rho-c)\).
Reflect horizontally, put
\(E=\min(503/480,\rho-c)\) and \(\delta=X-E\), and use H's
reflected chirality -s_H. When X+h_H>E, horizontal separation from
the contained endpoint rectangle is impossible. The complete coordinate
and original H-owned alternatives give either

\[
 y_H\ge c+r_{-s_H},\qquad\text{or}\qquad
 y_H\le-c-r_{s_H},
\]

where

\[
 r_+=\min\{h_H,(1/2+\delta b_H)/a_H\},\qquad
 r_-=\min\{h_H,(1/2+\delta a_H)/b_H\}.            \tag{11}
\]

This is the previously accepted signed endpoint-rectangle lemma. The
zero-b term is omitted. If (9) plus c+r_{s_H} is positive, it rules
out the downward alternative, so retain the larger of (9) and the upward
bound from (11). Otherwise keep (9). If X+h_H<=E, retain (9) directly.
The negative chirality, possible small signed main gap, and every physical
owner are included.

The complete second table proves that this resulting lower height is
strictly greater than 3/5, for all four (s_B,s_H) choices on the same
three-parameter root. Both f_- and f_+ are decreasing, so their interval
lower bounds use the upper h endpoint. Together with the complete exact
replay, this proves the bracketed-side theorem. An independent agent has
reviewed the physical reduction and freshly repeated both complete
Fraction replays with no discrepancy.

November 2, 2026, America/Chicago. This review concerns the exact immutable
`positive_forest.json` and `negative_forest.json` from the archived v13
research package, rather than a live search or a claimed whole-mask proof.

**Conclusion:** the finite ancestor splits give a complete cover of the
normalized larger-gap original-nine `001/00` caller by the inherited
11,278 terminal exclusions and **231 explicitly unproved path domains**.
The smaller-gap caller is separately closed by its accepted hand/table
proof, and the common reflection and whole-witness reversal cover both
transverse signs and `100/00`. There is no omitted equality face or
missing tree child in this package. **Neither mask is closed.**

This is a review of mathematical coverage, signed-root normalization,
analytic escape unions, exact path interpretation and version transfer.
It does not claim a fresh replay of all numeric leaf arithmetic. The
closed-leaf arithmetic is supplied by the unchanged source-bound complete
reports; the soundness of the necessary geometric rows remains the
separate physical-model review obligation. These two acceptance steps
must not be conflated.

## 3. The exact domain of a registered path

A path domain means the normalized strict original mask predicate,
conjoined with every recorded rational interval choice and original
pair or analytic escape choice. All unmentioned original data and
physical pairs remain quantified and constrained by the original mask.

An angle or coordinate bisection uses closed children `[l,m]` and
`[m,u]`. A non-midpoint rational cut is independently parsed as an exact
fraction and required to satisfy `l<m<u`. Its two children have the same
closed-face semantics. `reconstruct` is called even on open frontier
paths; an open leaf is not permitted to carry an unchecked or impossible
out-of-parent cut.

An analytic escape union is evaluated in first-satisfied order. If its
primary halfspaces are `r_k(z)<=b_k`, branch k retains that halfspace and
the preceding **closed complements** `r_j(z)>=b_j`, `j<k`. For any point
in the union, its first satisfied primary row has all predecessors
strictly false, and hence satisfies these closed complements. Thus the
ordered children cover the parent. Their equality faces may overlap;
the cover need not be disjoint. This use of closed predecessors never
creates a positive geometric clearance.

The frozen pre-v13 numeric leaves are replayed by their own row
interpreters. The v9--v13 inward-row threshold is consistently `11/30`;
there is no hidden change of threshold under the same path label.
The independently reconstructed intervals and choices agree for every
retained old leaf. Older versions that omit later necessary rows or
predecessor restrictions prove supersets, which is sufficient. Source
versions are not silently exchanged when interpreting row multipliers.

## 4. Mathematical justification of every ancestor union

The following table records the whole union required at each type of
internal node. The independent tree checker verified that every stated
child is present. Impossible physical options are represented by proved
leaves, rather than disappearing from the tree.

| Node type | Full child union | Why it covers the original parent |
|---|---|---|
| Angle/position split or rational cut | Both closed children | An interval is the union of its two closed subintervals |
| `proxy` | Four coordinate axes/signs | The two unchanged contained aligned insets have disjoint interiors, so one signed coordinate gap is at least their inset-halfwidth sum |
| `sat` | Eight possibilities: either original owner, either of its two axes, either sign | The separating-axis criterion for two physical squares retains all their side normals and both directions |
| `lower_escape` | `x_X<=0` or `y_X>=y_C` | The accepted C-slant consequence excludes any distinct original in `x>=0,y<=y_C`; these closed alternatives cover its complement |
| `row_escape` | `x_X<=x_B`, `x_X>=x_D`, or one of the four signed height gaps reaches `1/8` | The all-angle B,X,D near-horizontal row theorem excludes the complementary closed horizontal-order/height rectangle |
| `asymmetric_escape` | The two horizontal escapes; `y_X-y_B<=0` or `>=1/3`; either signed D/X height gap at least `1/16` | The accepted asymmetric row theorem excludes the complementary closed rectangle for both D signs |
| `positive_row_escape` | The two horizontal escapes, or one of the four signed forms of `|y_X-y_B|+|y_D-y_X|` is at least `1/2` | The positive-D total-variation terminal applies; every such node independently requires the whole current D interval nonnegative |
| `inward_row_escape` | `x_H<=x_A`, `x_I>=x_D`, or one of eight signed A,H,I,D variations is at least `11/30` | The inward-endpoint row terminal applies; every such node requires the whole A interval nonpositive and whole D interval nonnegative, with H/I already sorted |
| `alternate_four_escape` | One of eight fixed-tuple cone/outer-actual slacks is at most zero | If every slack were positive the selected original tuple and unchanged E,F,G would enter an accepted actual-outer mask |
| `general_alternate_escape` | The same eight-way union for one of the 105 eligible tuples | The selector list is complete for its declared six-label family; each successful selector is a valid accepted-mask terminal, without an assertion that one always succeeds |
| `f_side_escape` | X left of F or right of F, with all four signed two-link height-variation choices on each side | The accepted F-side routing barrier forces the relevant variation above `3/5`; horizontal equality is included on both sides |
| Cardinal cap escape | Either original is at signed coordinate at most `3/5`, or either sign of their transverse gap is at least `29/60` | The universal cap-column theorem forces strictly larger transverse separation when both coordinates are at least `3/5` |

For the positive row the four height forms are
`+(y_D-y_B)`, `-(y_D-y_B)`,
`+(2y_X-y_B-y_D)`, and `-(2y_X-y_B-y_D)`.
Their maximum equals `|y_X-y_B|+|y_D-y_X|` exactly, including equalities.
For the inward and F-side rows the corresponding maximum formula for
the sum of three or two absolute values gives the printed complete
eight- or four-sign union. No height sign is selected numerically.

The all-sign small-row union, if used in a future descendant, additionally
requires all seven original tangent intervals inside `[-1/50,1/50]`.
This implies its sine bound. The full extra strip and original shoulder
orders supply the remaining theorem hypotheses. The source checks that
guard; it is not needed for any split in these two archived forests.

The six near-horizontal/asymmetric escape rows intentionally include
their equality boundaries. The excluded row theorem applies on a
closed region, so some displayed escape faces can themselves be
impossible. Retaining them weakens the proof search and preserves
coverage; it never removes a physical contact face.

## 5. No tree path or root is silently lost

At every internal node the independent checker groups the next recorded
step by a single action and parameter. It then checks exactly the full
expected child set. Two different next questions under the same prefix
are rejected. It also rejects duplicate terminals and a terminal that
is an ancestor of another terminal. Each recorded leaf and each recorded
frontier path must start with the requested D-sign prefix.

The positive forest contains 130 frontier paths and the negative forest
101. Their exact ancestors, intervals and chosen original pairs are
recoverable from the immutable paths. The two roots overlap only as
permitted by closed interval faces; they are a cover, not a claim of
pairwise disjoint geometric volumes. The number 231 counts unproved
registered path obligations, not connected components of configuration
space and not the number of physically realizable configurations.

The archived complete arithmetic reports certify every inherited closed
leaf on these same forest hashes. Once the original physical-row
soundness review is accepted, any strict original configuration entering
an inherited excluded leaf is impossible. Therefore every hypothetical
strict counterexample lies in the union of the **231 open domains**.
This is the precise useful full-mask coverage conclusion; it does not
infer that any one of those domains is feasible.

## 3. The physical HD partition is exhaustive before means are considered

Two closed convex squares with disjoint open interiors can intersect
in a point or in a positive-length segment, with no positive area.
A positive segment lies on actual sides of both squares, and fixes
their common normal. It remains a shared contact when the unchanged
aggregate mean is either endpoint.

A physical singleton is either vertex/side-interior or vertex/vertex.
The first has a geometrically fixed normal owned by the actual side
unit. The second uses a normal strictly exposing both vertices under
the retained convention, and is free. A side endpoint is a vertex,
so no endpoint lies outside this cover. In particular an axis shared
by both parallel frames cannot supply an extra singleton owner row:
collinear side interiors give a segment, and two vertices use the
strict convention.

Thus the four alternatives in the assembly are exhaustive: shared
HD, H-owned singleton HD, D-owned singleton HD, and free singleton HD.
No argument reads off this classification from the chosen mean alone.

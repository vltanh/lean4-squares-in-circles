    V=[a-1,a] times [b-1,b],
    Z=(a,b), a,b>1/2, a^2+b^2=R^2,
    F_V=kappa*Z, kappa>0.

The strict a,b bounds are a statement about the physical corner mask.
They would not follow merely because one coefficient of a two-corner
mask happened to vanish. The source keeps that distinction throughout.

A circle point cannot belong to another contained unit: every inward

the opposite direction strictly exposes the single inner vertex. Both
allowed applications would coincide, contrary to the explicit hypothesis.
This parallel exit does not assume distinct physical contacts are already
distinct means; actual application distinctness is supplied as a premise.

For an East V axis, the actual triangle dot-product theorem makes the

kappa*a makes full East force balance impossible. North is identical.
All remaining V features are therefore West, South and its three

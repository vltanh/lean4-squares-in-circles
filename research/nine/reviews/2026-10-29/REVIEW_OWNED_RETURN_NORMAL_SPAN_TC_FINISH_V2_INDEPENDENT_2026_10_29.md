## 2. Actual-unit perpendicular span, including support-side endpoints

I reconstructed Section 1 for an arbitrary actual unit square M and
its actual minimum-g support point Q. In a proper own frame with
g=alpha e1+beta e2 and alpha,beta nonnegative, the proper quarter
turn is j=-beta e1+alpha e2.

If both coefficients are positive, Q is the unique minimum corner.
For every actual Y in M, the two inward coordinates of Y-Q belong
to [0,1]. Consequently its j coordinate is between -beta and
alpha, and hence between -1 and 1. This is a statement about the
entire square, including its interior and all boundary features.

If either coefficient vanishes, Q can lie anywhere on the complete
minimum support side. The transverse interval of the whole square
has length one. Subtracting any actual point of that interval gives
the same bounds [-1,1]. This includes either endpoint and every
original weighted shared-side mean. Neither an owner nor a unique
vertex is assumed in that row.

normal, or any physical application. In particular the inequality

    (Q-Y).j <= 1

holds at the original support point in every orientation of M.

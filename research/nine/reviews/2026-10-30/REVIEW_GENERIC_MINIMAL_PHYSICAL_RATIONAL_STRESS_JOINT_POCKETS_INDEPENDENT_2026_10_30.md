## 1. The imported certificate is genuinely physical and torque balanced

The theorem does not import the older purely translational planar-stress
claim. Its actual inputs are the stronger physical contact construction
and its matched-junction uncrossing. Their complete arguments were read.

The physical branch has one smooth atom for a singleton contact, and two
smooth endpoint atoms for a positive-length shared side. The latter pair
is a sufficient feasible branch for both signs of the relative rotation;
it is not incorrectly claimed to describe every nearby packing. The
side owner's normal rotates in that branch, and the source differentiates
that rotating normal. Its contact gradients are the genuine force and
moment contributions at the fixed physical common point.

Strict vertex/vertex normals can be fixed arbitrarily in their common
open exposing intervals before multiplier extraction. The center-dilation
direction strictly improves every active contact constraint. Choosing the
radius velocity sufficiently large also improves all active circle
constraints. Thus the physical multiplier theorem applies to the
particular preselected normal system, not merely to some unrelated
normal system.

At four-square junctions the actual tangent quadrants force a common
frame. The two opposite normals use the same positive pair `(a,b)` with
opposite signs on the second component. The uncrossing subtracts the
same amount from both opposite forces and adds four adjacent endpoint
forces at the very same physical point. I checked the four bodywise
vector totals: they agree exactly. Since the application point is
unchanged, both origin moments and moments about each body's center
also agree. The adjacent atoms already belong to the physical
two-endpoint side branch. Hence the output is simultaneously planar,
normalized, physical, and force-and-torque balanced.

The later thinning and shared-side aggregation delete only available
atoms and preserve those equations. The resulting body graph retains
the exterior spokes to every physically boundary body. This is the
needed input for the new theorem's initial planar-support class.

## 3. The complete atom matrix has the right physical rows

I checked the matrix column conventions against the predecessor's
physical gradient equations. An oriented pair atom contributes
`n,-n` to its two body force rows and `det(p,n),-det(p,n)` to the
corresponding origin-moment rows. A circle atom at z contributes
`2z`, zero origin moment, and one to the normalization row.

The change from center moments to origin moments is legitimate only
together with each body's force balance, which the matrix retains.
Adding `det(c_i,total force_i)` converts the center-moment equation
to the origin-moment equation. For the circle force, this cancels
its center-moment term and leaves zero, since it is radial about the
same fixed circle origin. Thus `A w=b` is the full normalized physical
system, not a translational relaxation.

All application points in A are fixed actual singleton points or
fixed endpoints of actual shared segments. There is no unknown mean
inside a coefficient of A. The mean appears only after solving the
endpoint weights. This is precisely what removes the earlier
force-dependent-coefficient difficulty.

The atom universe is finite. Four candidate circle vertices per square
and two contact atoms per unordered pair give the stated crude bound
`N<=n^2+3n`; it is harmless that many candidates are inactive or absent.
Only actual contacts and actual circle corners are ultimately retained.
The sum of all body origin-moment rows vanishes identically columnwise,
so the `(3n+1)`-row matrix has rank at most `3n` over the function field.

## 7. Identity-or-avoidance and full-network rank obstruction

After the full rational substitution, any stated polynomial expression
in normals, atom weights, aggregate magnitudes or mean coordinates is
one rational function over K. Injectivity of evaluation gives exactly
the two asserted alternatives: an identity on the support branch, or
nonvanishing at the common actual choice. This holds for expressions
specified after the support is known, provided their coefficients come
from the originally fixed K and their dependencies are the indicated
rational variables.

A vanishing identically substituted denominator is a separate branch,
not something that can be divided out. Relations imposed by equilibrium
or special physical geometry can indeed be identities. The theorem
never purports to avoid them. Each future local exclusion still has
to establish its actual nonidentity after matching and elimination.

Similarly every rank is controlled by its minors, whose evaluations
preserve zero versus nonzero exactly. The augmented-rank obstruction
therefore rules out normalized stresses on the proposed full support,
regardless of sign. Equality of ranks alone supplies only consistency;
the manuscript separately retains positivity and the actual physical
planar-embedding requirement. It does not substitute abstract graph
planarity for the sector-compatible physical embedding.

There are finitely many subsets and minors. The finite-product version
is therefore sound for a specified finite family of support expressions,
after retaining identity branches and legitimate denominators. The
algebraically independent choice gives the stronger uniform interface.

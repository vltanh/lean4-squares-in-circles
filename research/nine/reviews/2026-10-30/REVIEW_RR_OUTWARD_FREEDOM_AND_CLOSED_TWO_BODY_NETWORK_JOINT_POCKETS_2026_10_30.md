## 2. The augmented core graph and retained exterior spokes

For an outward blocker, every common separator from U to W has
strictly positive N component. The whole intersection is therefore
on U's top face. The original applications lie strictly below that
face because both are side-interior applications at one height.

The source adds U--W only as a geometric edge. Its endpoint at a
shared segment can be selected away from other finitely many
junctions without changing any original force application. At a
singleton with at most three incident squares the local graph
admits the physical cyclic-sector drawing. At four incident
squares, all four sectors are aligned right angles. A point-only
opposite pair has a common separator parallel to U's transmission
axis and hence N component zero. Such a pair is not a genuine
outward blocker. This removes precisely the new opposite-pair
crossing which could invalidate the augmented physical embedding.

The source is consequently entitled to use the purely geometric
parts of [P], Sections 5--6, for the cycle plus this one edge.
No stress sparsification, new positive edge, changed normal or
altered body degree is inferred from that drawing. The existing
circle spokes can still be avoided simultaneously.

If the added edge were an exterior chord, one of the two original
cycle arcs would be cut off from the unbounded face. Because W is
neither positive neighbor X nor Y of U, each of those arcs has one
of X,Y as an internal vertex. The corresponding circle spoke would
then start at a vertex incident only to bounded faces and could
not reach its actual outer-circle endpoint. The contradiction uses
the preserved physical spokes, rather than an abstract outerplanar

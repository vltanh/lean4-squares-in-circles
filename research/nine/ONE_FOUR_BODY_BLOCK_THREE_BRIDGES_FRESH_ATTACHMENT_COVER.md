## 2. Complete hand orbit count

For three direct leaves, their root multiplicities are 2+1 or 1+1+1.
For C4 the double and single roots are adjacent or opposite; the
three-distinct placement has one orbit. For a diamond the double
root is a hub or a tip, and the single root is either the other
vertex of the same type or a vertex of the other type. These are
four orbits. A three-element subset of the diamond is either both
hubs and one tip, or one hub and both tips, adding two orbits.

For a length-two arm and one direct leaf, the arm root and leaf root
form an ordered pair: the attached tree types differ. C4 gives the
same-root, adjacent and opposite cases. On a diamond the first
root is a hub or a tip; the second is the same vertex, the other
vertex of the same type, or a vertex of the other type. These are
six orbits. No automorphism interchanges a hub and a tip.

A single path or fork has one root orbit on C4 and two on a
diamond. Hence the complete original counts are

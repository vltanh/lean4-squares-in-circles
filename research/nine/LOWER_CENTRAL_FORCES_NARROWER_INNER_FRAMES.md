## 1. Every central original clears each inner main vertically by 1/2

Retain an original outer-actual cone four-chain A,B,C,D in the closed
disk Q=1105/256. Its middle comparison may be weak. Let F be any
original central outsider, |xF|<=29/60, and let M be either original
inner main. Write

    h=hM, g=1/(4h), eta=h+g-1,
    1/2<=h<13/20, eta<1/30.

The strict original main bands imply the uniform bound

    D=|xF-xM|<3/2-h,
    D-g<1/2-eta.

For the less immediate horizontal order, the bound is even smaller;
it is enough to use the larger absolute endpoint of the original
main band. The aligned inset of M and the contained radius-one-half
disk of F are disjoint in their interiors. Their distance inequality is

    max(D-g,0)^2+max(|yF-yM|-g,0)^2>=1/4.

Because 0<=max(D-g,0)<1/2-eta, this yields

    |yF-yM|>g+sqrt(eta-eta^2).                         (1)

At eta=0 the strict horizontal margin still makes the vertical excess
positive. For all the allowed h, exact algebra gives

    eta-eta^2-(1/2-g)^2
      =(2h-1)^2*(-2h^2+4h-1)/(8h^2)>=0.              (2)

Indeed -2h^2+4h-1 is increasing on this interval and is at least 1/2.
Also 1/2-g>=0. Taking nonnegative square roots in (2), then using (1),
proves the uniform original-pair consequence

    |yF-yM|>1/2                                      (3)

for each main separately, irrespective of which horizontal half holds F.
This does not assert a new cone relation or projection separation.

## 2. A lower central bounds the main-height spread

In the routed low-floor 010/11 domain, suppose F is below both mains.

## 3. The original physical main pair forbids a wide frame

Put H=max(hB,hC), K=min(hB,hC). The accepted endpoint resource gives

    etaB+etaC<1/30,
    K<=min(H,3/5),
    0<xC-xB<=d=61/30-H-K.

Every original owned physical separator has support at least
s=1+H-K. Assume H>=57/100 and a vertical gap at most c=23/30. Write
k=sqrt(1/2-H^2), a=H+k, b=H-k. The two signed projections of any
actual owned axis are bounded by a*d+b*c or b*d+a*c. This follows
exactly as in WIDE_WEAK_MIDDLE_PAIR_VERTICAL_GAP_HAND_PROOF.md:
both projections increase with the folded owner angle throughout the
range, because tan(theta)<1/2, d>=11/15, d<=29/30,
c-d*tan(theta)>0 and d-c*tan(theta)>0.

For fixed H, the two residuals s-a*d-b*c and s-b*d-a*c decrease with
K. Their worst permitted K is therefore min(H,3/5).

For 57/100<=H<=3/5, use K=H. The residuals are A-q*k and A+q*k, with

    A=2H^2-(14/5)H+1, q=19/15-2H>0.

    (A-q*k)'=4H-14/5+2k+qH/k>11/50>0,

    A=269/5000, q=19/150, k<419/1000,
    A-q*k>269/5000-(19/150)(419/1000)=109/150000>0.

1/2-(57/100)^2=1751/10000<(419/1000)^2.

For 3/5<=H<=13/20, use K=3/5. Now

    A=(H-3/5)^2+1/25, q=2/3-H,
    1/60<=q<=1/15, k<3/8.

Thus A+q*k>0 and

    A-q*k>1/25-(1/15)(3/8)=3/200>0.

All original signed owner projections are strictly below the necessary
physical support, a contradiction. We have proved the separate hand
implication

    H>=57/100  =>  |yC-yB|>23/30.                   (5)

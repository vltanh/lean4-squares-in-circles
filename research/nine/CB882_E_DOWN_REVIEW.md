# Independent review of the E-down rational table

This note records the scope of a separate agent's mathematical and
arithmetic review of [the E-down exclusion](CB882_E_DOWN_TABLE.md).
It is not a human review, a Lean formalization, or an additional
premise of the theorem. The proof itself is the finite rational data
and the continuous arguments cited by that chapter.

The initial discovery tree had 129 visited nodes, 64 contradiction
leaves, 57 genuine case splits and eight CD-sign wrappers. It had
176 certificate events: 112 simultaneous-bound events containing
3201 deductions, and 64 contradiction events. An independent exact
rational replay checked all 93,369 positive integer weight
occurrences, every contradiction reserve, every simultaneous old-box
bound deduction, every parameter update, and the complete tree.
It invoked no feasibility or optimization solver. This first replay
used the discovery model's rational row arrays, so it was followed
by the independent row reconstruction below.

The hand proof that E below A forces F above B removes the entire
lower B/F half of that discovery tree. Its endpoint-inset/incircle
argument, outward-corner comparison, three exhaustive halfwidth
intervals and rational reserves were separately reviewed. No result
from the E-down exclusion table is used in that hand proof.

The published table is the resulting complete seven-terminal cover.
It has no angle or center subdivisions. There are nine bound
contexts, 308 simultaneous deductions, seven contradiction contexts,
1111 literal event-local rows and 5833 positive integer weight
occurrences. All seven terminal reserves are strictly positive.

A second independent calculation read only the published Markdown
row and certificate appendices. It imported neither the discovery
model nor its JSON output. From separately written versions of the
mathematical formulas in the table chapter, it reconstructed each of
the 1111 printed normalized rows. This included the signed product
planes, selected AE/CD owners and signs, frame-support mismatch
bounds, squared-distance secants, width mismatch, interval halfwidths,
inset tangents and secants, ordinary and exact-Q corner planes, and
the one used actual-square/incircle product row. Every literal row
matched a necessary member of its stated family.

That second calculation also checked all 5833 printed weights and
all 315 rational reserve inequalities directly from the Markdown.
Each of the 308 bound deductions was evaluated against its original
context box, never against an earlier deduction in the same batch.
Every input/output center interval and every signed-beta-to-parameter
transition was checked, including the two CD sign intersections in
each secondary AE branch. All displayed frame and inset intervals
were independently reconstructed from their parameter intervals.
Every printed row is used, and every weight references a printed row.

The initial coordinate bounds and the AE/CD covering alternatives
were checked against their cited geometric chapters. The literal
finite-cover table was checked independently of the discovery
traversal: its first two AE branches each have three CD terminals,
and its third AE branch has one terminal without choosing a CD axis.
Closed endpoints, both signs of E in the secondary case, and all
nine independent original frames remain present.

The previously documented review boundary for the foundational
weighted two-square cap and the original extraction/ancestry proof
still applies; see [the earlier hand-review note](CB882_HAND_REVIEW.md).
Their statements are used as established dependencies. This review
does not claim an independent reproof of the entire foundational
packing argument or a closure of the remaining E-above-A caller.

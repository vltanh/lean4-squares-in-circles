## 1. Radius-free positive circle mass in every deletion branch

Let G be one connected component of the original positive body-pair
graph. At each actual body i let c_i be its center, let F_i be its
entire outward radial circle resultant, and use outward pair-normal
notation so its unchanged force equation is

    F_i + sum_(j adjacent i) mu_ij*n_ij = 0,
    mu_ij>0, n_ji=-n_ij.                              (1)

The circle coefficients are nonnegative. No radius bound or private
degree hypothesis is needed in this section.

**Branch-mass lemma.** For any vertex A of G, every nonempty connected
component X of G-A has strictly positive total original circle weight.

Suppose all original circle coefficients in X were zero. Since G is
connected, X has at least one positive edge to A; its only neighbors
outside X are A. Sum (1) over X. Internal pair terms cancel, and
F_i=0 for every i in X. It follows that the complete pair-force sum
at A from its actual neighbors in X is zero too.

Consequently, in the system consisting of the actual edges within X
and all actual X--A edges, the pair-force sum is zero at every
vertex of X union {A}. This is an identity deduced from the full
original equations. It does not discard a force in applying an
original lower-degree theorem at A.

For an actual touching pair i,j with normal n_ij, write h_i(n) for
the unit's half-width in that direction. Its actual common support
line gives the exact center identity

    (c_j-c_i).n_ij=h_i(n_ij)+h_j(n_ij)>=1.             (2)

The identity holds for every actual corner or side owner and every
original shared mean, because the entire common face has the same
support value. Each half-width is at least 1/2. Multiply the zero
partial force equations by the corresponding actual centers and
sum. Each retained unordered pair contributes

    mu_ij*(c_i-c_j).n_ij
       =-mu_ij*(h_i(n_ij)+h_j(n_ij))<0.

At least one such pair exists, so the sum cannot equal zero. This
contradiction proves the lemma. Nonnegativity of the original circle
coefficients now supplies at least one actual circle-loaded body in
X and hence at least one physical boundary body there.

This is the center-virial proof underlying the deletion-branch mass
statement in the existing articulation-splitting source. It needs
neither a radial bridge nor a genuine leaf in the branch. Individual
zero coefficients and arbitrary physical zero-load circle contacts
remain unaffected.

### 3.1. Every non-E unit avoids the disk and every non-E edge has moment

of that unit in int(E) would, by interior approximation, produce
overlapping open interiors. Compactness gives positive clearance.

No radial actual contact can join two such units. If its unchanged
application and normal were parallel, orient it as p=t*n, t>0,
with an inner unit U maximizing n and an outer unit V minimizing n.

p=t*n has positive coordinate there, n's component is positive
and the actual maximizing coordinate is a+1. Thus

    t>1, a<=t-1<=epsilon.

If U's other interval also missed zero, the second absolute support
coordinate would also exceed one and t>sqrt(2)>d. Therefore the
other interval contains zero, and U has the actual point (a,0)
of norm at most epsilon. This contradicts disk avoidance.

Thus every original positive edge with both endpoints different
from E has nonzero origin moment. The proof includes every actual
shared mean and endpoint; its support value is not changed.

Every cyclic block must therefore contain E. This is the old

All nontrivial cyclic blocks of G consequently share the actual

**Low-radius block restriction.** Every original Q edge has zero

Thus c_E is inside the resulting Jordan cycle. Section 2 supplies

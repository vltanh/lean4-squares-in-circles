## 4. A complete replacement-row exclusion, including equality

The [near-horizontal replacement-row theorem](WEAK_OUTER_NUMERIC_NEAR_HORIZONTAL_REPLACEMENT_ROW_HAND_TERMINAL.md)

\[
x_B\le x_X\le x_D,\qquad
|y_X-y_B|\le1/8,\qquad |y_D-y_X|\le1/8.
\]

The new B,X and X,D links need not be actual. Every physical tilt and
both original separator owners are retained. The proof first establishes
the original pair estimate

\[
d_{ij}\ge1+|h_i-h_j|+q(t_{\rm owner}),\qquad
q(t)=\sqrt{1+t^2}-1-t/8.
\]

The scalar payment inequality

\[
q(s)+(h(s)-h(t))_++\frac12 T(t)\ge0
\]

holds for all `s,t in [0,1]`, with equality only at `s=t=0`.
Here T is the maximum of the two already accepted normalized endpoint
tolls, never their sum. A complete two-case analytic proof and exact
integer square comparisons establish the inequality. Summing along
B,X,D cancels the width variation and supplies the alternate A,B,X,D
cap terminal.

The [complete independent review](GLOBAL_OUTER_LINK_EXTRACTION_REPLACEMENT_ROW_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md)
accepts every original owner and axis, the full scalar domain and its
equality conditions, and the closed-Q strict-caller completion.

Its equality face is resolved inside the original caller. Cap equality
forces aligned B,X,D and the right anchors
`D=(3/2,-1/16),G=(1,15/16)`. Physical X/G separation puts X below D,

\[

Hence p=0, C is aligned, and C,D is actual, contradicting the original
strict weak bit. This excludes the complete closed row region at Q,

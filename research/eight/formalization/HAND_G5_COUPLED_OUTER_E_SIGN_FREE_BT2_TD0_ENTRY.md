# Positive GA with BT2/TD0 permits either original E sign

## 1. The complete same-radius entry theorem

Put
\[
 q_L=19577/5000,\qquad Q_1=1957767069/500000000,
 \qquad h=7/10,\qquad q_L\le q\le q_*<Q_1.
\tag{1}
\]
Use the actual same-radius boundary-neighbor domain of
[the uniform-radius pair theorem](HAND_G5_UNIFORM_RADIUS_PRIMARY_PAIR_TRANSPORT.md).
H,A are positive sorted outer states and B,D negative sorted
outer states at q. G,T may have either sign and either radial
kind. E is on its true core or its actual outer q circle and
may have **either** signed tangent. Retain the actual witnesses
\[
 HG2,\quad GA1\text{ with }x=d_{GA}>0,\quad
 AB0\text{ or }AB2,\quad BT2,\quad TD0,
 \quad DE2/3,\quad EH0/1.
\tag{2}
\]
All errors other than the displayed x have arbitrary signs;
the real frame spread and both captured variations are below h.

**Theorem.** No such boundary-neighbor cycle exists at
q at most q*. Thus this complete positive-GA branch forces
q>q*, with either original E sign.

At x=0, GA1 and GA0 are the same actual projection. That case
already has the original hard GA owner and is not asserted to
be strictly impossible. This distinction is essential at the
sharp reflected hard configuration.

The new fact is an extension of the exceptional E contact
stress to an arbitrary EH0 error. The aligned-contact scalar
polynomials remain unchanged. The argument below includes the
additional ordinary angle in the H block explicitly; it does
not discard that angle or replace a positive E by a negative E
without checking its actual neighboring inequalities.

## 2. Normalize a positive outer E at its fixed original frame

For this section retain sole DE3 with error e at least zero,
EH0 with error rho of either sign, and an outer E with y=y_E>0.
All other states, frames and witnesses are fixed. The actual
two projections are
\[
 a_D+a(y)\sin e-y\cos e\ge W(e),
\]
\[
 a_H\cos\rho+b_H\sin\rho+y\ge W(\rho),
 \qquad W(s)=(1+\cos s+|\sin s|)/2,
\tag{3}
\]
where
\[
 a(y)=\sqrt{q-(y+1/2)^2}-1/2,\qquad
             0\le y\le c_q:=\sqrt{q/2}-1/2.
\tag{4}
\]
Define the actual scalar contact demand
\[
 y_*=\max\{0,W(\rho)-a_H\cos\rho-b_H\sin\rho\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The original EH0 inequality gives y*<=y. Decrease y to y*
along (4), with the E frame unchanged. EH0 remains feasible.
On the sorted arc, -1<=a'(y)<=0; therefore
\[
 \frac{d}{dy}\{a_D+a(y)\sin e-y\cos e\}
       =a'(y)\sin e-\cos e<0.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus DE3 improves or is unchanged throughout this motion.
Sorting improves, the actual outer circle remains q, and
the state remains outside the true core.

Here is direct endpoint eligibility. Put
\(\alpha=\arctan(y_*/a(y_*))\),
\(\beta_H=\arctan(b_H/a_H)\), and
\(\beta_D=\arctan(b_D/a_D)\). The two actual gaps are
\[
 g_{DE}=\pi/2+e-\beta_D-\alpha,\qquad
 g_{EH}=\pi/2+\rho+\alpha-\beta_H.
\tag{7}
\]
The E diagonal was already excluded in the original q-domain
by the nearest-axis theorem and its auxiliary-ceiling argument
in Section 2 of the uniform-radius note. Hence E is strictly
sorted before this motion, and remains so. It follows that
gDE>0. Also alpha>=0 and betaH<=pi/4 give
\[
                         g_{EH}\ge\pi/4-h>0.
\tag{8}
\]
Their sum is unchanged. The other five gaps and all other
states and witnesses are unchanged, so winding one and the
boundary-neighbor conditions hold directly. No nonneighbor
separation is imported. Every actual error, frame spread and
variation is identical to its original value.

If y*=0, the existing
[sole-DE theorem](HAND_G5_POSITIVE_GA_OUTER_E_SOLE_DE_AND_SECONDARY_EXCLUSION.md)
applies, including its weak E-sign boundary. If y*>0, the
new endpoint has the exact, possibly nonaligned contact
\[
           a_H\cos\rho+b_H\sin\rho+y_E=W(\rho).
\tag{9}
\]
The rest of this note proves the requisite sharp obstruction
at (9). In particular no E-frame transport is needed here.

## 3. The arbitrary-contact stress and its primary split

More generally, assume sorted contained states at q<=q*,
H,A positive, B,D negative, G,T of either sign, yE>=0,
the owners HG2, GA1, either AB, BT2, TD0, DE3, EH0,
the exact contact (9), and x=dGA>=0, e=dDE>=0.
All other actual errors, including rho, are arbitrary;
their sum is zero and both variations are at most h.
Put t=x+e. We shall prove
\[
                              x=0.
\tag{10}
\]

On .1<=t<=.16 use the exact sharp dual k,l,c,eta of
[the critical contact theorem](HAND_G5_POSITIVE_GA_EXCEPTIONAL_E_ALIGNED_SHARP_CRITICAL_STRIP.md).
Outside that strip use the three rational families of
[the outside-strip theorem](HAND_G5_POSITIVE_GA_EXCEPTIONAL_E_ALIGNED_OUTSIDE_CRITICAL_STRIP.md).
In either case put
\[
 n(x)=k(\cos x+\eta\sin x),\qquad
 p(e)=\cos e+\eta\sin e,
\tag{11}
\]
and weight the seven actual witnesses by (n,k,l,c,c,1,p).
Their exact E force is still
\[
                         \sin e(a_E+\eta y_E).
\tag{12}
\]
The EH0 error does not occur in (12); its rotation acts on H.

Fix t, the ordinary errors and the actual coordinates, and
split x+e=t before taking the four anchor supports. The varying
p coefficient multiplies exactly
\[
 p(e)\{W(\rho)-a_H\cos\rho-b_H\sin\rho-y_E\}=0.
\tag{13}
\]
This is the only use of EH contact in the split proof. It
requires (9), not rho=0. The G support and the actual H
transverse residual are unchanged. Since yE>=0, the E part
has second derivative
\[
        -(1/2+y_E)\cos e+(a_E-1/2)\sin e
                       \le-\tfrac12\cos e+.915\sin e.
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently all strict split-curvature estimates in the two
parent notes hold without alteration. Their two scalar faces
remain (x,e)=(0,t) and (t,0), with rho retained among the
ordinary errors. This scalar comparison changes no actual
frame or coordinate.

## 4. Retain the added EH0 angle in the H block

The four baseline pairs are still
\[
                    H:(p,n),\quad A:(k,l),\quad
                    B:(l,c),\quad D:(1,c).
\tag{15}
\]
If u is the actual HG error, the two H force components are
\[
             p\cos\rho+n\sin u,\qquad
             p\sin\rho+n\cos u.
\]
Their exact squared norm is
\[
                    p^2+n^2+2pn\sin(u+\rho).
\tag{16}
\]
Thus H now has two ordinary arguments. In the parent notation,
its valid negative coefficient becomes
\[
                            \nu_H=\max(p,n).
\tag{17}
\]
The A,B,D coefficients remain l,c,c. The ordinary signed
sum is still -t, and its budgets remain P<=h-t, N<=h.
The separate-concavity concentration therefore has one new
possible same-block saturation, at H. All one-angle pure and
separated alternatives keep the parent formulas, using (17).

For clarity, if a<=b are the H pair coefficients and P=h-t,
the new same-block term minus its pure negative term at t is
at least
\[
 b\left\{\frac ab(\cos P+\sin P-1)+\cos h-\cos t\right\}.
\tag{18}
\]
Every H ratio used below exceeds 2/3. The bracket for ratio
2/3 is nonnegative on 0<=t<=h: it is convex, decreasing,
and zero at t=h. Equivalently, with P=h-t its second
derivative is
\[
 (\cos h-2/3)\cos P+(\sin h-2/3)\sin P>0,
\]
and its derivative at t=h is sin(h)-2/3<0. The elementary
Taylor bounds at h verify the displayed strict signs.
Thus this extra H saturation cannot improve on its pure term.

## 5. The sharp conclusion uses the unchanged scalar certificates

Sections 3--4 leave exactly the E/G endpoint baselines and
ordinary envelopes of the parent exceptional-contact proofs.

entry statement for outer E: a cycle with x>0, e>=0 and
arbitrary actual EH0 error is impossible at q<=q*, whether
the original E tangent was nonpositive or positive.

## 6. The finite all-E join and equality bookkeeping

For core E, the first row of
[the either-sign core theorem](HAND_G5_COUPLED_CORE_E_SIGN_SYMMETRIC_POSITIVE_ENTRY.md)
already excludes (2), including every E owner and error sign.

[signed nonpositive-primary reselection theorem](HAND_G5_POSITIVE_GA_OUTER_E_SIGNED_NONPOSITIVE_PRIMARY_RESELECTION.md)
reselects every nonpositive DE3 as actual DE2 and every
nonpositive EH1 as actual EH0, for either original E sign.
All states, frames and other witnesses remain fixed.

There are now exactly four E alternatives:

1. **Both secondary.** The
   [all-secondary E theorem](HAND_G5_POSITIVE_GA_OUTER_E_SECONDARY_E_ALL_T_OWNERS.md)
   is strict in this BT2/TD0 row and independent of E's sign.
2. **Only EH1 primary.** The
   [sole-EH theorem with signed coordinates](HAND_G5_SOLE_E_POSITIVE_GA_SIGNED_COORDINATE_EXTENSIONS.md)
   is strict for either E sign and all ordinary errors.
3. **Only DE3 primary.** Sections 2--5 and the nonpositive-E
   sole-DE theorem exclude it for x>0 at q<=q*.
4. **Both E primaries positive.** The
   [same-radius paired-E theorem](HAND_G5_COUPLED_POSITIVE_E_PAIR_SAME_RADIUS_ENDPOINTS.md)
   moves E alone to an actual DE-aligned or EH-aligned endpoint.
   Its possibly changed sign is permitted in alternatives 2
   and 3. All six other states, their frames and witnesses,
   the actual q and both variations are retained.

These alternatives exhaust the original E owners, signed errors
and radial kinds. They prove the theorem in Section 1.

The argument excludes x>0 at the original sharp ceiling and
never identifies a transported E with its ancestor. At x=0,

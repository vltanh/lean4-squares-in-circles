# Complete entry from the aligned-HG negative-primary endpoint

## 1. Exact same-radius theorem

Put
\[
       q_L=19577/5000,\qquad Q=9789/2500,
       \qquad q_L\le q\le Q,
       \qquad h=7/10.
\tag{1}
\]
Retain a sorted true-core necessary seven-cycle at the actual
circle ceiling q, with real frame spread at most h. H,A have
nonnegative signed tangents, B,D,E have nonpositive signed
tangents, G is nonpositive, and T has arbitrary signed tangent.
The actual retained owners are
\[
 HG,AB,TD\in\{0,2\},\quad GA1,\quad BT2,
             \quad DE\in\{2,3\},\quad EH\in\{0,1\},
\tag{2}
\]
and the sole assumed alignment and primary order are
\[
                         \theta_H=\theta_G>\theta_A.
\tag{3}
\]
For a necessary system stated independently of its original
candidate, retain the original implication
\(TD0\Longrightarrow y_T>0\).

Assume E is on the true-core boundary or on the outer q-circle.
Assume also that D is already outer at q, or that a primary
lift of D to that circle leaves its other retained witness
unchanged or improved. The latter is automatic when TD2 is
retained or TD is aligned. These radial qualifications are
part of the theorem. They hold for original boundary anchors
and for descendants obtained by moving only the intermediate
G or T in the primary-pair reductions. No lift of an arbitrary
contained D through a negative TD0 coefficient is asserted.

**Complete negative-endpoint theorem.** There is a necessary
descendant at the same actual q with actual GA0 and BT2 and
all hypotheses of the
[arbitrary-sign hard-owner sharp theorem](HAND_G5_ARBITRARY_SIGNS_AND_FRAMES_HARD_OWNERS_SHARP_REDUCTION.md).
If GA0 is unavailable initially, this descendant has a strict
H-circle loss at that same q. Consequently
\[
 q\ge q_*,\qquad
 \text{initially unavailable GA0}\ \Longrightarrow\ q>q_*.
\tag{4}
\]
There is no assumption about AB or TD alignment, T's sign,
the H/D frame order, E's frame, the DE or EH owner, or either
mixed error sign. The proof below covers their complete
finite alternatives without changing an actual owner until
the replacement witness has been proved feasible.

This theorem is stated on the working band (1). It does not
silently identify an outer state at a smaller radius with an
outer state at q after a ceiling enlargement.
For an original packing below \(q_L\), the original
fixed-witness radial-boundary reduction must instead be
performed at the enlarged ceiling \(q_L\) before using
this boundary entry theorem. Its outer anchors then lie on
that actual working circle, while its chosen witnesses and
frames are retained. An eligible resulting cycle in (2)--(3)
would contradict (4) at \(q_L<q_*\). Merely enlarging the
ceiling of an already constructed necessary descendant does
not supply this radial eligibility.

## 2. Genuine negative GA1 supplies the local cuts

If actual GA0 is already feasible, select that actual witness
and apply the hard-owner theorem directly. E has its required
radial alternative:
\[
               a_E\le7/12\quad\hbox{or}\quad
               a_E\ge c_q\ge\max\{b_H,b_D\},
       \qquad c_q=\sqrt{q/2}-1/2.
\tag{5}
\]
No state needs to change in that case.

Assume henceforth that actual GA0 is unavailable. Put
\(t=\theta_H-\theta_A>0\). The actual comparison in
Sections 2--3 of
[the aligned-HG local theorem](HAND_G5_GLOBAL_PRIMARY_ENTRY_ALIGNED_HG_NEGATIVE_GA_EH0.md)
gives
\[
 0<t<.09,
 \qquad .1<b_G<.156,
 \qquad b_H>.844,
 \qquad a_H<1.
\tag{6}
\]
If necessary H may be raised to its q-circle at its fixed
tangent: aligned HG is unchanged and either EH projection
increases, because its H-primary coefficient is positive
throughout the real-frame interval. The upper bound
\(a_H<1\) remains valid after this lift by (6) and containment.
No A or G coordinate changes in this preparation, so GA0
remains unavailable and GA1 remains actual.

For either EH owner, a nonpositive EH error has projection
at most \(a_H<1\), while its required width is at least one.
Thus
\[
                          p=\theta_H-\theta_E>0.
\tag{7}
\]
If EH0 is the actual owner, the full-interval local H/G
transport applies immediately. If EH1 is retained and
\(p\le.22\), its short-EH1 alternative applies immediately.
Both cases reach actual GA0 at the same q with strict H-circle
loss. They keep A,B,T,D,E and their frames fixed.

## 3. Every long EH1 angle is an outside E position

It remains to consider actual EH1 with p>.22. The two
original-data span theorems,
[TD0](HAND_G5_NEGATIVE_GA_ALIGNED_HG_TD0_SPAN_BOUND.md) and
[TD2](HAND_G5_GLOBAL_PRIMARY_ENTRY_NEGATIVE_GA_TD2_SPAN_BOUND.md),
give, for either actual transverse owner,
\[
                         \alpha=\theta_H-\theta_D<.22.
\tag{8}
\]
They allow arbitrary AB frames and either T sign, and they
use no E state or mixed witness. No state is changed in
deriving (8).

Since p> .22>alpha, E must lie strictly below D. Put
\[
                       n=\theta_D-\theta_E=p-\alpha>0.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus the actual DE error is negative. The two remaining
possibilities are its actual retained owners DE3 and DE2;
there is no unstated sign or frame branch.

For DE3 use
[the outside transport on either E radial arc](HAND_G5_GLOBAL_PRIMARY_ENTRY_LONG_EH_OUTSIDE_TRANSPORT.md).
For DE2 use
[the same-radius DE2 outside transport](HAND_G5_GLOBAL_PRIMARY_ENTRY_LONG_EH_DE2_OUTSIDE_TRANSPORT.md).
The latter uses the stated outer-D hypothesis. If its permitted
D lift is required, it improves DE2 and leaves TD2 or aligned
TD unchanged; the more general explicit lift-eligibility
assumption in Section 1 serves the same purpose. H is already
outer after the permitted preparation of Section 2.

Each transport changes only E after those eligible lifts.
It preserves the actual DE and EH inequalities until one of
three concrete stopping events:

* actual EH0 becomes feasible;
* the positive EH1 error reaches .22;
* DE aligns with residual EH1 error p-n>.22.

The third event is impossible here, because the unchanged
sum p-n is alpha<.22 by (8). The first two events therefore
exhaust the continuation. Weak contacts, flat or curved
core arcs, outer arcs, and zero-tangent endpoints are all
included in the two cited local proofs. The actual E frame
moves toward H inside its original interval, and both
positive and negative variations decrease.

At either attained event, apply the full-EH0 or short-EH1
H/G transport. Its hypotheses remain present: H's tangent,
A,G and their frames were unchanged by the E transport;
GA1 is still actual and GA0 is still unavailable. It follows
that this final path moves H/G a positive amount before
GA0 becomes feasible, and records its strict H-circle loss.

## 4. Preservation of the necessary system and the original equality test

A,B,T and their frames remain identical throughout. D's
tangent and frame remain identical, and D's primary changes
only in the explicitly eligible initial lift for the DE2
outside branch. The E-only part retains its radial kind:
core E has \(a_E\le7/12\), while outer E stays on its
same q-circle and remains sorted. Thus (5) persists during
that part. The final H/G transport decreases H's tangent,
so (5) persists there as well.

G remains normal on its true flat core during the final
transport. T keeps its original arbitrary sign, and the
original TD0 sign implication is unchanged. All real frames
stay in the original interval. The actual transverse owners
remain in their stated sets, except that a newly available
actual EH0 or GA0 may now be selected. The other mixed
witness is retained, including its weak ties.

The descendant consequently has exactly the inputs of the
arbitrary-sign GA0/BT2 sharp theorem. It yields q>=q_*.
At q=q_*, that theorem requires each of its five anchors,
including H, to have the stationary circle value. The final
H/G transport has a strict H-circle loss at this same q,
which contradicts that equality requirement. This proves
the strict part of (4). No transported anchor is identified
with its ancestor, and an E loss is not substituted for the
necessary H loss.

If GA0 was originally feasible, applying the hard theorem
to the unchanged original states supplies its complete
original equality classification. If it was unavailable,
the present theorem excludes original equality outright.

## 5. What is available before the opposite primary is converted

The local H/G theorem used above does not use BT2. It
applies with an actual BT3 retained whenever EH0 is available
or positive EH1 has error at most .22. The two E-only outside
transports likewise use no B/T inequality, except for the
explicit eligibility of a possible D primary lift.

BT2 enters this assembly in two places: the original-data
span estimate (8), and the final hard-owner sharp theorem.
Accordingly, the same actual local construction reaches GA0
while retaining BT3 if a separate argument supplies the
span (8), or if the EH0/short-EH1 condition already holds.
In such a use its conclusion is a necessary descendant with
GA0 and a strict H-circle loss; the sharp conclusion waits
for the opposite actual primary to be converted as well.
This distinction keeps both-primary iteration separate from
the complete one-primary theorem proved in Sections 1--4.

\[

\]

Retain the actual implications `HG2 => yG<0` and `TD0 => yT>0`, both signed
error variations below .7, and real frame spread below .7. All contact
inequalities are weak. A zero primary error is an actual secondary tie.

There are two different boundary operations:

* **Recession boundary construction:** from original squares, retain an
  initially selected physical signed SAT axis on each neighboring edge and
  move centers to first core/outer contact in the intersection of their two
  incident halfplanes. Physical frames and cyclic order survive. Individual
  rays and tangent ratios need not survive.
* **Fixed-ray outer expansion:** from a boundary cycle at q in [5/2,Q],
  keep core centers fixed and expand outer centers along their rays to Q.
  Every initially feasible signed axis survives, as do signs, ratios, frames,
  gaps and radial kinds. Derive classifications at Q and return to the known
  feasible q-cycle; no inward monotonicity is asserted.

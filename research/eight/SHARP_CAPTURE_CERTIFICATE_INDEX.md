## 4. Reproduction and scope

The archived original manifests describe the original executions, including executable hashes and complete closed-piece coverage. To produce a new replay on another platform, regenerate the header from the exact JSON, compile the recorded source with undefined-behavior sanitization, and run the corresponding recorded driver into a new output directory. Do not overwrite the original manifests or logs: platform/compiler changes may legitimately change executable hashes while a new exact replay still checks the same mathematical domains.

Live execution output was kept in `/tmp`; only closed outputs were published after a complete audit and then audited again. Earlier truncated or interrupted outputs are preserved separately and never counted as certificates.
